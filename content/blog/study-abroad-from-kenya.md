As of October 2026, Kenyan students can apply to the UK, the US, Canada, Ireland, Germany and Australia. If you live in Kenya, you need a UK TB test at IOM in Nairobi (US$107) and a medical exam for Canada. US F-1 visas for Kenyans can be valid for 60 months. Some UK universities accept KCSE English at grade B.

## Where can Kenyan students study abroad?

Your options include the [UK](/blog/study-in/uk/), the [USA](/blog/study-in/usa/), [Canada](/blog/study-in/canada/), [Ireland](/blog/study-in/ireland/), [Germany and the rest of Europe](/blog/study-in/europe/) and [Australia](/blog/study-in/australia/).

This guide covers only what is different for Kenyan citizens. For the general steps, read [How to apply for a master's abroad, step by step](/blog/how-to-apply-for-a-masters-abroad/).

## What visa rules apply to Kenyan citizens?

Most rules below depend on your passport. The UK TB test and Canada's medical exam depend on where you have lived. So they can apply to anyone living in Kenya, and may not apply to a Kenyan who lives elsewhere.

### UK: TB test, money and English

Kenya is on the UK's TB test list. You need a test if all of these are true:

- you are coming to the UK for 6 months or more
- you have lived in a listed country for 6 months or more
- you were living there, or in another listed country, within the last 6 months

The certificate is valid for 6 months from the date of your x-ray.

The gov.uk page lists one approved clinic in Kenya: IOM Kenya, at 78 UN Crescent, Gigiri, Nairobi. As of October 2026:

- the fee is US$107 for applicants aged 11 and over, and US$62 for under 11s
- you pay in US dollars before the exam, at CBA Bank's Gigiri branch or by M-Pesa Paybill at the clinic
- the fee is not refunded if you test positive, decide not to travel or are refused a visa
- you should book at least 10 weeks before you plan to travel, and say the test is for a UK visa

Kenya is not on the list of nationalities that may skip sending proof of money, so send your financial evidence with your application. See [Bank statements for a UK Student visa](/blog/uk-student-visa-bank-statements/).

Kenya is also not on the list of countries whose nationals are exempt from proving English. For degree-level study, your university can assess your English itself, at a level equivalent to CEFR B2.

You apply online first, then book an appointment at a visa application centre. The [gov.uk list of centres](https://www.gov.uk/find-a-visa-application-centre) links to VFS Global's booking page for Kenya. For costs and timings, read [UK Student visa 2026: cost, money and timeline](/blog/uk-student-visa-guide/).

### USA: visa terms

The State Department's reciprocity table for Kenya shows these terms as of October 2026:

| Visa | Entries | Valid for | Reciprocity fee |
|---|---|---|---|
| F-1 (student) | Multiple | 60 months | None |
| M-1 (student) | Multiple | 24 months | None |
| J-1 (exchange visitor) | Multiple | 24 months | None |

Kenya is not named in Presidential Proclamation 10998, the US entry restrictions in force since 1 January 2026. The State Department's notice of 2 February 2026 does not list it either. See [Studying in the USA](/blog/study-in/usa/).

### Canada: visitor visa and medical exam

Kenya is on Canada's list of visa-required countries, so you need a visitor visa to travel there, as well as your study permit.

For a stay of more than 6 months, you may need a medical exam. You need one if you lived in or travelled to a listed country for 6 months or more in a row in the year before. Kenya is on that list. See [Studying in Canada](/blog/study-in/canada/) for the study permit steps.

### Ireland and Germany

Ireland lists Kenya as a visa-required nationality (list updated 12 May 2025). See [Studying in Ireland](/blog/study-in/ireland/).

Germany also needs a visa from Kenyan citizens. The German Embassy in Nairobi (page dated 19 July 2024) says you apply online through the Federal Foreign Office's Consular Services Portal, then hand in your application at TLScontact. Processing usually takes about 1 to 2 months. The visa fee is €75, payable in Kenyan shillings. For language certificates in Kenya, the Embassy names the Goethe-Institut, ÖSD, IELTS and TOEFL. See [Studying in Europe](/blog/study-in/europe/).

## How do universities treat KCSE and Kenyan degrees?

Each university sets its own rules. These three examples come from each university's own Kenya page.

- **University of Manchester (UK).** KCSE holders must complete a university-recognised foundation programme before a bachelor's. It can usually accept a high grade in KCSE English taken within the last seven years. For courses that ask for IELTS 6.5 to 7.0, the minimum is usually a B. For a master's, it can waive the English requirement if your Kenyan degree was taught entirely in English.
- **University of Sheffield (UK).** It accepts KCSE English at grade B or above in place of IELTS, but not B-. It treats a Second Class Honours, Upper Division (a 60% average or GPA 3.0 out of 4.0) as a UK 2:1. A Lower Division (a 50% average or GPA 2.4) counts as a 2:2, and some of its courses consider a 2:2.
- **University of Toronto (Canada).** Its graduate school lists Kenya among the countries whose degrees normally exempt you from an English test. Your university must be recognised, and all teaching and exams must have been in English. Some applicants may still need to prove their English.

At these examples, KCSE alone does not lead straight into Year 1 of a UK bachelor's. Both Manchester and Sheffield name the NCUK foundation year at Brookhouse School in Nairobi as one route. For test options, read [Which English test do you need?](/blog/english-tests-for-study-abroad/)

## Will your foreign degree be recognised in Kenya?

The Commission for University Education (CUE) recognises and equates university qualifications. Its standards say it will not recognise a foreign qualification if:

- the programme is not accredited and recognised
- the university is not accredited and recognised by an accrediting body, under conventions Kenya has signed or the laws of the country where it operates
- the degree does not meet the minimum workload
- you did not meet the minimum admission requirements in Kenya's university standards
- it came through a collaborative arrangement that CUE has neither authorised nor recognised

The minimum workload is 630 instructional hours for a master's, 473 for a postgraduate diploma and 315 for a postgraduate certificate. A doctorate needs at least three academic years of study.

As of October 2026, a first application by the holder costs KES 6,000, or KES 10,000 for the same-day express service. Foreign certificates must be certified by an Advocate and Notary Public, or by the university that issued them.

Before you choose a short, online or partnership course, ask the university to confirm its accreditation and teaching hours in writing. For campuses abroad, see [UK and Australian university campuses in Dubai](/blog/study-in/dubai/).

## Which scholarships can Kenyan students apply for?

These schemes are open to Kenyan citizens:

| Scheme | What it is for | Status, October 2026 |
|---|---|---|
| Chevening | UK master's | 2027-2028 round closed |
| Commonwealth Master's | UK master's, through the Ministry of Education | Closed 20 October 2026 |
| Commonwealth Shared | UK master's, through a UK university | Opens 10 November, closes 8 December 2026 |
| GREAT Scholarships | £10,000 off UK tuition | 2026-27 list shows 16 universities |
| Fulbright Foreign Student | Master's in the US | 2027/2028 round closed 20 April 2026 |
| Australia Awards | Study in Australia | Kenya takes part |

For Commonwealth Scholarships, you must live permanently in Kenya or another eligible Commonwealth country, and hold at least a 2:1, or a 2:2 plus a relevant postgraduate qualification. You must also be unable to afford UK study without the award. Shared Scholarship applicants must not have studied or worked for one academic year or more in a high-income country.

For Fulbright, the US Embassy asks for four years of university study, equal to a US bachelor's. A three-year degree may be accepted only after 13 years of primary and secondary school, and each US university decides. A TOEFL score of 90 is desirable and 100 preferable. The GRE is required except for law, business and applied arts, and business applicants need a GMAT score of at least 550.

GREAT Scholarships need a Kenyan passport and permanent residence in Kenya. Each university sets its own deadline.

For more schemes, read [Government scholarships for international students](/blog/government-scholarships-for-international-students/).

## Which documents need extra steps?

- KNEC lists results confirmation, certification and equation services on its website. Ask your university whether it needs KNEC to confirm your KCSE results.
- Kenya is not a party to the Apostille Convention, so Kenyan documents cannot get an apostille. If a university or embassy asks for legalised copies, ask which route it accepts.
- CUE needs certified copies of your certificates and transcripts when you come home. Get copies certified by your university before you leave.

## What to do next

1. If you live in Kenya, book your IOM TB test at least 10 weeks before you travel.
2. Check your KCSE English grade against the Kenya page of each university on your list.
3. If you are eligible, apply for a Commonwealth Shared Scholarship between 10 November and 8 December 2026.
4. Before you accept an offer, check the course against CUE's standards, especially the teaching hours.
