As of October 2026, Kuwaiti citizens need a UK Student visa for courses over 6 months, but may not need to send bank statements. If you live in Kuwait, you need no TB test. Kuwait's accreditation body, NBAQ, lists recommended universities and programmes abroad. It also says to get your cultural office's approval before you enrol. The Ministry of Higher Education (MOHE) publishes a scholarship plan each year.

## Where can you study from Kuwait?

You can study in the [UK](/blog/study-in/uk/), [Ireland](/blog/study-in/ireland/), the [USA](/blog/study-in/usa/), [Canada](/blog/study-in/canada/), [Europe](/blog/study-in/europe/), [Australia](/blog/study-in/australia/), [New Zealand](/blog/study-in/new-zealand/), [Singapore](/blog/study-in/singapore/), [Malaysia](/blog/study-in/malaysia/) or [Dubai](/blog/study-in/dubai/). This guide covers what is different for students from Kuwait. For the general steps, read [How to apply for a master's abroad](/blog/how-to-apply-for-a-masters-abroad/).

## What UK visa rules apply to Kuwaiti citizens?

For a course longer than 6 months, you need a Student visa. For a course of 6 months or less, you need an electronic travel authorisation (ETA) or a Standard Visitor visa. As a visitor, you can study at an accredited institution for up to 6 months, including English language courses. As of October 2026, an ETA costs £20.

Two rules work in your favour if you are a Kuwaiti citizen living in Kuwait:

- No TB test. Kuwait is not on the UK's list of countries where you need a tuberculosis test. You need one only if you have lived in a listed country for 6 months or more and were living there, or in another listed country, within the last 6 months.
- Fewer documents. Kuwait is on the list in paragraph ST 22.1 of Appendix Student. If you apply from the country where you live, or from the UK, you do not need to send your financial evidence with the application. The same goes for the documents you used to get your offer.

The decision maker may still ask for that evidence, so keep it ready. You must still hold the money for 28 days in a row. As of October 2026, that is £1,529 a month in London or £1,171 a month elsewhere, for up to 9 months. For applications made from 30 November 2026, the figures are £1,570 and £1,203. [Bank statements for a UK Student visa](/blog/uk-student-visa-bank-statements/) explains how to show it.

To find the UK visa application centre, use GOV.UK's finder. For Kuwait, it links to the VFS Global page for the UK visa centre there. The fee, the health surcharge and the timeline are in the [UK Student visa guide](/blog/uk-student-visa-guide/).

## Which other visa rules depend on a Kuwaiti passport?

As of October 2026, from each government's official pages:

| Destination | Rule for Kuwaiti citizens |
|---|---|
| UAE (Dubai) | As a GCC citizen, no visa, entry permit or sponsor needed to enter |
| USA | F-1 student visa: no reciprocity fee, multiple entries, valid for up to 60 months |
| Canada | Visitor visa needed, not an eTA |
| Germany | Visa needed before you travel |

For the UAE, you show your passport or national ID card when you arrive. The UAE page covers entry only, so ask your university what it needs to enrol you. Check a Dubai campus on NBAQ's search like any other university abroad. [UK and Australian university campuses in Dubai](/blog/branch-campuses-in-dubai/) explains what degree you get there.

## What if you live in Kuwait on another passport?

Visa rules follow your passport. The UK TB rule follows where you have lived.

- If you have lived in Kuwait for the last 6 months or more, you do not need a UK TB test. This is true even if your passport is from a listed country, such as India or Pakistan.
- The ST 22.1 exemption depends on your nationality. India and Pakistan are not on its list, so their citizens send full financial evidence and the documents used to get their offer.
- MOHE scholarships and the cultural offices are for Kuwaiti students.

For the rules that follow your passport, see the Students from pages, for example [India](/blog/students-from/india/) and [Pakistan](/blog/students-from/pakistan/).

## How do universities abroad treat Kuwaiti qualifications?

Here are two examples from UK universities' own country pages, as of October 2026:

- The University of Glasgow does not accept Kuwaiti school qualifications on their own. It asks for A-levels, the IB, Glasgow International College or another accepted qualification. For a master's, it normally wants a bachelor's with 3.0/4 where it asks for a UK 2:1, and 2.7/4 for a 2:2.
- The University of Birmingham says holders of the Shahadat-al-thanwiia-al-a'ama are not normally eligible for direct entry to a bachelor's. It points them to its foundation pathways.
- For a master's, Birmingham considers four-year bachelor's degrees from recognised Kuwaiti universities. It expects a GPA of 3.0/4, 3.75/5 or 75% for a 2:1, and 2.8/4, 3.5/5 or 70% for a 2:2.

Neither page lists an English test waiver for Kuwaiti qualifications, so plan to take a test. See [Which English test do you need?](/blog/english-tests-for-study-abroad/) Each university sets its own rules, so check your course page too.

## Will Kuwait recognise your foreign degree?

NBAQ is Kuwait's national body for academic accreditation and quality assurance. It was set up in 2010, and one of its jobs is to identify institutions abroad whose graduates' qualifications can be accredited. Its search platform lets you look up recommended universities by country, university, specialisation and degree level.

The recommended lists are set by ministerial decision and updated from time to time. They cover programmes as well as universities. A programme marked X is no longer on the official lists.

NBAQ's search page adds these rules, as of October 2026:

- You must get the cultural office's approval before you enrol in any programme.
- For a UK programme, get its subject code from the university, or its HECoS, CAH or JACS code, and use it to check the lists.
- The programme must follow the traditional education system. Programmes from continuing education colleges, and evening programmes for people not studying full-time, do not count, even at a recommended university.
- The programme must already have graduated its first cohort.
- A master's must not be an executive programme.
- For limits on numbers at universities and language institutes, contact your cultural office.

NBAQ's Decision 5/2014 sets its criteria for choosing institutions outside Kuwait. They include institutional accreditation in the home country, and programme accreditation by a body NBAQ recognises. The programmes must reach a distinguished level in international rankings that NBAQ approves. The university must have a published admissions and transfer policy, and must follow a system of regular attendance during the academic week.

## How do Kuwait's cultural offices help?

Kuwait's cultural offices abroad look after Kuwaiti students. The Kuwait Cultural Office in Washington is one example. It is the cultural division of Kuwait's embassy, with offices in Washington DC, Los Angeles and Ottawa.

It places, counsels and advises Kuwaiti students at universities in the [USA](/blog/study-in/usa/) and [Canada](/blog/study-in/canada/). It also authenticates transfer credits, transcripts and degrees for Kuwaiti students in the USA. If you are self-funded and want health insurance or a social allowance, you register as Private Unsupervised. First you create an account on MOHE's Dayrah Academic Online System, then you register on the office's student portal.

US universities ask to join NBAQ's list through this office. If your programme is not on the list, ask the cultural office before you accept an offer. For another country, ask MOHE which cultural office covers it.

## What scholarships can Kuwaiti students apply for?

- MOHE scholarships. The Ministry of Higher Education publishes a scholarship plan for each academic year. The Washington office, for example, posts the 2025-2026 plan for the USA, which lists subjects with minimum grades. It also posts MOHE's scholarship rules for undergraduate students, effective July 2025.
- Fulbright. Kuwait is on the Fulbright Foreign Student Program's country list. It is for graduate students, young professionals and artists. You apply through the Fulbright office for your country, which is a Fulbright Commission or the US Embassy.
- Chevening. As of October 2026, Kuwait is not in Chevening's list of countries you can apply from.

For other schemes, see [Government scholarships for international students](/blog/government-scholarships-for-international-students/).

## Which documents need extra steps?

As of October 2026, Kuwait is not a party to the Hague Apostille Convention. A UK apostille may not be enough on its own, so ask your cultural office which stamps your certificates need.

If you are asked to legalise a UK certificate, the UK Legalisation Office adds the apostille. As of October 2026, it costs £45 per document, plus postage or courier, and usually takes up to 25 working days. A qualification certificate must first be certified by a UK public official, such as a notary or a solicitor.

## What to do next

1. Search NBAQ's list for your exact programme, using its subject code for a UK course.
2. Get your cultural office's approval before you enrol.
3. If you want a MOHE scholarship, read this year's plan for your country and subject.
4. Hold your visa money for 28 days, even if you may not need to send statements.
