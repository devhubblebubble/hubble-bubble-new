As of October 2026, Egyptian citizens need a visa to study in the UK, the US, Canada, Ireland and Germany. Living in Egypt does not trigger a UK TB test or a Canadian medical exam. UK universities such as Manchester ask Thanaweya Amma holders to do a foundation programme first. Back home, the Supreme Council of Universities equates foreign degrees, with extra rules for branch campuses.

## Where can Egyptian students study abroad?

Your options include the [UK](/blog/study-in/uk/), the [USA](/blog/study-in/usa/), [Canada](/blog/study-in/canada/), [Ireland](/blog/study-in/ireland/), [Germany and the rest of Europe](/blog/study-in/europe/) and [Australia](/blog/study-in/australia/).

This guide covers only what is different for Egyptian citizens. For the general steps, read [How to apply for a master's abroad, step by step](/blog/how-to-apply-for-a-masters-abroad/).

## What visa rules apply to Egyptian citizens?

Most rules below depend on your passport. The UK TB test and Canada's medical exam depend on where you have lived. So if you have lived in a listed country, they can apply to you whatever your passport.

### UK: TB test, money and English

Egypt is not on the UK's TB test list. You only need a test if all of these are true:

- you are coming to the UK for 6 months or more
- you have lived in a listed country for 6 months or more
- you were living there, or in another listed country, within the last 6 months

If you have lived outside Egypt, check the [gov.uk list](https://www.gov.uk/tb-test-visa/countries-where-you-need-a-tb-test-to-enter-the-uk).

Egypt is not on the list of nationalities that may skip sending proof of money, so send your financial evidence with your application. See [Bank statements for a UK Student visa](/blog/uk-student-visa-bank-statements/).

Egypt is also not on the list of countries whose nationals are exempt from proving English. For degree-level study, your university can assess your English itself, at a level equivalent to CEFR B2.

You apply online first, then book an appointment at a visa application centre. The [gov.uk list of centres](https://www.gov.uk/find-a-visa-application-centre) links to VFS Global's booking page for Egypt. For costs and timings, read [UK Student visa 2026: cost, money and timeline](/blog/uk-student-visa-guide/).

### USA: visa terms

The State Department's reciprocity table for Egypt shows these terms as of October 2026:

| Visa | Entries | Valid for | Reciprocity fee |
|---|---|---|---|
| F-1 (student) | Multiple | 60 months | None |
| M-1 (student) | Multiple | 60 months | None |
| J-1 (exchange visitor) | Multiple | 60 months | None |

Egypt is not named in Presidential Proclamation 10998, the US entry restrictions in force since 1 January 2026. The State Department's notice of 2 February 2026 does not list it either. See [Studying in the USA](/blog/study-in/usa/).

### Canada: visitor visa, and no medical exam for Egypt

Egypt is on Canada's list of visa-required countries, so you need a visitor visa to travel there, as well as your study permit.

Canada's medical exam country list does not include Egypt. So time spent in Egypt alone does not mean you need one. See [Studying in Canada](/blog/study-in/canada/) for the study permit steps.

### Ireland and Germany

Ireland lists Egypt as a visa-required nationality (list updated 12 May 2025). See [Studying in Ireland](/blog/study-in/ireland/).

Germany also needs a visa from Egyptian citizens. The German Embassy in Cairo's study visa sheet (marked February 2025) says you book an appointment on the TLScontact website and apply at its visa application centre. Processing takes about 4 to 5 weeks, but can take longer. School and university diplomas must be shown in the original with legalisation, and you must legalise them before you submit your documents. See [Studying in Europe](/blog/study-in/europe/).

## How do universities treat the Thanaweya Amma and Egyptian degrees?

Each university sets its own rules. These three examples come from each university's own pages.

- **University of Manchester (UK).** Thanaweya Amma holders must complete a university-recognised foundation programme before a bachelor's. It considers 85% or higher for its INTO Manchester foundation programmes. For science and engineering with an integrated foundation year, it considers 90% or higher overall, with at least 90% in mathematics and physics. For a master's, it asks for a first degree from a recognised Egyptian university with a high percentage or GPA.
- **University of Glasgow (UK).** Its Egypt page gives the grade equivalents in the table below.
- **University of Toronto (Canada).** Egypt is not on its graduate school's list of countries whose degrees normally exempt you from an English test. So plan to take a test.

| Egyptian bachelor's (University of Glasgow) | UK equivalent |
|---|---|
| 75%, GPA 3 out of 4, Very Good or B | 2:1 |
| 65%, GPA 2.7 out of 4, Good or C | 2:2 |

For test options, read [Which English test do you need?](/blog/english-tests-for-study-abroad/)

## Will your foreign degree be recognised in Egypt?

The Supreme Council of Universities (SCU) says it is the only body in Egypt that equates degrees from institutions outside Law 49 of 1972. That covers bachelor's, master's and doctoral degrees and postgraduate diplomas. Its guidelines and FAQ pages set these rules:

- The university that awards your degree must be recognised by the higher education ministry of its own country.
- The SCU decides each case after you graduate, when you send all your documents.
- Degrees in practical subjects need full-time study.
- For a branch campus in a different country from the parent university, you must show that the host country's higher education ministry recognises the branch. You must also show that the degree qualifies you for postgraduate study there.
- Your certificate must be certified by the higher education ministry of the awarding country, then authenticated. For UK degrees, that is the British Council in Agouza, then Egypt's Ministry of Foreign Affairs.

You apply online through the SCU's equivalence system, then send your papers as it instructs. As of October 2026, the fee for Egyptian applicants is EGP 1,500 for a bachelor's and EGP 2,500 for a master's. The SCU does not return documents, so it accepts copies with a live "true copy" stamp.

Before you choose a branch campus, an online course or a part-time course in a practical subject, ask the SCU how it would treat it. For campuses abroad, see [UK and Australian university campuses in Dubai](/blog/study-in/dubai/).

## Which scholarships can Egyptian students apply for?

These schemes are open to Egyptian citizens:

| Scheme | What it is for | Status, October 2026 |
|---|---|---|
| Chevening | UK master's | 2027-2028 round closed |
| GREAT Scholarships | £10,000 off UK tuition | 2026-27 list shows 8 universities |
| Fulbright Egypt | US master's, up to two years | AY2028-2029 applications open February 2027 |
| DAAD | Study in Germany | Advice from the Cairo regional office |
| Australia Awards | Study in Australia | Egypt takes part |

Egypt is not on the eligible country list for Commonwealth Scholarships.

Fulbright Egypt's master's grant covers a travel allowance, a stipend, health benefits, university tuition and other allowances. You must be an Egyptian citizen living in Egypt. Your bachelor's must be graded at least Good in the humanities and social sciences, or Very Good in the sciences. You need one English score, such as TOEFL iBT 80, IELTS 6.5 or Duolingo 105. Applicants without significant experience in the US or overseas get preference.

DAAD's regional office in Cairo, in Zamalek, gives advice on scholarships and funding. One DAAD scheme, the STEM study scholarships, funds a full master's in Germany in mathematics, computer science, the natural sciences or engineering. It is aimed at high-achieving students from developing and emerging countries. It is only for tuition-free courses and lasts 10 to 24 months. Applications open in June. Check in the DAAD scholarship database that the current call is open to applicants from Egypt.

GREAT Scholarships need an Egyptian passport, permanent residence in Egypt and an undergraduate degree. Each university sets its own deadline.

For more schemes, read [Government scholarships for international students](/blog/government-scholarships-for-international-students/).

## Which documents need extra steps?

- For a German study visa, legalise your school and university certificates before you apply.
- Egypt is not a party to the Apostille Convention, so Egyptian documents cannot get an apostille. If a university or embassy asks for legalised copies, ask which route it accepts.
- When you come home, the SCU needs your foreign certificate certified and authenticated, as set out above. Check the route for your study country before you leave it.

## What to do next

1. If you have lived outside Egypt, check the UK TB list and Canada's medical exam list.
2. Read each university's Egypt page for the grade it needs, such as Very Good for a 2:1 at Glasgow.
3. Before you choose a branch campus or an online course, check the SCU's rules.
4. For a US master's, watch for Fulbright Egypt's AY2028-2029 round, which opens in February 2027.
