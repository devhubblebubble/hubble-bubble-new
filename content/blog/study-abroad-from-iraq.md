As of October 2026, Iraqi citizens need a UK Student visa for long courses and a visitor visa even for short ones. If you live in Iraq, you need a TB test and must send financial evidence. Back home, Iraq's equivalency law rejects distance study and asks you to live in the country of study. The KRG wants you registered before you start.

## Where can you study from Iraq?

You can study in the [UK](/blog/study-in/uk/), [Ireland](/blog/study-in/ireland/), the [USA](/blog/study-in/usa/), [Canada](/blog/study-in/canada/), [Europe](/blog/study-in/europe/), [Australia](/blog/study-in/australia/), [New Zealand](/blog/study-in/new-zealand/), [Singapore](/blog/study-in/singapore/), [Malaysia](/blog/study-in/malaysia/) or [Dubai](/blog/study-in/dubai/). This guide covers what is different for students from Iraq. For the general steps, read [How to apply for a master's abroad](/blog/how-to-apply-for-a-masters-abroad/).

## What UK visa rules apply to Iraqi citizens?

For a course longer than 6 months, you need a Student visa. For a course of 6 months or less, you need a Standard Visitor visa.

### Do you need a TB test?

Iraq is on the UK's list of countries where you need a tuberculosis test. You need one if all of these are true:

- you are coming to the UK for 6 months or more
- you have lived in a listed country, such as Iraq, for 6 months or more
- you were living in a listed country within the last 6 months

You must be tested at a clinic approved by the Home Office. GOV.UK lists the approved clinics in Iraq, run by IOM in Baghdad and Erbil. GOV.UK updated IOM Baghdad's address on 1 July 2026, so check the page before you book. A clear certificate is valid for 6 months from the date of your x-ray.

### Do you need to send bank statements?

Yes. Iraq is not on the list in paragraph ST 22.1 of Appendix Student, so you send financial evidence with your application.

The money must be in the account for at least 28 days in a row. The end of that 28-day period must be within 31 days of the date you apply. As of October 2026, you show £1,529 a month in London or £1,171 a month elsewhere, for up to 9 months. Announced rule changes (HC 584) raise these to £1,570 and £1,203 for applications made from 30 November 2026. [Bank statements for a UK Student visa](/blog/uk-student-visa-bank-statements/) explains which documents count.

### Where do you apply?

To find the UK visa application centre, use GOV.UK's finder. For Iraq, it links to the VFS Global page for UK visas in Iraq. Check that page for the current centres and opening times. The fee, the health surcharge and the timeline are in the [UK Student visa guide](/blog/uk-student-visa-guide/).

## Which other rules depend on an Iraqi passport?

As of October 2026, from each government's official pages:

| Destination | Rule for Iraqi citizens |
|---|---|
| USA | F-1 student visa: no reciprocity fee, multiple entries, valid for up to 12 months |
| Canada | Visitor visa needed, not an eTA |
| Germany | Visa needed before you travel |

Germany also has a rule on documents. The German Embassy in Baghdad and the Consulate General in Erbil have stopped legalising Iraqi public documents, with the approval of the Federal Foreign Office. Their information sheet is dated February 2021. The German authority that receives your document decides whether to accept it, so ask your university what it needs. See [Studying in Germany as an international student](/blog/study-in-germany/).

## What if you are Iraqi but live in another country?

Visa rules follow your passport. The UK TB rule follows where you have lived.

- If you have lived in a country that is not on the list, such as the UAE, for the last 6 months or more, you do not need a UK TB test.
- You still send full financial evidence, because the ST 22.1 list goes by nationality.
- HCED scholarships, below, are only for Iraqis who have lived in Iraq for the last two years.

If you live in Iraq on another passport, the TB rule applies to you too. For the rules that follow your passport, see the Students from pages, for example [Iran](/blog/students-from/iran/) and [Turkey](/blog/students-from/turkey/).

## How do universities abroad treat Iraqi qualifications?

Here are two examples from UK universities' own country pages, as of October 2026:

- The University of Glasgow does not accept Iraqi school qualifications on their own. It asks for A-levels, the IB, Glasgow International College or another accepted qualification.
- For a master's, Glasgow normally wants a bachelor's with 75% to 85% where it asks for a UK 2:1, and 70% to 80% for a 2:2. It depends on the university you attended.
- The University of Birmingham says holders of the Sixth Form Baccalaureate, the Iraqi high school leaving certificate, are not normally eligible for direct entry to a bachelor's. It considers them for its foundation pathways with an overall average of 85% or above.
- For a master's, Birmingham considers bachelor's degrees from recognised Iraqi universities. It expects a GPA of 3.0/4 or 75% for a 2:1, and 2.8/4 or 70% for a 2:2.

Neither page lists an English test waiver for Iraqi qualifications, so plan to take a test. See [Which English test do you need?](/blog/english-tests-for-study-abroad/)

## Will Iraq recognise your foreign degree?

The Ministry of Higher Education and Scientific Research (MoHESR) equates foreign degrees under Law No. 20 of 2020, amended in 2025. The main rules:

- Your certificate must come from a notable university or institute that is recognised in the country that grants it.
- Certificates from correspondence or distance study are not recognised. Nor are those from universities and institutes set up for the education of foreigners.
- Branches of foreign universities outside Iraq are treated the same as their parent universities.
- For a bachelor's or diploma, you must live in the country of study for the whole course, apart from official holidays and breaks.
- For a higher diploma or master's, you must live there for at least one academic year in total. For a doctorate, it is one calendar year after a master's, or two academic years after a bachelor's.
- Time spent learning the language, and supplementary study, does not count towards the minimum study period.

The HCED terms also refer to MoHESR's guide to recognised study-abroad universities. Check your university against it before you accept an offer.

## What does the Kurdistan Region ask?

If you want to work or continue studying in the Kurdistan Region, the KRG Ministry of Higher Education and Scientific Research equates your degree. Its Directorate of Equalization of Certificates is in Erbil. Its rules, as of October 2026:

- The degree must be from a university on the ministry's list of accredited universities. The list is updated every year and is free to check online.
- You must fill in the online form and get a code before your studies start.
- Online education and certificates are not accepted.
- A master's is recognised only "in the form of courses and letters", meaning taught courses and a thesis.
- To have a master's equated, your bachelor's must be at least 65%. For PhD study, your master's must be at least 70%.
- Your certificates must be approved by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, the Iraqi Embassy or the KRG Representation in the country of study.

As of October 2026, the fee is 75,000 Iraqi dinars for citizens of the Kurdistan Region and 150,000 for citizens of central and southern Iraq. It takes 15 to 30 working days.

## What scholarships can Iraqi students apply for?

- HCED. The Higher Committee for Education Development in Iraq offered 1,000 places abroad in its 2026 round, for master's, PhD and medical training. This is part of 5,000 scholarships launched by the Prime Minister on 15 May 2023. The round opened on 15 February 2026, was extended to 30 April 2026, and has closed.
- The 2026 terms asked for Iraqi nationality and residence in Iraq for the last two years. Master's applicants could be up to 33, and PhD applicants up to 40. The PhD track was only for employees of ministries and government institutions.
- Other HCED terms: a secondary school average of at least 65, IELTS Academic UKVI 5.0 or an equivalent, and an offer from a university in MoHESR's guide. Master's applicants needed a bachelor's ranking in the top 25% of graduates. Scholars commit to serve in state institutions for twice their study period.
- Chevening. Iraq is a Chevening country, and applications for 2027-2028 have closed. You need an undergraduate degree and at least two years' work experience after it (2,800 hours). You must commit to return home for at least two years after the award.
- Fulbright. Iraq is on the Fulbright Foreign Student Program's country list. It is for graduate students, young professionals and artists, and you apply through the Fulbright office for your country.

For other schemes, see [Government scholarships for international students](/blog/government-scholarships-for-international-students/).

## Which documents need extra steps?

As of October 2026, Iraq is not a party to the Hague Apostille Convention. The KRG, for example, wants your certificates approved in the country of study, as above.

If you are asked to legalise a UK certificate, the UK Legalisation Office adds an apostille. As of October 2026, it costs £45 per document, plus postage or courier, and usually takes up to 25 working days. A qualification certificate must first be certified by a UK public official, such as a notary or a solicitor.

## What to do next

1. Check your university against MoHESR's guide, or the KRG list if you plan to work in the Kurdistan Region. For the KRG, register before you start.
2. Choose a course you will attend in person in the country of study.
3. Book your TB test at an approved clinic, but not too early, because the certificate lasts 6 months.
4. Put your money in your account and leave it there for at least 28 days before you apply.
