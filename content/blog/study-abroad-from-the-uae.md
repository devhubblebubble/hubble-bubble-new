As of October 2026, UAE citizens can usually apply for a UK Student visa without sending bank statements. The UAE is also not on the UK's TB test list. A US F-1 student visa costs Emiratis an extra $1,100 reciprocity fee. At home, a 2025 resolution says your university abroad must pass ministry ranking tests, or your degree will not count for federal government jobs.

## Where can Emirati students study?

Options include [the UK](/blog/study-in/uk/), [the USA](/blog/study-in/usa/), [Canada](/blog/study-in/canada/), [Australia](/blog/study-in/australia/), [Europe](/blog/study-in/europe/), or a branch campus at home in [Dubai](/blog/study-in/dubai/). Read the recognition rules below before you choose a university abroad, because they can rule some universities out.

## What is different for UAE citizens applying for a UK Student visa?

### Do you need to send bank statements?

Usually not. Paragraph ST 22.1 of Appendix Student lists nationalities that do not have to send some evidence with the application. This covers evidence of money and the documents used to get their offer. The UAE is on that list.

This works only if you apply from the country where you live, or from inside the UK. UK Visas and Immigration (UKVI) can still ask for the evidence before it decides. So keep the money in your account for 28 days in a row, with your statements ready. [Bank statements for a UK Student visa](/blog/uk-student-visa-bank-statements/) explains the rules.

### Do you need a TB test?

Your passport does not decide this. Where you have lived does. You need a TB test if all of these are true:

- you are coming to the UK for 6 months or more
- you have lived in a listed country for 6 months or more
- you were living there, or in another listed country, within the last 6 months

The UAE is not on the list. If you lived in a listed country, such as India or Pakistan, within the last 6 months, you may still need a test.

### Where do you give your fingerprints?

You apply online, then prove your identity. Depending on your passport, you use the UK Immigration: ID Check app, or you give your fingerprints and photo at a visa application centre. On gov.uk's [visa application centre finder](https://www.gov.uk/find-a-visa-application-centre), the UAE entry links to VFS Global's centre page for the UAE.

The fee is £558 from outside the UK, and a decision usually takes 3 weeks. For the full process, see the [UK Student visa guide](/blog/uk-student-visa-guide/).

### What about short courses?

For a course of 6 months or less, UAE citizens need an electronic travel authorisation (ETA) or a Standard Visitor visa, not a Student visa. A visitor can study at an accredited institution for up to 6 months, including English language courses. An ETA costs £20.

## Which other visa rules depend on a UAE passport?

As of October 2026, from each government's official pages:

| Destination | Rule for UAE citizens |
|---|---|
| USA | F-1 student visa: $1,100 reciprocity fee, multiple entries, valid up to 48 months |
| Canada | No visitor visa. You fly with an eTA |
| Germany | No visa for visits of up to 90 days in any 180 days |

In the USA, the reciprocity fee is paid on top of the visa application fee.

In Canada, if your study permit is approved from outside the country, you get a port of entry letter and an eTA. The letter is not the permit itself.

The German Federal Foreign Office's list shows that some nationalities, such as Australians and Americans, can enter without a visa and then apply for a residence permit. The UAE is not one of them. So before a course longer than 90 days, check the German embassy's student visa rules. See [studying in Europe](/blog/study-in/europe/).

## Do universities abroad accept UAE school certificates and degrees?

Each university sets its own rules. Two examples, from their own country pages, as of October 2026:

- The University of Edinburgh says applicants with the Tawjihiyya or Thanawiya General School Certificate will usually need a foundation year. UAE applicants would normally need A levels, the IB, US or Indian qualifications that meet its minimum requirements.
- The University of Glasgow says national school qualifications are not accepted for undergraduate entry. You need A levels, the IB, a foundation programme or another accepted qualification. For a master's that needs a UK 2:1, it asks for a bachelor's degree with a GPA of 3.0. Where it needs a 2:2, it asks for 2.69.

Edinburgh says all applicants must show their English, whatever their nationality. Neither page lists a test waiver for UAE qualifications. See [which English test you need](/blog/english-tests-for-study-abroad/).

## Will the UAE recognise your foreign degree?

The Ministry of Higher Education and Scientific Research (MoHESR) recognises qualifications from accredited universities outside the UAE. Its recognition report is an official document for jobs, further study, visas and government services in the UAE.

### What does Resolution No. (5) of 2025 say?

This resolution, issued on 22 May 2025, sets standards for Emirati students abroad, self-funded or on a scholarship. Your university must pass one of these tests:

| Where you study | Rank in your subject | Overall rank |
|---|---|---|
| Any country | Top 50 | Not needed |
| USA, UK or Australia | Top 100 | Top 100 |
| Another English-speaking country | Top 200 | Top 200 |
| A non-English-speaking country | Top 300 | Top 300 |

The resolution refers to global rankings accredited by the ministry, so ask MoHESR which rankings it uses.

If you do not comply, your qualification is not recognised. It cannot be used for employment, promotion, study leave or qualification-related benefits in the federal government sector. The resolution takes effect three months after it is published in the Official Gazette. It does not give the publication date or a start date, so ask MoHESR for the start date. Students already abroad get one year from that date to adjust, and a committee can consider exceptions.

### What else does the ministry check?

In June 2023, the Ministry of Education, which handled recognition then, replaced its equivalency system. The new recognition system looks at each university's ranking and field. The ministry said that for universities ranked 1 to 200, checking that the certificate is valid is enough. Degrees taken fully by distance learning became acceptable, and the minimum physical presence rule was dropped.

These changes did not apply to specialised professions such as engineering, medicine and law. If you plan one of those subjects, ask MoHESR about the rules before you choose an online course.

### What about a branch campus at home?

MoHESR has a separate recognition service for qualifications from licensed, ministry-accredited institutions in the UAE. If you study at a branch campus in Dubai, check that your programme is accredited by the ministry. The [Study in Dubai guide](/blog/study-in/dubai/) explains what to ask.

## Which scholarships can Emirati students get?

From the official UAE pages, as of October 2026:

- MoHESR scholarships: for UAE nationals, for undergraduate and graduate study abroad at universities the ministry recognises. You apply through its scholarship eService. A scholarship can be cancelled for a low GPA or for changing colleges.
- Scholarship Office (SCO), Presidential Court: selects outstanding Emirati students for prestigious universities abroad, through two programmes.
- Khotwa, from ADEK (the Abu Dhabi Department of Education and Knowledge): gives young Emirati students the chance to study internationally.
- Betha, from the TDRA ICT Fund: for undergraduate and graduate students of ICT subjects. Scholars have studied in the UAE, the USA, Australia and Japan.

As of October 2026, the UAE is not in the country list on Chevening's application page. For other schemes, see [government scholarships for international students](/blog/government-scholarships-for-international-students/).

## What if you live in the UAE but are not a UAE citizen?

Visa rules follow your passport. Where you apply usually follows where you live.

- UK evidence: the lighter rule depends on your nationality. If your country is not on the list, send full evidence of money and the documents used to get your offer, even when you apply in the UAE.
- UK TB test: this depends on where you have lived, so your time in the UAE counts.
- US fee: the $1,100 reciprocity fee is for UAE passports. Other nationalities have their own schedule.
- At home: Resolution No. (5) of 2025 applies to UAE nationals. u.ae lists the MoHESR, SCO and Betha scholarships as scholarships for UAE nationals, and ADEK describes Khotwa as a programme for young Emirati students.

For your own nationality's rules, see the guides for students from [India](/blog/students-from/india/), [Pakistan](/blog/students-from/pakistan/), [Egypt](/blog/students-from/egypt/) and [other countries](/blog/topics/home-country/).

## What to do next

1. Check your target university's subject and overall rankings against the Resolution No. (5) tests before you apply.
2. Read the scholarship terms on the MoHESR, SCO and ADEK sites, and note their dates.
3. For the UK, keep your money in place for 28 days, even if you may not need to send statements.
4. If you plan to study in the USA, add the $1,100 reciprocity fee to your budget.
