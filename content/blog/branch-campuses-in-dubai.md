Several UK and Australian universities run campuses in Dubai. Birmingham and Heriot-Watt say their Dubai students get the same degree as at home, and the University of Wollongong in Dubai says its degrees are issued by UOW Australia. As of October 2026, the bachelor's fees in the examples below run from AED 63,449 to AED 162,203 a year. Dubai study alone does not lead to a UK or Australian post-study work visa.

## What is a branch campus?

A branch campus is a campus that a university runs in another country. You live and study in Dubai, but the university's home is in the UK or Australia.

Each university decides how its Dubai degree works, so read its own pages. Before you accept an offer, ask what your certificate and transcript will say.

## What degree do you get from a Dubai campus?

Here is what four universities say on their own pages, as of October 2026:

| University | What it says about the degree | Approvals in Dubai and the UAE |
|---|---|---|
| University of Birmingham Dubai | The same University of Birmingham degree as UK-based students | Courses accredited by MoHESR |
| Heriot-Watt University Dubai | The same Heriot-Watt degree at every campus | Programmes accredited by MoHESR |
| University of Wollongong in Dubai (UOWD) | All degrees issued by UOW Australia | Licensed by KHDA, degrees accredited by the CAA |
| Middlesex University Dubai | Programme quality equivalent to the London home campus | Licensed by KHDA, CAA licence since 9 January 2025 |

Middlesex University Dubai's accreditation and course pages do not say who awards the degree. Ask its admissions team to confirm this in writing.

Some campuses let you spend time at the home university. On its AI and Computer Science BSc, Birmingham Dubai says you can choose to spend your second year in Birmingham, subject to your grades. Heriot-Watt's Go Global scheme lets Dubai students transfer to the UK or Malaysia for a semester, a year or longer, if the programme runs there. Heriot-Watt's UK campuses are in Scotland, so see [studying in Scotland](/blog/study-in-scotland/) if a transfer interests you.

## Who regulates branch campuses in Dubai?

Two levels of government are involved:

- KHDA (the Knowledge and Human Development Authority) regulates education quality in Dubai, oversees private institutions and certifies higher education degrees from licensed Dubai universities.
- The federal Ministry of Higher Education and Scientific Research (MoHESR) leads higher education in the UAE. The Commission for Academic Accreditation (CAA) licenses institutions and accredits their programmes.

Middlesex University Dubai says all its programmes are reviewed by KHDA's University Quality Assurance International Board (UQAIB), and that its KHDA licence is renewed each year.

Federal accreditation matters if you may need your degree recognised in the UAE later. MoHESR's recognition service covers qualifications from licensed and Ministry-accredited UAE institutions, and its recognition report is used as an official document for jobs. Before you enrol, check your programme on the CAA's list of accredited programmes, which u.ae links to.

## How do student visas work at a Dubai campus?

You need a UAE student residence visa. A parent who lives in the UAE can sponsor you, or your accredited university can. The UAE also issues a Golden visa to outstanding students.

Two examples, as of October 2026:

- Middlesex University Dubai issues student visas for 12 months at a time. The fee is AED 5,555.22 if you apply from outside the UAE, including processing, Emirates ID and medical insurance. There is also a refundable deposit of AED 2,500, less cancellation charges.
- The University of Wollongong in Dubai issues 1-year visas. The fee is AED 8,237 from outside the UAE, plus a one-time AED 2,500 Visa Maintenance Charge. Any unused balance is refunded after the visa is cancelled.

Most degrees last longer than a year, so budget for a renewal each year.

## Can you get a UK or Australian post-study work visa from a Dubai campus?

Not from Dubai study alone.

For the UK Graduate visa, you must be in the UK when you apply, hold a Student visa, and have studied a UK course with that visa for a minimum time. For a course longer than 12 months, that means at least 12 months of study in the UK. For a course of 12 months or less, it means the whole course. A semester or a year at a UK campus does not make you eligible on its own. See [UK Graduate visa: 2 years or 18 months](/blog/uk-graduate-visa-18-months-or-2-years/).

For Australia's Temporary Graduate visa (subclass 485), you must have held an Australian Student visa in the last 6 months and have a recent qualification from a CRICOS-registered course. Study in Dubai is not done on an Australian Student visa, so it does not meet these rules. See [studying in Australia](/blog/study-in-australia/).

## Dubai campus or the home country: how do they compare?

| Question | Dubai branch campus | Studying in the UK or Australia |
|---|---|---|
| Is it the home degree? | At some universities: check each one | Yes |
| What does the visa cost? | AED 5,555.22 to AED 8,237 a year in the two examples above | UK: £558 plus a £776 a year health surcharge. Australia: from AUD 2,500 |
| Is there a post-study work visa? | No UK or Australian one | UK Graduate visa or Australia's 485, if you qualify |

The UK Graduate visa gives 2 years if you apply on or before 31 December 2026, and 18 months if you apply from 1 January 2027.

## Is a Dubai branch campus a good fit for you?

It can suit you if:

- your family lives in the UAE and you want to stay close to them
- you want a UK or Australian university's degree and plan to work in the Gulf
- you would like the option of a semester or a year at the home campus

Think carefully if:

- you want to work in the UK or Australia after graduating, since Dubai study alone will not qualify you
- you will need UAE Ministry recognition and your programme is not on the CAA's accredited list
- you expect Dubai to be cheap: Birmingham Dubai's BSc is AED 162,203 a year, so compare total costs

## What to do next

1. Ask the university, in writing, what your certificate and transcript will say.
2. Check that your programme is on the CAA's list of accredited programmes.
3. Add up tuition, visa fees, deposits and yearly renewals for the whole degree.
4. If you want work experience in the UK or Australia, compare studying there with a Dubai campus. The guide to [which country you should study in](/blog/which-country-should-you-study-in/) can help.
