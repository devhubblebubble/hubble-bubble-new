Scotland uses the same UK Student visa and Graduate visa as the rest of the UK. Undergraduate honours degrees usually take four years. As of October 2026, you show £1,171 a month for up to nine months, or £1,203 if you apply from 30 November 2026. Free tuition only covers eligible Scottish students, so international students pay international fees.

## Do you need a different visa to study in Scotland?

No. You apply for the UK Student visa, and the same rules apply across the UK.

As of October 2026, the fee from outside the UK is £558. A decision usually takes 3 weeks. You also pay the Immigration Health Surcharge of £776 for each year.

For the full process, read the [UK Student visa guide](/blog/uk-student-visa-guide/).

## Which universities are in Scotland?

Universities Scotland represents 19 higher education institutions, and Study in Scotland gives the same number. They include the Universities of Aberdeen, Dundee, Edinburgh, Glasgow, St Andrews, Stirling and Strathclyde.

The other members are:

- Abertay University
- Edinburgh Napier University
- Glasgow Caledonian University
- Glasgow School of Art
- Heriot-Watt University
- Open University in Scotland
- Queen Margaret University Edinburgh
- Robert Gordon University
- Royal Conservatoire of Scotland
- Scotland's Rural College
- University of the Highlands and Islands
- University of the West of Scotland

## How long is a degree in Scotland?

Undergraduate honours degrees usually take four years. The University of Glasgow says its honours degrees usually take four years, and the University of St Andrews says most of its degrees do.

You usually study three subjects in first year, then narrow down. At St Andrews, the third and fourth years are the honours years.

A Student visa can usually cover up to 5 years of a degree-level course, so a four-year degree fits. But you pay four years of fees and living costs.

## Do international students get free tuition in Scotland?

No. SAAS, the Scottish student funding body, pays tuition fees for eligible Scottish-domiciled students who meet its residence conditions. The University of Glasgow explains this on its fees page for students from Scotland.

International students pay the international fee, which each university sets.

## How much are tuition fees in Scotland?

Fees depend on the university, the subject and your start year. These examples come from three universities' own fee pages, as of October 2026.

| University | Course | Year | International fee a year |
|---|---|---|---|
| Glasgow | Undergraduate arts and social sciences | 2027/28 | £28,275 |
| Glasgow | Undergraduate science and engineering | 2027/28 | £33,708 |
| St Andrews | Undergraduate arts, divinity or science | 2026/27 | £33,250 |
| Aberdeen | MSc Data Science | 2027/28 | £26,250 |
| Aberdeen | LLM (general) | 2027/28 | £23,000 |

Fees can rise while you study. St Andrews adjusts its fees each year, typically by 3% to 5%. Aberdeen tells students to budget for a rise of 5% to 9% a year unless the fee is fixed.

Four years at Glasgow's 2027/28 arts rate comes to £113,100, before any rises.

## How much money do you need to show for the visa?

The figure depends on where you study. London means the City of London and the 32 London boroughs, so courses in Scotland use the lower figure.

| When you apply | Per month | For 9 months |
|---|---|---|
| Up to 29 November 2026 | £1,171 | £10,539 |
| From 30 November 2026 | £1,203 | £10,827 |

Applications made before 30 November 2026 are decided under the rules in force on 29 November 2026. For London, the figures are £1,529 now and £1,570 from 30 November 2026.

You also need your first-year course fees, as shown on your CAS. The money must be held for 28 days in a row, and that period must end within 31 days of your application. Read more in [bank statements for a UK Student visa](/blog/uk-student-visa-bank-statements/).

## How much does it cost to live in Scotland?

The University of Glasgow estimates £1,100 to £1,700 a month to live and study in Glasgow. It published this estimate in August 2026.

The University of Aberdeen tells students to budget £1,171 a month, the same as the visa figure.

Treat the visa figure as a minimum. Your rent and lifestyle decide the real cost. For a full breakdown, read [how much it costs to study in the UK](/blog/cost-of-studying-in-the-uk/).

## Can you work while you study in Scotland?

The work rules are the same as in the rest of the UK. How much you can work depends on your course and whether it is term time. See [how many hours international students can work](/blog/student-work-hours-by-country/).

## Can you stay and work in Scotland after you graduate?

Yes, on the UK Graduate visa.

- It lasts 2 years if you apply on or before 31 December 2026.
- It lasts 18 months if you apply on or after 1 January 2027.
- It lasts 3 years after a PhD.

The application fee is £937. You also pay the healthcare surcharge, usually £1,035 for each year.

If you start a four-year degree in 2027, you will apply after the change. Under the current rules, plan for 18 months. Read [UK Graduate visa: 2 years or 18 months](/blog/uk-graduate-visa-18-months-or-2-years/).

## Is Scotland a good fit for you?

Scotland can suit you if you want a UK degree with a broad start and a lower visa money figure than London.

Good points:

- The visa money figure is lower than London's: £1,171 a month against £1,529.
- In a four-year degree, you can try three subjects before you choose.
- You can choose from 19 institutions, all under the same UK visa rules.

Downsides:

- A four-year degree means four years of fees and living costs.
- Free tuition does not apply to international students.
- From 1 January 2027, the Graduate visa drops to 18 months.

To compare Scotland with other countries, read [which country should you study in](/blog/which-country-should-you-study-in/).

1. Shortlist two or three universities from the Universities Scotland list.
2. Check each university's fee page for your course and start year, and ask if the fee is fixed.
3. Work out your visa money with the outside-London figure for your application date.
4. Plan for the Graduate visa length that will apply when you finish.
