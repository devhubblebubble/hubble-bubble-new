For a September start, aim to send your master's applications by March. As of October 2026, UCAS advises applying at least six months ahead, and many UK courses close in June or July. The steps are similar in most countries: shortlist, check requirements, take an English test and gather documents. Then apply, accept an offer, pay a deposit, get your visa document and apply for the visa.

## What are the steps, in order?

1. **Shortlist courses.** Start with the work you want to do after the course, then find courses that lead there. The guide on [choosing a master's that leads to a job](/blog/choose-a-masters-that-leads-to-a-job/) shows how.
2. **Check the entry requirements.** Each course page lists the degree, grades and English level it wants. For UK taught master's, UCAS says you need a degree "normally to 2:1 standard or above".
3. **Take an English test.** Book it early so the result is ready in time. See [which English test you need](/blog/english-tests-for-study-abroad/).
4. **Gather your documents.** The full list is below.
5. **Apply.** For most master's courses you apply to each university directly. Some German universities use uni-assist.
6. **Reply to offers and meet the conditions.** See [what to do after a conditional or unconditional offer](/blog/conditional-vs-unconditional-offer/).
7. **Pay the deposit**, if the university asks for one. Read the refund terms first.
8. **Get your visa document**, such as a CAS for the UK or a CoE for Australia.
9. **Apply for your visa.**
10. **Book accommodation and travel.** Avoid paying for anything you cannot cancel until your visa is granted.

## When do master's courses start in each country?

| Country | Main start | Second start |
|---|---|---|
| UK | September or October | January, at some universities |
| Germany | Winter semester: September or October | Summer semester: March or April |
| Australia | February or March | July |

UCAS says many UK postgraduate courses start in September and October. In Germany, dates differ by university. At the Technical University of Munich (TUM), the winter semester 2026/27 starts on 1 October 2026. At Hochschule Bielefeld (HSBI), it starts on 1 September 2026. The summer semester 2027 starts on 1 April 2027 at TUM and 1 March 2027 at HSBI.

Study Australia says the academic year "starts in February or March and ends in November", with a mid-year start in July. For Ireland and Canada, check the start date on each course page.

## How many months before a September start should you do each step?

| Time before the start | What to do |
|---|---|
| 10 months or more | If you need funding from your home country, start now. UCAS says to allow an extra four months or more. |
| 9 to 7 months | Shortlist, check entry requirements, take your English test, ask your referees. |
| About 6 months (March) | Send your applications. UCAS says to apply at least six months ahead "to be safe". |
| 5 to 2 months | Reply to offers, meet the conditions, pay the deposit. |
| 2 months to 3 weeks | Get your CAS or equivalent, apply for the visa, book accommodation. |

From outside the UK, you can apply for a Student visa up to 6 months before your course starts. A decision usually takes 3 weeks, so do not leave it to the last month.

Here is one real example. For most taught courses starting in September 2027, the University of Birmingham gives international applicants these dates:

- Apply by 2 July 2027.
- Pay the deposit by 30 July 2027.
- Meet your conditions by 30 August 2027.
- Birmingham issues CAS numbers by 3 September 2027.

Some Birmingham courses have different dates, so always check your own course and offer.

### What about a January start?

Some UK universities also take master's students in January. At Birmingham, the international deadline for most taught courses starting in January 2027 is 30 October 2026. The deposit is due by 7 December 2026, and conditions must be met by 14 December 2026.

In Germany, the summer semester is the second intake, and deadlines come early. TUM's physics master's programmes, for example, take summer semester applications from 1 September to 30 November.

For Australia's July start, Study Australia says applications "usually open around April each year".

## What documents do you need?

Check each course page. It may ask for:

- **Transcripts** (mark sheets) for your degree. Birmingham asks international applicants for "official transcripts of your qualifications translated into English".
- **Your degree certificate.** If you are still studying, the university can ask for it later as a condition of your offer.
- **A CV**, if the course asks for one.
- **References.** The course page says how many and from whom. Ask your referees early.
- **A personal statement.** See [how to write a personal statement for a master's](/blog/masters-personal-statement/).
- **A portfolio**, if the course asks for one.
- **An English test result**, unless you are exempt.
- **Your passport.** Make sure your name is spelled the same way on every document.

Some universities are strict about how documents arrive. The University of Oxford, for example, needs electronic final transcripts sent directly by the institution that issued them. You cannot send them yourself.

If you studied in India and plan to go to Germany, check the APS rule. The German Missions in India ask for a certificate from the APS (Academic Evaluation Centre) with a student visa application.

## How do you apply in each country?

### UK

UCAS says you apply for most postgraduate courses "directly to the course provider". A few, such as some master's in social work, nursing or medicine, are in the UCAS application service.

Check whether there is an application fee. The University of Birmingham, for example, charges none for taught postgraduate courses.

### Germany

You apply either directly to the university or through uni-assist. The university's own page tells you which.

As of October 2026, uni-assist charges €75 for your first chosen course of study and €30 for each extra one in the same semester. It starts work only after you have paid and submitted your application.

Some universities use a VPD (preliminary review documentation) instead. uni-assist checks your documents and gives you this certificate, then you apply to the university yourself.

### Australia

You apply to the university. If it accepts you, it sends a Letter of Offer. Study Australia says to read it carefully, and not to accept it if you are not happy with any of its terms.

### Canada

You apply to the school. If it accepts you, it gives you a letter of acceptance, which you upload with your study permit application.

## Which document do you need for the visa?

| Country | Document | When you get it |
|---|---|---|
| UK | CAS | After the university offers you a place |
| Australia | CoE | After you accept the offer and pay the deposit |
| Canada | Letter of acceptance | When the school accepts you |
| Germany | Proof of admission | When the university admits you |
| Ireland | Letter of acceptance | When you have an unconditional place |

- **UK:** a CAS (Confirmation of Acceptance for Studies) is a reference number from your university. You must apply for your visa within 6 months of receiving it. See [what is a CAS](/blog/what-is-a-cas/).
- **Australia:** the CoE (Confirmation of Enrolment) comes after you accept your Letter of Offer and pay your deposit, says Study Australia.
- **Canada:** a provincial or territorial attestation letter (PAL/TAL) is "usually required" too. You do not need one for a "degree-granting graduate program at the master's or doctoral level at a public DLI starting January 1, 2026". A DLI is a designated learning institution.
- **Germany:** the German Missions in India ask for "proof of admittance to the study course".
- **Ireland:** the Embassy of Ireland in New Delhi asks for a letter confirming "unconditional placement" on an eligible course. You must also pay fees first: in full if they are under €6,000, or at least €6,000 if they are more.

## When can you apply for the visa?

- **UK:** up to 6 months before your course starts, from outside the UK. The fee is £558 and a decision usually takes 3 weeks, as of October 2026. You may also need an ATAS certificate if you will study "sensitive topics" at RQF level 7 or above. See the [UK Student visa guide](/blog/uk-student-visa-guide/) and [bank statements for a UK Student visa](/blog/uk-student-visa-bank-statements/).
- **Australia:** online through ImmiAccount. You answer Genuine Student questions and need Overseas Student Health Cover (OSHC) for your whole stay. See [studying in Australia](/blog/study-in-australia/).
- **Canada:** your school will be asked to confirm that your letter of acceptance is valid. If it does not reply in time, your application is returned and the fee refunded. See [studying in Canada](/blog/study-in-canada/).
- **Germany:** through the German embassy or consulate, with proof of money (a blocked account is one way), health insurance and any language certificates. See [studying in Germany](/blog/study-in-germany/).
- **Ireland:** the New Delhi checklist also asks for an English test certificate and private medical insurance. See [studying in Ireland](/blog/study-in-ireland/).

## What are the common mistakes?

- **Applying late.** Many UK courses close in June or July, and TUM's physics master's programmes close on 31 May for the winter semester.
- **Sending one personal statement everywhere.** The University of St Andrews careers service says to "create a new one for every application you make".
- **Missing a deadline after the offer.** Deposit, conditions and CAS dates sit close together. At Birmingham, conditions for September 2027 must be met by 30 August 2027.
- **Paying the wrong person.** uni-assist says payment requests come only from uni-assist, through its own portal. If anyone asks you to pay outside the uni-assist portal, check with the university first.
- **Forgetting extra checks**, such as ATAS for some UK subjects or the APS certificate for Indian students going to Germany.

## What to do next

1. Write down three things for each course on your shortlist: the deadline, the start date and the documents it asks for.
2. Book your English test and ask your referees this month.
3. Send your applications by March for a September start, or earlier if a course deadline is earlier.
