As of October 2026, Turkish citizens do not need a TB test for a UK Student visa unless they have lived in a listed country. Turkey is not on the UK's lighter evidence list, so you send proof of money with your application. German visa appointments in Turkey go through iDATA. Türkiye can take full part in Erasmus+, and YÖK decides how a foreign degree is recognised back home.

## Your options

The Study in guides cover [the UK](/blog/study-in/uk/), [Europe](/blog/study-in/europe/), [the USA](/blog/study-in/usa/), [Ireland](/blog/study-in/ireland/), [Canada](/blog/study-in/canada/) and [Australia](/blog/study-in/australia/). This page covers only the rules that are different for Turkish citizens. Visa rules follow your nationality, while where you apply usually follows where you live. If you live outside Turkey, check the rules in that country too.

## What are the UK visa rules for Turkish citizens?

You apply for the same Student visa as everyone else: see the [UK Student visa guide](/blog/uk-student-visa-guide/). Four rules depend on your nationality or where you have lived, as of October 2026:

| Rule | For Turkish citizens |
|---|---|
| TB test | Turkey is not on the list |
| Lighter evidence list | Turkey is not on it, so send proof of money |
| ATAS | Turkey is not exempt |
| English | Turkey is not exempt, so show CEFR level B2 for a degree |

The TB rule follows where you have lived, not your passport. You need a test if all of these are true:

- you are coming to the UK for 6 months or more
- you have lived in a listed country, such as Azerbaijan or Georgia, for 6 months or more
- you lived there within the last 6 months

For the money, show your course fees for one academic year plus living costs for up to 9 months: £1,171 a month outside London or £1,529 in London. For applications from 30 November 2026, these rise to £1,203 and £1,570. See [bank statements for a UK Student visa](/blog/uk-student-visa-bank-statements/).

If your master's or PhD needs an ATAS certificate, apply early. It is free but takes at least 30 working days.

As part of your application, you prove your identity. gov.uk's visa application centre finder links applicants in Turkey to [VFS Global's centre list](https://visa.vfsglobal.com/tur/en/gbr/attend-centre).

## What are the visa rules for Germany and the Netherlands?

For a stay of more than 90 days, such as a degree, Turkish citizens need a national visa for Germany. The German missions in Turkey say:

- The missions are the Embassy in Ankara, the Consulates General in Istanbul and Izmir, and the Consulate in Antalya.
- You book an appointment through iDATA's online system. Turkish and non-Turkish nationals use separate booking systems, so register family members of different nationalities in the same one.
- iDATA may charge a €39.92 service fee, a €12 appointment fee, or both, paid in Turkish lira.
- You can show your living costs with a blocked account from any provider, or with a German declaration of commitment (Verpflichtungserklärung).

In the Netherlands, Turkish nationals pay the regular €254 for a study residence permit. Under the EC-Turkey Association Agreement, the IND charges Turkish nationals a lower rate for some later permits, such as job-seeking after study.

Read more in [studying in Europe](/blog/study-in/europe/) and [studying in Germany](/blog/study-in-germany/).

### Can Turkish students use Erasmus+?

Yes. The European Commission lists Türkiye as a third country associated to Erasmus+, which can fully take part in all its actions. An exchange does not replace the visa: for more than 90 days in Germany, you still need a national visa.

## What about the USA, Ireland and Canada?

- **USA.** The US Embassy in Ankara and the Consulate General in Istanbul process nonimmigrant visas, including the F-1. For Turkish citizens, an F-1 can be valid for up to 60 months with multiple entries, and there is no reciprocity fee. You still pay the $185 application fee and the $350 SEVIS fee. See [studying in the USA](/blog/study-in/usa/).
- **Ireland.** Turkey is on Ireland's list of visa-required nationalities, so you need a visa to study there. See [studying in Ireland](/blog/study-in/ireland/).
- **Canada.** Türkiye is on Canada's visa-required list, so you need a visitor visa as well as a study permit. Canada issues the visa at the same time as your first study permit, with no separate application. See [studying in Canada](/blog/study-in/canada/).

## How do UK universities treat Turkish qualifications?

Each university sets its own rules. These three examples come from each university's Turkey page, as of October 2026:

| University | Lise Diplomasi for undergraduate entry | Lisans for a 2:1 | Lisans for a 2:2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Leeds | 72% to 88% in the final year, approved schools only | 80% or GPA 3.0 | 75% or GPA 2.5 |
| Warwick | 80% average for ABB, approved schools | GPA 2.8 to 3.29 | GPA 2.5 to 2.79 |
| Surrey | 65% in the final year for BBC | GPA 2.8 to 3.0 | GPA 2.6 to 2.8 |

Warwick's GPA range depends on which Turkish university you attended. All three point to a foundation programme if you do not meet direct entry.

None of the three pages lists an English test waiver for Turkish qualifications, so plan to take a test. Leeds, for example, asks for IELTS 6.5 overall for postgraduate study. See [which English test you need](/blog/english-tests-for-study-abroad/).

## Will your foreign degree be recognised in Turkey?

YÖK, the Council of Higher Education, handles recognition and equivalence for foreign associate, bachelor's and master's diplomas. The Interuniversity Council assesses doctorates. If you apply for postgraduate study in Turkey with a foreign bachelor's or master's, you need YÖK equivalence.

YÖK's application guidance sets out these steps:

1. Upload your documents online at denklik.yok.gov.tr.
2. Bring the documents to your appointment, where staff check them.
3. If your course was taught in a language that ÖSYM tests, send an ÖSYM result of at least 60 in that language, or an equivalent exam result. YÖK refuses the application if you do not send it within 2 years.
4. Wait for the decision. YÖK says the time depends on the country, the programme and its correspondence.

Before you enrol, check YÖK's rules for your country and course type, especially for online study and branch campuses. If you are unsure, ask YÖK before you pay a deposit.

## Which scholarships can Turkish students get?

- **YLSY (Ministry of National Education).** Funds master's and PhD study abroad in return for compulsory service. You need a bachelor's GPA of at least 2.50 out of 4, or 65 out of 100, and an ALES score of at least 75. It covers school fees, a monthly stipend set by country, your health costs and travel. Afterwards, you serve twice the length of your funded study. If you leave early, you repay the costs with interest. A new guide, with that year's age limits, is published each year.
- **Jean Monnet Scholarship Programme.** Set up under a 1989 agreement between Türkiye and the European Commission, it funds postgraduate study in EU member states in listed areas of EU law and policy. Final-year students, postgraduate students, university staff and people working in the public or private sector can apply. It covers tuition up to €22,000, €700 to €2,050 a month depending on the country, and €3,500 for costs such as visas, travel and insurance. Applications for 2027/28 close on 27 October 2026.
- **GREAT Scholarships.** £10,000 towards a one-year taught master's at listed UK universities, for Turkish passport holders who are also permanent residents of Turkey. For 2026/27, the universities included Loughborough, Swansea, Kent and Warwick, and each set its own deadline.
- **Chevening.** Open to citizens of Türkiye. Applications for 2027/28 have closed, so watch for the next round.
- **Fulbright Türkiye.** Its master's grant for Turkish citizens pays up to $60,000 a year, for up to two years. You need a GPA of at least 3.00 out of 4, and TOEFL iBT 75 or IELTS 6. The 2027/28 deadline was 10 April 2026. Grantees must return to Turkey for two years under the J-1 visa rule.

More schemes are in [government scholarships for international students](/blog/government-scholarships-for-international-students/).

## Which documents need extra steps?

Türkiye has been a party to the Hague Apostille Convention since 29 September 1985. The UK, Germany, the Netherlands, Ireland and the USA are parties too, and Canada joined from 11 January 2024. An apostille is a stamped official certificate attached to a document once its signature, stamp or seal has been checked. Ask your university or visa office which documents need one before you pay for it.

## What to do next

1. Check whether your UK course needs ATAS, and whether you have lived in a TB test country.
2. Check your shortlisted universities' Turkey pages for the Lise or Lisans grades they want.
3. Note the dates: Jean Monnet closes on 27 October 2026, and YLSY publishes a new guide each year.
4. Before you enrol, check how YÖK will treat your degree.
