Decide what work you want first, then choose the country where you can train for it, afford it and stay on to work. As of October 2026, most state universities in Germany charge no tuition. A master's in Canada can lead to 3 years of post-study work, and Australia usually gives 2 to 3 years. The UK Graduate visa is 18 months if you apply from 1 January 2027.

## Why decide on the work before the country?

A master's abroad usually takes one or two years. The work you train for can fill many years after that.

If you start with a country, you choose from whatever courses happen to be there. If you start with the work, you can test every option against one question: will this help me get that job?

The work also tells you what to check in each country. You need to know how people get into that job there, which language employers expect, and whether the job can lead to a work visa.

For a step-by-step method, read [how to choose a master's that leads to a job](/blog/choose-a-masters-that-leads-to-a-job/).

## How do the main study countries compare?

This table gives one example fee for each country and the main visa and work rules, as of October 2026. Every figure comes from an official or university page listed in the sources.

| Country | Example master's fee | Money to show for the visa | Work while studying | Staying to work after |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| UK | £34,815 (Glasgow, 12 months, 2027/28) | First-year fees plus £1,171 a month (£1,529 in London), up to 9 months | 20 hours a week in term (degree level) | Graduate visa: 2 years, or 18 months if you apply from 1 January 2027 |
| Ireland | €20,800 a year (Limerick, 2026/27) | €10,000 a year, plus fees | 20 hours a week in term, 40 in holidays | Up to 2 years after a master's |
| Germany | Usually none at state universities; TUM €4,000 or €6,000 a semester | €11,904 for the first year | 140 full days or 280 half days a year | Up to 18 months to find work |
| Australia | AUD 57,640 a year (ANU, 2027) | AUD 29,710 a year for living costs | 48 hours a fortnight in term | Usually 2 to 3 years |
| Canada | CAD 24,028 a year (national average) | CAD 23,448 a year plus fees and travel (outside Quebec) | 24 hours a week off campus in term | Up to 3 years after a master's |
| South Korea | KRW 3,828,000 a semester (SNU engineering) | Proof of funds; ask your embassy for the amount | 30 hours a week in term (master's, with TOPIK 4) | D-10 job-seeking visa |
| Dubai branch campuses (UAE) | AED 76,491 in total (Middlesex Dubai, 2026) | No amount listed on u.ae | Ask your university | No UK or Australian post-study visa |

A few notes on the table:

- UK: for applications made from 30 November 2026, the monthly figures rise to £1,203 outside London and £1,570 in London. Applications made before then use the current figures.
- Canada: the fee is EduCanada's average for international graduate students.
- South Korea: your course and your Korean level decide your hours, and you need permission first.
- Fees are examples only. Check the fee for your own course and start year.

## What should you know about each country?

### The UK

A UK master's can take one year: Glasgow's MSc Data Science & Artificial Intelligence, for example, is 12 months full-time. As of October 2026, the Student visa costs £558 from outside the UK, plus a health surcharge of £776 a year. Scotland uses the same visa and the outside-London money figure, so read the [UK Student visa guide](/blog/uk-student-visa-guide/) and [studying in Scotland](/blog/study-in-scotland/).

If you are a national of Afghanistan, Cameroon, Myanmar or Sudan, check the UK's [visa brake](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/visa-brake-changes-to-the-uk-visa-system) first. Since 26 March 2026, Student visa applications made outside the UK by main applicants of those nationalities are refused, even with a CAS.

### Ireland

You study and live in English, and Education in Ireland describes Ireland as the only English-speaking country in the Eurozone. As of October 2026, you show €10,000 a year on top of your fees, and Stamp 2 lets you work 20 hours a week in term and 40 in the holidays. After a master's, Stamp 1G gives 12 months, then 12 more if you show you have taken steps to find work: see [studying in Ireland](/blog/study-in-ireland/).

### Germany

DAAD says state universities generally charge no fees for most courses, though Baden-Württemberg and TUM charge non-EU students. As of October 2026, you need €11,904 for your first year, usually in a blocked account. The Federal Employment Agency says English alone is often not enough to find a job, so plan to learn German: see [studying in Germany](/blog/study-in-germany/).

### Australia

Australia's Temporary Graduate visa usually gives 2 to 3 years after a degree, and you can work 48 hours a fortnight in term. Budget carefully: as of October 2026, the Student visa costs from AUD 2,500 and ANU's two-year Master of Computing is AUD 57,640 a year. Since 2 October 2026, most students cannot include a partner or children in their visa application: see [studying in Australia](/blog/study-in-australia/).

### Canada

Since 1 January 2026, master's and doctoral students at public institutions have not needed a provincial attestation letter. A master's of at least 8 months can lead to a 3-year post-graduation work permit, with CLB 7 or NCLC 7 in all four language skills. Your partner may get an open work permit only on some programmes, such as a master's of 16 months or longer: see [studying in Canada](/blog/study-in-canada/).

### South Korea

Seoul National University, as one example, lists KRW 3,828,000 a semester for engineering, based on the 2026 academic year. With TOPIK 4 and permission from the immigration office, master's students can usually work 30 hours a week in term. Graduates of Korean universities can apply for the D-10 job-seeking visa: see [studying in South Korea](/blog/study-in-south-korea/).

### Dubai branch campuses (UAE)

Some UK and Australian universities run campuses in Dubai, and an accredited university can sponsor your UAE student visa. Fees vary widely: Middlesex Dubai's MSc Data Science and Artificial Intelligence is AED 76,491 in total (September 2026), and Birmingham Dubai's MSc Accounting and Finance is AED 180,617 a year (September 2027). Dubai study alone does not lead to the UK Graduate visa or Australia's 485 visa: see [UK and Australian campuses in Dubai](/blog/branch-campuses-in-dubai/).

## What else should you compare?

The table leaves out three things that can change your choice.

- **Language at work.** In Germany, the Federal Employment Agency says many companies expect at least basic German. In South Korea, your TOPIK level sets how many hours you can work.
- **Family.** On a UK Student visa, you can bring a partner or children only on a PhD or other research-based postgraduate degree, or if you are government-sponsored. Since 2 October 2026, most Australian Student visa applicants cannot include family. Read [can you bring family on a UK Student visa?](/blog/uk-student-visa-dependants/)
- **Real living costs.** The money you show for a visa is a minimum. The University of Glasgow, for example, estimates £1,100 to £1,700 a month to live and study in Glasgow.

## How to choose between them

1. **Name the work.** Write down two or three job titles you would enjoy and be good at. Use titles you could find in a job advert.
2. **Find out how people get into it.** For each country, check the qualifications, any professional accreditation and the language employers expect.
3. **Check the route to a work visa.** Find out whether that job can lead to a work visa, and whether the post-study period gives you time to find it.
4. **Add up the full cost.** Count tuition for the whole course, the money to show, visa fees and your university's own living cost estimate.
5. **Check the rules for your life.** Look at work hours, family rules and the language you will need day to day.
6. **Shortlist two countries.** Compare two or three real courses in each, using fees from each course page.
7. **Plan backwards from the deadline.** Note the application deadline and the visa steps for each course, then set your dates.

## What are the common mistakes?

- Choosing a country because a friend or relative went there.
- Comparing tuition alone, without the money to show, visa fees and living costs.
- Assuming post-study rules will stay the same. The UK Graduate visa, for example, falls to 18 months for applications from 1 January 2027.
- Choosing an English-taught course in Germany or Korea with no plan to learn the local language.
- Assuming a Dubai campus leads to a UK or Australian post-study work visa.

## What to do next

1. Write down the two or three jobs you want to aim for.
2. Read the guide for each country on your shortlist, using the links above.
3. Compare real courses in your top two countries, with fees from each course page.
