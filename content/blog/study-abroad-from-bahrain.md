As of October 2026, Bahraini citizens need a UK Student visa for courses over 6 months, but may not need to send bank statements. If you live in Bahrain, you need no TB test. For a degree to count at home, Bahrain asks for a campus in the country of study, regular attendance and at least four years for a bachelor's. The Crown Prince's programme gives 20 scholarships a year.

## Where can you study from Bahrain?

You can study in the [UK](/blog/study-in/uk/), [Ireland](/blog/study-in/ireland/), the [USA](/blog/study-in/usa/), [Canada](/blog/study-in/canada/), [Europe](/blog/study-in/europe/), [Australia](/blog/study-in/australia/), [New Zealand](/blog/study-in/new-zealand/), [Singapore](/blog/study-in/singapore/), [Malaysia](/blog/study-in/malaysia/) or [Dubai](/blog/study-in/dubai/). This guide covers what is different for students from Bahrain. For the general steps, read [How to apply for a master's abroad](/blog/how-to-apply-for-a-masters-abroad/).

## What UK visa rules apply to Bahraini citizens?

For a course longer than 6 months, you need a Student visa. For a course of 6 months or less, you need an electronic travel authorisation (ETA) or a Standard Visitor visa. As a visitor, you can study at an accredited institution for up to 6 months, including English language courses. As of October 2026, an ETA costs £20.

Two rules work in your favour if you are a Bahraini citizen living in Bahrain:

- No TB test. Bahrain is not on the UK's list of countries where you need a tuberculosis test. You need one only if you have lived in a listed country for 6 months or more and were living there, or in another listed country, within the last 6 months.
- Fewer documents. Bahrain is on the list in paragraph ST 22.1 of Appendix Student. If you apply from the country where you live, or from the UK, you do not need to send your financial evidence with the application. The same goes for the documents you used to get your offer.

The decision maker may still ask for that evidence, so keep it ready. You must still hold the money for 28 days in a row. As of October 2026, that is £1,529 a month in London or £1,171 a month elsewhere, for up to 9 months. For applications made from 30 November 2026, the figures are £1,570 and £1,203. Applications made before that date use the old figures. [Bank statements for a UK Student visa](/blog/uk-student-visa-bank-statements/) explains how to show it.

To find the UK visa application centre, use GOV.UK's finder. For Bahrain, it links to the VFS Global page for the UK visa centre there. The fee, the health surcharge and the timeline are in the [UK Student visa guide](/blog/uk-student-visa-guide/).

## Which other visa rules depend on a Bahraini passport?

As of October 2026, from each government's official pages:

| Destination | Rule for Bahraini citizens |
|---|---|
| UAE (Dubai) | As a GCC citizen, no visa, entry permit or sponsor needed to enter |
| USA | F-1 student visa: no reciprocity fee, multiple entries, valid for up to 60 months |
| Canada | Visitor visa needed, not an eTA |
| Germany | Visa needed before you travel |

For the UAE, you show your passport or national ID card when you arrive. The UAE page covers entry only, so ask your university what it needs to enrol you. Bahrain's criteria for study abroad, below, apply to a campus in Dubai too. [UK and Australian university campuses in Dubai](/blog/branch-campuses-in-dubai/) explains what degree you get there.

## What if you live in Bahrain on another passport?

Visa rules follow your passport. The UK TB rule follows where you have lived.

- If you have lived in Bahrain for the last 6 months or more, you do not need a UK TB test. This is true even if your passport is from a listed country, such as India or Pakistan.
- The ST 22.1 exemption depends on your nationality. India and Pakistan are not on its list, so their citizens send full financial evidence and the documents used to get their offer.
- CPISP, below, is for Bahraini citizens only.

For the rules that follow your passport, see the Students from pages, for example [India](/blog/students-from/india/) and [Pakistan](/blog/students-from/pakistan/).

## How do universities abroad treat Bahraini qualifications?

Here are two examples from UK universities' own country pages, as of October 2026:

- The University of Glasgow does not accept Bahraini school certificates on their own. It asks for A-levels, the IB, Glasgow International College or another accepted qualification. For a master's that asks for a UK 2:1, it normally wants a bachelor's with a GPA of 2.8 or 78%.
- The University of Birmingham says holders of the Al-Thanawiyan are not normally eligible for direct entry to a bachelor's. It considers them for its foundation programme with an overall average of 85% or above. Its Engineering and Physical Sciences Pathway also needs 85% in Mathematics.
- For a master's, Birmingham considers four-year bachelor's degrees from recognised Bahraini universities. It expects a GPA of 3.0/4, 3.75/5 or 75% for a 2:1, and 2.8/4, 3.5/5 or 70% for a 2:2.

Neither page lists an English test waiver for Bahraini qualifications, so plan to take a test. See [Which English test do you need?](/blog/english-tests-for-study-abroad/) Each university sets its own rules, so check your course page too.

## Will Bahrain recognise your foreign degree?

Under Decree-Law No. 19 of 1995, the National Committee for Evaluation of Academic Qualifications assesses foreign qualifications. The Undersecretary of the Ministry of Education chairs it. On its proposal, the Minister of Education decides whether your qualification is equivalent to a Bahraini one.

Bahrain's eGovernment portal publishes criteria for joining institutions of higher education outside Bahrain. Check your choice against them before you accept an offer:

- The institution and the programme must be licensed and approved by the higher education and accreditation body in the country of study.
- The institution must have a campus in the country of study. It must not work as a broker for another institution's degree.
- The institution and the programme must be listed on the e-Government platform. The institution should appear in an international ranking.
- A bachelor's must be studied by regular attendance on campus. It must last at least four academic years or 120 accredited semester hours (or the equivalent), including any foundation year.
- You cannot skip the foundation year unless you hold A-levels or the IB.
- Students should not enrol in International Year One programmes offered by British institutions.
- A master's must last at least one academic year. You can study it part-time with academic supervision, except in medical, engineering, health and scientific subjects, which must be full-time.
- Medicine and dentistry must last at least five academic years.

If your bachelor's lasts three years, add your foundation year and check that the total meets the four-year rule. If you are unsure, ask the Ministry of Education before you enrol. If a four-year bachelor's appeals to you, read [Studying in Scotland: what is different](/blog/study-in-scotland/).

## What scholarships can Bahraini students apply for?

- The Crown Prince's International Scholarship Programme (CPISP). HRH Prince Salman bin Hamad Al Khalifa set it up in 1999. It gives fully funded university scholarships.
- To apply to CPISP, you must be a Bahraini citizen in 11th grade with a GPA of 97% or higher. Eligible applicants then take assessments of English and critical reasoning.
- CPISP gives 20 scholarships a year. It raised the number from 10 in May 2023. Scholars are not obliged to return to Bahrain or to work for a specific organisation.
- Fulbright. Bahrain is on the Fulbright Foreign Student Program's country list. It is for graduate students, young professionals and artists. You apply through the Fulbright office for your country, which is a Fulbright Commission or the US Embassy.
- Chevening. As of October 2026, Bahrain is not in Chevening's list of countries you can apply from.

For other schemes, see [Government scholarships for international students](/blog/government-scholarships-for-international-students/).

## Which documents need extra steps?

Bahrain is a party to the Hague Apostille Convention. It came into force for Bahrain on 31 December 2013. An apostille is a stamped official certificate. It confirms that the signature, stamp or seal on a document matches official records.

If you are asked to legalise a UK certificate, the UK Legalisation Office adds the apostille. As of October 2026, it costs £45 per document, plus postage or courier, and usually takes up to 25 working days. A qualification certificate must first be certified by a UK public official, such as a notary or a solicitor.

## What to do next

1. Choose your course, then check the institution and the programme against Bahrain's criteria and the e-Government list.
2. If you are in 11th grade with a GPA of 97% or more, apply to CPISP.
3. Check whether your school certificate needs a foundation year first, and book your English test.
4. Keep your money in your account for 28 days, even if you may not need to send statements.
