As of October 2026, Pakistani students need a TB test from an approved clinic for a UK Student visa. They must also send full proof of money. Pakistan is not on the US entry restriction lists in force since 1 January 2026. The State Bank of Pakistan lets banks send up to US$70,000 a year for each student, and HEC issues equivalence letters for foreign degrees.

## Where can you study from Pakistan?

You can apply to universities in the [UK](/blog/study-in/uk/), the [USA](/blog/study-in/usa/), [Canada](/blog/study-in/canada/), [Australia](/blog/study-in/australia/), [Ireland](/blog/study-in/ireland/) and the rest of [Europe](/blog/study-in/europe/), among others. This guide covers only what is different for students from Pakistan. For the general steps, read [How to apply for a master's abroad, step by step](/blog/how-to-apply-for-a-masters-abroad/).

Visa rules depend on your nationality, and where you give fingerprints depends on where you live. Here, the visa rules are for Pakistani passport holders, and the centres are those in Pakistan.

## What visa rules apply to Pakistani citizens?

### UK

You need a tuberculosis (TB) test if all of these are true:

- you are coming to the UK for 6 months or more
- you have lived in a listed country for 6 months or more
- you were living there within the last 6 months

Pakistan is on the gov.uk list. The rule depends on where you have lived, not on your passport.

The test must be at a clinic approved by the Home Office. The gov.uk page for Pakistan, updated 20 February 2026, lists approved clinics in Islamabad, Lahore, Karachi and Mirpur (AJK). Where it shows a fee, it is PKR 14,000 to PKR 15,000 for applicants aged 11 and over. The certificate is valid for 6 months from the date of your x-ray.

Pakistan is not on the differential evidence list in the gov.uk money rules, so send full evidence of your money. See [Bank statements for a UK Student visa](/blog/uk-student-visa-bank-statements/).

To give your fingerprints and photo, gov.uk's [list of visa application centres](https://www.gov.uk/find-a-visa-application-centre) links Pakistan to centres run by VFS Global. Check the cities and opening times there.

Pakistan is not on the UK's visa brake list. Since 26 March 2026, that rule has refused Student visa applications made outside the UK by main applicants who are nationals of [Afghanistan](/blog/students-from/afghanistan/), Cameroon, Myanmar and Sudan.

### USA

Presidential Proclamation 10998 restricts US visas for nationals of the countries it lists. It took effect on 1 January 2026. Pakistan is not on its full or its partial list, as shown on the State Department page last updated 2 February 2026.

The proclamation requires a report every 180 days on whether to continue, end or change the suspensions. So the lists can change, and you should check the State Department page before you apply.

As of October 2026, the State Department's reciprocity table for Pakistan covers F-1 student visas. It lists no reciprocity fee, multiple entries and a validity period of 60 months.

### Canada

Canada has visa application centres for biometrics in Islamabad, Karachi and Lahore (canada.ca, last modified 24 August 2026).

### Germany and the Schengen area

Pakistani nationals need an airport transit visa to change planes at a German airport. The Federal Foreign Office lists exemptions, such as a valid visa from an EU or Schengen state, Canada, Japan or the USA. A UK visa is not on that list. Check this before you book a flight to the UK through Germany.

## How do UK universities treat Pakistani qualifications?

Each university sets its own rules. Three examples from their Pakistan pages, as of October 2026:

- **University of Glasgow:** for undergraduate entry, its page says "Country qualifications not accepted". You need A-levels, the IB, Glasgow International College or another accepted qualification. For a master's, it normally accepts a four-year bachelor's. A course that asks for a UK 2:1 needs 58% to 68%, or a GPA of 2.9 to 3.25, depending on the institution you attended.
- **Liverpool John Moores University (LJMU):** it asks for the HSSC with at least 75% for undergraduate entry. For a master's, it accepts a four-year bachelor's with 55% or 2.5 out of 4.0. A two-year bachelor's counts only with a two-year master's, at 55% or 2.7 out of 4.0. Its page also says it "will not be accepting applications from Pakistan for the academic year 2026/27".
- **Kingston University:** it asks for the HSSC with 65% overall, plus IELTS 6.0. For a master's, it asks for a four-year bachelor's with a GPA of 2.5, or a master's taken after a two-year or three-year bachelor's.

In all three examples, a two-year bachelor's on its own is not listed for master's entry.

For English, Kingston says the HSSC is "Not accepted as proof of CEFR B2 English". For postgraduate study, it considers proof that you studied fully in English at an institution on its approved list. LJMU does not accept LanguageCert, Pearson Versant or ELLT from students from Pakistan. See [Which English test do you need?](/blog/english-tests-for-study-abroad/)

## How does HEC recognise a foreign degree?

The Higher Education Commission (HEC) issues equivalence letters for foreign degrees, and you apply online. As of October 2026, the fee is PKR 5,000 for each degree, diploma or certificate. It does not cover any verification fee your foreign university charges.

HEC asks for:

- your CNIC (or Pakistan Origin Card)
- the degree, and the transcript or diploma supplement
- the main page of your passport
- the visa and the entry and exit stamps for your study period
- registration with your professional body where it applies, such as the Pakistan Engineering Council

You must also send HEC proof that your degree is genuine. It can be an email from your university's official address, or a sealed letter.

Check these points before you choose a course:

- HEC says "it is mandatory that the university must be recognized by the government of respective country".
- HEC treats an online degree the same as a campus degree only if three things are true. The university is accredited by a body HEC recognises, it runs both campus and distance programmes, and the two degrees cannot be told apart.
- For a foreign degree taught in Pakistan, check HEC's approved list of institutions offering degrees with foreign collaboration.
- HEC "determines only the level of education". Its equivalence letter does not show your CGPA or grades.

## How much money can you send abroad from Pakistan?

The State Bank of Pakistan's Foreign Exchange Manual (chapter 17, paragraph 38) sets the rules. As of October 2026, banks can send up to US$70,000, or the same in another currency, per student per calendar year. The limit covers application fees, tuition, living costs and similar costs.

To send tuition and living costs, you must already have your admission. Your bank will ask for:

- the application form in Appendix V-82
- copies of your CNIC or Form B, and your parent's or guardian's CNIC
- a copy of your passport and your letter of admission
- a cost sheet from the institution showing the costs

Tuition and other dues must go directly to the institution's account. Living costs that the institution does not collect can be sent to you. For your first boarding and lodging costs, banks can release cash equal to US$5,000, endorsed on your passport.

Requests above the limit go, with reasons, to the SBP Banking Services Corporation in Karachi.

## Which scholarships can Pakistani students apply for?

Each scheme below lists Pakistan, as of October 2026. See also [Government scholarships for international students](/blog/government-scholarships-for-international-students/).

- **Commonwealth Scholarships (UK):** the 2027/28 Commonwealth Master's round closed on 20 October 2026. In Pakistan, HEC runs the nominations. For 2026/27 entry, its criteria asked for a first division in a 16-year degree and a HAT score of at least 60 out of 100. Commonwealth Shared Scholarships, through UK universities, open on 10 November 2026 and close at 16:00 GMT on 8 December 2026.
- **Chevening (UK):** the Pakistan page says applications for 2027-2028 are closed.
- **GREAT Scholarships (UK):** for 2026-27, 13 UK universities offered £10,000 towards tuition for a one-year taught postgraduate course. You must hold a Pakistani passport and be a permanent resident of Pakistan. Check the British Council page for 2027-28.
- **Fulbright (USA):** the US Embassy in Pakistan says Fulbright funds a master's or PhD in the USA, and points to USEFP for details. Awards cover tuition, books, airfare, a living stipend and health insurance.
- **HEC Overseas Scholarships for PhD in Selected Fields (Phase III):** HEC aims to train 1,800 PhD scholars at universities abroad. You must be a Pakistani or AJK national with 18 years of education, and a CGPA of 3.0 or a first division. The age limit is 35, or 40 for some public sector staff. These criteria are for a call that closed on 30 May 2021, so check HEC for a new call.
- **Australia Awards:** Pakistan is a participating country, and DFAT lists a contact in Pakistan for enquiries.

## Which documents need attesting?

- **Degrees and transcripts:** HEC attests Pakistani degrees. You apply on the HEC e-portal, pay online, then send the originals of all your qualifications from matric onward by courier. Urgent walk-in cases are returned the same day. As of October 2026, the fee is PKR 1,000 for each original and PKR 700 for each photocopy.
- **SSC and HSSC certificates:** the Inter Boards Coordination Commission (IBCC) attests certificates issued by boards. Your board must verify them first, and the verification must show on the IBCC portal before you apply. Only blood relatives can apply for you.

Ask each university and embassy which attested documents it needs.

## What to do next

1. Read the Pakistan page of each university on your list, for its entry rules and any pause on applications.
2. Book your TB test so the certificate is still valid on the day you apply. It lasts 6 months from your x-ray.
3. Start HEC and IBCC attestation early, and plan your fee payments within the SBP limit.
4. Note the Commonwealth Shared Scholarships window: 10 November to 8 December 2026.
