As of October 2026, Iranian nationals can apply for a UK Student visa, and Iran is not on the UK TB test list. Some courses need ATAS clearance. Several UK universities say they cannot, or may be unable to, accept payments from Iran. The USA has fully suspended visas for Iranian nationals since 1 January 2026, and Canada has no visa application centre in Iran.

## Where can you study from Iran?

You can apply to universities in the [UK](/blog/study-in/uk/), [Germany and the rest of Europe](/blog/study-in/europe/), [Canada](/blog/study-in/canada/), [Australia](/blog/study-in/australia/), [Malaysia](/blog/study-in/malaysia/), [Dubai](/blog/study-in/dubai/) and elsewhere. The [USA](/blog/study-in/usa/) has suspended visas for Iranian nationals, as set out below. This guide covers only what is different for students from Iran.

Visa rules follow your nationality. Where you give your fingerprints usually depends on where you live, so an Iranian citizen living in another country usually applies there.

## What visa rules apply to Iranian citizens?

### UK

Gov.uk's [list of visa application centres](https://www.gov.uk/find-a-visa-application-centre), checked on 22 October 2026, links Iran to VFS Global's site. It does not tell applicants in Iran to use another country, as it does for [Afghanistan](/blog/students-from/afghanistan/). Check the centre's own page for opening times before you apply.

Iran is not on the gov.uk TB test list. You still need a test if all of these are true:

- you are coming to the UK for 6 months or more
- you have lived in a listed country, such as Afghanistan or Pakistan, for 6 months or more
- you were living there within the last 6 months

Iran is not on the differential evidence list in the gov.uk money rules, so send full evidence of your money. See [Bank statements for a UK Student visa](/blog/uk-student-visa-bank-statements/).

Iran is not on the UK's visa brake list. Since 26 March 2026, that rule has refused Student visa applications made outside the UK by main applicants who are nationals of Afghanistan, Cameroon, Myanmar and Sudan.

### Do you need ATAS?

ATAS (the Academic Technology Approval Scheme) applies to all nationalities except those on gov.uk's exempt list. Iran is not on that list. So if your course needs ATAS, you must get the certificate before you apply for your Student visa.

Your subject has a CAH3 code, which your university will typically put on your offer letter. Gov.uk has an online check of whether that subject needs ATAS. ATAS is free and takes at least 30 working days (6 weeks), and processing may take longer from April to August. A certificate is valid for 6 months for a visa application.

### USA

Presidential Proclamation 10998 took effect on 1 January 2026. Under it, the State Department "fully suspended visa issuance to nationals of 19 countries", including Iran. The suspension covers all nonimmigrant and immigrant visa categories, so it includes student visas.

The State Department lists exceptions, including:

- "Dual nationals applying with a passport of a nationality not subject to a suspension"
- lawful permanent residents of the USA
- people whose travel the State Department finds would serve the US national interest, case by case

Another exception covers "Immigrant visas for ethnic and religious minorities facing persecution in Iran". It is for immigrant visas, not student visas.

People who held valid visas on 1 January 2026 are not subject to the proclamation. No visas issued before that date "have been or will be revoked pursuant to the Proclamation". The proclamation requires a report every 180 days on whether the suspensions should be "continued, terminated, modified, or supplemented".

Iran is not on the Fulbright Foreign Student Program's country list. Its site says that if your country is not listed, you are not eligible to apply.

### Canada

Canada has no visa application centre in Iran. Canada.ca says: "If there's no VAC in the country or territory you're applying from, you can go to any other VAC." Make sure you can enter that country before you book your biometrics appointment.

### Germany

The German Embassy in Tehran's study visa leaflet says you need €992 a month, or €11,904 for each study year, for living costs. It gives these as the 2026 figures. It accepts these kinds of proof:

- a blocked account at a German bank holding the first year's sum, with only one-twelfth released each month
- a formal declaration of commitment (Verpflichtungserklärung) under sections 66 to 68 of the Residence Act, from someone with permanent residence in Germany, no older than 6 months
- a scholarship from German public funds, a recognised German funding organisation, the Iranian Ministry of Education and Research (as the leaflet names it), or Iranian educational institutions
- a signed blocked account declaration, with a bank-confirmed statement showing the full sum

With the last option, the leaflet says your visa will carry a condition. You must carry the amount in cash when you enter Germany, then open a blocked account there and pay it in. German customs says you must declare cash of €10,000 or more in writing when you enter Germany from outside the EU.

Iranian nationals also need an airport transit visa to change planes at a German airport. The Federal Foreign Office lists exemptions, such as a valid visa from an EU or Schengen state, Canada, Japan or the USA. For the wider German steps, see [Studying in Germany as an international student](/blog/study-in-germany/).

## How do UK universities read Iranian qualifications?

Two examples from each university's Iran page, as of October 2026:

- **University of Glasgow:** for undergraduate entry, it accepts the High School Diploma taken over 4 years. An average score of 19 matches AAA at A-level, and 18 matches AAB. For a master's, it normally asks for a bachelor's with 14/20 where a UK 2:1 is needed, or 13/20 for a 2:2.
- **Nottingham Trent University (NTU):** it considers the High School Diploma (Metevaseth, year 12) with 14/20 for direct entry to a bachelor's. For a master's, a 2:1 means 13/20 or 65% from a state university, or 14/20 or 70% from a private one. A 2:2 means 11/20 or 55% from a state university, or 12/20 or 60% from a private one.

At NTU, the grade you need depends on whether your university is state or private. Neither page lists an English test waiver for Iranian qualifications, so plan for a test. See [Which English test do you need?](/blog/english-tests-for-study-abroad/)

## How do you pay UK fees from Iran?

Plan this before you accept an offer. Several UK universities say they cannot, or may be unable to, take payments from Iran, because of sanctions. Examples from their own pages, as of October 2026:

- **Queen Mary University of London:** "We cannot accept payments from sanctioned countries", and its list includes Iran. Students linked to a sanctioned country must fill in a Pre-pay advice form before they try to pay.
- **University of Leeds:** "We may be unable to accept payment from Belarus, Cuba, Iran, North Korea, Russia, Syria, Ukraine or Venezuela." It screens such payments closely, and restrictions also apply to refunds.
- **Royal Holloway, University of London:** its Flywire service cannot legally accept payments from sanctioned countries, including Iran. You cannot use Convera GlobalPay if you or the payer live in Iran.
- **Brunel University London:** students must not pay the university from Iran, "nor where source of fund is a sanction country".

Ask your university's finance team in writing how you can pay, and who can pay for you, before you accept. Ask the same about your deposit and any refund.

## How will your degree be recognised in Iran?

Check this before you choose a university, a branch campus or an online course. Ask Iran's Ministry of Science, Research and Technology how it will treat your degree. Its website, msrt.ir, could not be read for this guide in October 2026, so its rules are not summarised here.

## Which scholarships can Iranian students apply for?

- **Chevening (UK):** Iran has a Chevening page, and applications are closed as of October 2026. The page says: "You should take independent advice about how your participation in the Chevening scheme might be perceived in your own country, including the possible reaction of your own Government as well as any related risk." It also asks you to "refrain from publicly advertising your application, including on social media."
- **Germany:** a scholarship counts as proof of money for your visa if it comes from one of the sources the Tehran leaflet lists, shown in the Germany section above.
- **Fulbright (USA):** not open to applicants from Iran, because Iran is not on its country list.

For other schemes, see [Government scholarships for international students](/blog/government-scholarships-for-international-students/), and check that each one lists Iran.

## Which documents need translating for Germany?

The German Embassy in Tehran says documents must be in German, or come with a German translation pre-certified by the Iranian Foreign Ministry. It also says educational documents no longer need to be legalised by the German embassy. Bring them to your appointment sorted in the order the leaflet lists.

## What to do next

1. Before you accept a UK offer, ask the university in writing how you can pay your deposit and fees.
2. Check whether your course needs ATAS as soon as you have an offer, and allow at least 6 weeks.
3. For Germany, choose your proof of money from the Tehran leaflet. If you carry €10,000 or more in cash, declare it in writing at customs.
4. Ask Iran's Ministry of Science, Research and Technology how it will treat your degree before you enrol.
