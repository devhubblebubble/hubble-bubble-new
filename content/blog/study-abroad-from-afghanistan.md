As of October 2026, Afghan nationals cannot get a UK Student visa by applying from outside the UK. Applications submitted after 12:01am on 26 March 2026 are refused under the UK visa brake. The USA has fully suspended visas for Afghan nationals since 1 January 2026. Germany takes student visa applications from Afghans in Islamabad, and Canada lets you use a centre in another country.

## Where can you study from Afghanistan?

Two rules shape your choices in 2026. The [UK](/blog/study-in/uk/) refuses Student visa applications made outside the UK by Afghan nationals, and the [USA](/blog/study-in/usa/) has suspended visas for them. Other destinations, such as [Germany and the rest of Europe](/blog/study-in/europe/), [Canada](/blog/study-in/canada/), [Australia](/blog/study-in/australia/) and [Malaysia](/blog/study-in/malaysia/), set their own visa steps. Check each one on its official site.

These rules follow your nationality, not where you live. So an Afghan citizen living in [Pakistan](/blog/students-from/pakistan/) or [Iran](/blog/students-from/iran/) is still covered by the UK and US rules below. Where you give your fingerprints usually depends on where you live.

| Date | Change |

## What are the UK rules for Afghan nationals?

### The visa brake

Gov.uk says any Student visa applications submitted online after 12:01am on 26 March 2026 by main applicants who are Afghan nationals will be refused. The brake applies to applications made outside the UK. Gov.uk says this "includes if you already have either a valid Certificate of Sponsorship (CoS) or a valid Confirmation of Acceptance for Studies (CAS)". It covers Skilled Worker visas for Afghan nationals too.

The brake does not cancel visas already granted. If you are already in the UK and eligible, you can still apply to extend your visa or switch route there.

Gov.uk also says:

- "The visa brake is based only on the main nationality you give on your application form."
- "The visa brake will be reviewed regularly."
- "It is not permanent, but it will only end when the UK government considers it appropriate to do so."

Any change will be published on [gov.uk](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/visa-brake-changes-to-the-uk-visa-system).

Universities have updated their pages too. For example, Nottingham Trent University (NTU) says that "due to changes in UK Government policy, we are currently no longer able to sponsor Student visas from Afghanistan." It adds: "If you are already in the UK there is no change."

### Where you apply and the TB test

Two other UK rules depend on where you live. They apply to anyone living in Afghanistan, whatever their passport.

- **Visa application centre:** gov.uk's [list of centres](https://www.gov.uk/find-a-visa-application-centre) tells applicants in Afghanistan to choose a centre in a different country.
- **TB test:** Afghanistan is on the gov.uk TB test list. Gov.uk lists one approved clinic, the Afghanistan Health Assessment Centre at Motahar Hospital in Kabul. Its fee was US$120 for applicants aged 11 and over when gov.uk last updated the page, on 16 February 2024. A certificate is valid for 6 months from the x-ray.

## What are the US rules for Afghan nationals?

Presidential Proclamation 10998 took effect on 1 January 2026. Under it, the State Department "fully suspended visa issuance to nationals of 19 countries", including Afghanistan. The suspension covers all nonimmigrant and immigrant visa categories, so it includes student visas.

The State Department lists exceptions, including:

- "Dual nationals applying with a passport of a nationality not subject to a suspension"
- lawful permanent residents of the USA
- people whose travel the State Department finds would serve the US national interest, case by case

People who held valid visas on 1 January 2026 are not subject to the proclamation. No visas issued before that date "have been or will be revoked pursuant to the Proclamation".

The proclamation requires a report every 180 days on whether the suspensions should be "continued, terminated, modified, or supplemented". So the rule can change.

Afghanistan appears on the Fulbright Foreign Student Program's country list. Any Fulbright award would still need a US visa.

## What are the rules for Canada and Germany?

### Canada

Canada has no visa application centre in Afghanistan. Canada.ca says: "If there's no VAC in the country or territory you're applying from, you can go to any other VAC." It lists centres in Islamabad, Karachi and Lahore, for example. Make sure you can enter that country before you book.

### Germany

The German Embassy in Kabul says its visa section "remains closed until further notice". Its page on work and study visas, dated 21 April 2026, says: "Applications for national visa (e.g. employment or students) may now be submitted in Islamabad."

Since 1 June 2026, study visa applications are made online through the Consular Service Portal. Once your documents are uploaded, you book an appointment that fits your Pakistani entry visa. You only need to travel shortly before your confirmed appointment.

The page also says: "Depending on the purpose of your trip, it can take up to six months to check your application." You also need proof of money, for example a blocked account. See [Studying in Germany as an international student](/blog/study-in-germany/).

Afghan nationals need an airport transit visa to change planes at a German airport. The Federal Foreign Office lists exemptions, such as a valid visa from an EU or Schengen state, Canada, Japan or the USA.

## How do UK universities read Afghan qualifications?

These rules matter if you can apply from inside the UK, because the visa brake covers applications made outside it. Two examples from each university's Afghanistan page, as of October 2026:

- **Nottingham Trent University:** for a bachelor's degree, it asks for the Technicum, Technical or Grade 14 Diploma with 65% or more. For a master's, it asks for 80% or more in a BSc or BEng from Kabul University. It assesses Afghan master's degrees case by case.
- **University of Lincoln:** for year one of a bachelor's degree, it asks for a recognised International Foundation programme, the IB with 27 to 34 points, or three A Levels.

Neither page lists an English test waiver for Afghan qualifications. Both refer to IELTS. See [Which English test do you need?](/blog/english-tests-for-study-abroad/)

## Which scholarships are open to Afghan students?

- **DAFI (UNHCR) in Pakistan:** for registered Afghan refugees with a valid Proof of Registration (PoR) card. It funds study at HEC-recognised universities, colleges and polytechnics in Pakistan. Support includes tuition, study materials, transport, accommodation and other allowances.
- **DAFI (UNHCR) in Iran:** UNHCR Iran says refugee students can apply through its partner FDA. The scholarship covers tuition fees and living costs for undergraduate study.
- **Hilde Domin Programme (DAAD, Germany):** for students and doctoral candidates from any country who are at risk of being denied educational or other rights at home. You cannot apply yourself. A university or other eligible organisation in Germany must nominate you.

For other schemes, see [Government scholarships for international students](/blog/government-scholarships-for-international-students/). Check that each scheme's country list includes Afghanistan before you apply.

## Which documents should you prepare?

- For Germany: your documents uploaded to the Consular Service Portal, and a Pakistani entry visa that covers your appointment in Islamabad.
- For a flight that changes planes in Germany: an airport transit visa, unless an exemption applies.
- For a UK visa, if the TB rules apply to you: a certificate from an approved clinic, valid for 6 months from the x-ray.
- For DAFI in Pakistan: a valid PoR card.

## What to do next

1. Check the gov.uk visa brake page and the State Department page before you plan on the UK or the USA. Both rules are under review.
2. If you choose Germany, set up your Consular Service Portal account and plan your Pakistani entry visa around the appointment.
3. If you are a registered refugee in Pakistan or Iran, ask UNHCR about DAFI.
4. Check the transit rules for every country on your flight route.
