The main government scholarships for 2027 entry are Chevening, Commonwealth and GREAT Scholarships in the UK, DAAD in Germany, the Government of Ireland scholarship, the Global Korea Scholarship and Australia Awards. As of October 2026, Chevening and the Commonwealth Master's round for 2027/28 have closed. Commonwealth Shared Scholarships open on 10 November 2026 and close on 8 December 2026.

## Which government scholarships can you apply for?

Each row comes from the scheme's official site, as of October 2026.

| Scheme | Who can apply | What it pays | 2027 entry window |
|---|---|---|---|
| Chevening (UK) | Citizens of eligible countries with 2 years' work experience after their degree | Tuition, monthly stipend, flights, visa fee | Opened August 2026, closed October 2026 |
| Commonwealth Master's (UK) | Citizens of 43 eligible Commonwealth countries | Tuition, £1,712 a month (£2,000 in London), airfare | Closed 20 October 2026 |
| Commonwealth Shared (UK) | The same 43 countries, through a UK university | Tuition, £1,712 a month (£2,000 in London), airfare | 10 November to 8 December 2026 |
| GREAT (UK) | Citizens of 18 listed countries | £10,000 towards tuition | Set by each university |
| DAAD (Germany) | Graduates, with programmes by country | €992 a month for students (current example) | Set by programme and country |
| Government of Ireland | Students from outside the EU/EEA, Switzerland and UK | €10,000 plus a full fee waiver | Last round closed 12 March 2026 |
| Global Korea Scholarship | Non-Korean citizens, graduate applicants under 40 | Airfare, Korean course, tuition, monthly allowance | Graduate round February to March |
| Australia Awards | Citizens of participating countries | Government-funded full-time study | Dates set by country |

## Who can get a Chevening Scholarship?

Chevening Scholarships are funded by the UK Government and pay for a one-year master's at a UK university. They cover:

- tuition fees
- a monthly living allowance, set by whether you study in London
- economy travel to and from your country, plus arrival and departure allowances
- your visa application fee, and up to £75 for a TB test where needed

To apply, you must:

- be a citizen of a Chevening-eligible country or territory
- have an undergraduate degree that lets you enter a UK master's
- have at least two years of work experience gained after your undergraduate degree, equivalent to 2,800 hours
- apply to three different eligible UK courses and get an unconditional offer from at least one by the deadline
- commit to return to your home country for at least two years after the scholarship ends

You cannot apply if you hold British or dual British citizenship (with some exceptions), or if you have studied in the UK before on a UK government-funded scholarship.

For 2027/28, applications opened in August 2026 and closed in October 2026. Results come from mid-June 2027, and the deadline to submit an unconditional university offer is 17:00 BST on 8 July 2027. If you missed this round, watch the [Chevening application timeline](https://www.chevening.org/scholarships/application-timeline/) for the next one.

## Who can apply for a Commonwealth Scholarship?

The Commonwealth Scholarship Commission in the UK (CSC) funds master's and PhD study for people from eligible Commonwealth countries. For 2027/28, 43 countries are eligible for its master's schemes, including Bangladesh, Ghana, India, Kenya, Nigeria, Pakistan, South Africa and Sri Lanka.

Both master's schemes pay approved tuition fees and an approved return airfare. The stipend is £1,712 a month, or £2,000 a month at universities in the London area. You must:

- be a citizen of, or have refugee status in, an eligible Commonwealth country, and live there permanently
- hold at least an upper second-class (2:1) first degree, or a lower second plus a relevant postgraduate qualification
- be able to start in the UK by September 2027
- be unable to afford to study in the UK without the scholarship

There are two master's routes for 2027/28:

- Commonwealth Master's Scholarships: you apply to a national nominator as well as the CSC. The CSC closing date was 16:00 BST on 20 October 2026, and nominators set their own closing dates. This round is closed.
- Commonwealth Shared Scholarships: you apply through a participating UK university. Applications open on 10 November 2026 and close at 16:00 GMT on 8 December 2026. The CSC also says you must "not have studied or worked for more than one (academic) year or more in a high income country".

The CSC also offers Commonwealth PhD Scholarships for 17 least developed countries and vulnerable states. The 2027/28 round closed on 20 October 2026.

## What is a GREAT Scholarship?

GREAT Scholarships give £10,000 towards tuition fees for a one-year taught postgraduate course in the UK. Each one is jointly funded by the UK government's GREAT Britain Campaign and the British Council, with participating UK universities.

For 2026-27, over 140 scholarships were offered by over 60 universities, for students from 18 countries: Bangladesh, China, Egypt, France, Ghana, Greece, India, Indonesia, Italy, Kenya, Malaysia, Mexico, Nigeria, Pakistan, Spain, Thailand, Turkey and Vietnam.

You apply through each university's scholarship page, and each university sets its own deadline. The India page, for example, asks for an Indian passport and permanent residence, an undergraduate degree and the university's English requirement.

As of October 2026, the British Council page lists the 2026-27 scholarships, so check it for the 2027-28 list.

## Can you get a DAAD scholarship for a master's in Germany?

DAAD scholarships do not have to be repaid. DAAD gives its current monthly payments as an example: €992 for students and €1,300 for doctoral students. A flat-rate travel allowance is paid, and health, accident and personal liability insurance is often included.

DAAD says its master's scholarships are mainly for people from industrial countries. For developing and emerging countries, it names programmes such as Development-Related Postgraduate Courses (EPOS) and study scholarships for STEM subjects.

Deadlines depend on the programme and your country, so read the call for your programme in the DAAD scholarship database. For EPOS courses in the 2027/2028 intake, each course gives its own deadline on its website. See also [Studying in Germany as an international student](/blog/study-in-germany/).

## What does the Government of Ireland scholarship pay?

The Government of Ireland International Education Scholarship (GOI-IES) pays a €10,000 stipend for one year of full-time study. Your Irish institution also waives your full tuition fee and registration charge. There are 60 scholarships a year.

To apply, you need:

- a domiciliary of origin outside the EU/EEA, Switzerland and the United Kingdom
- an offer, when you apply, for a full-time, in-person course at an eligible Irish institution
- a course that is a taught master's of at least one year, a level 9 postgraduate diploma, or a research master's or PhD
- no previous GOI-IES scholarship

Russian and Belarusian citizens cannot apply.

The last round, for 2026/27, closed at 5pm Irish time on 12 March 2026. As of October 2026, the Higher Education Authority (HEA) page still shows that call, so check it for 2027 dates. See also [Studying in Ireland as an international student](/blog/study-in-ireland/).

## How does the Global Korea Scholarship work?

Study in Korea describes the Global Korea Scholarship (GKS) as a government-invited scholarship for degree study. It covers airfare, Korean language training, tuition and a monthly allowance.

For a graduate degree:

- you and both your parents must hold non-Korean citizenship
- you must be under 40
- you need a bachelor's degree, or a master's for a doctorate
- you need an average of at least 80% in your most recent programme

Graduate scholars study Korean for a year, then take a 2-year master's or a 3-year doctorate.

Study in Korea lists the graduate round as February to March, with final results in June. As of October 2026, the 2027 graduate round had not opened. The 2027 undergraduate guidelines, for applicants under 25, were posted on 9 September 2026. See also [Studying in South Korea as an international student](/blog/study-in-south-korea/).

## Which countries can apply for Australia Awards?

Australia Awards Scholarships are for citizens of participating countries, for full-time undergraduate or postgraduate study at Australian institutions. Study Australia describes them as government-funded grants for students from a selection of developing countries. The aim is that scholars return home and contribute to their country's development.

Participating countries are in Asia, the Pacific and Africa. They include Bangladesh, Nepal, Pakistan, Sri Lanka, Egypt, Ghana, Kenya, Nigeria and South Africa. As of October 2026, India and the Gulf states are not on the list.

The Department of Foreign Affairs and Trade (DFAT) does not publish one set of dates. It points applicants to the Australia Awards contact for their country, listed on its [participating countries page](https://www.dfat.gov.au/people-to-people/australia-awards/participating-countries). See also [Studying in Australia as an international student](/blog/study-in-australia/).

## What are the common mistakes with scholarships?

- Missing a step. Commonwealth Master's applicants apply to a national nominator as well as the CSC.
- Applying to a scheme your country is not on. Check the official country list first.
- Paying someone to apply for you. Apply only through the scheme's official site, your university or an official nominator.
