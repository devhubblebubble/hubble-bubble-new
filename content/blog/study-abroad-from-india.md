As of October 2026, if you live in India, a UK Student visa needs a TB test and proof of money. Germany asks for an APS certificate when your qualifications are from India, and some master's applicants also need the dMAT. Under the RBI's Liberalised Remittance Scheme, you can send up to US$250,000 a year. Back home, the UGC grants equivalence to foreign degrees.

## What are your options?

You can study in the [UK](/blog/study-in/uk/), [Ireland](/blog/study-in/ireland/), the [USA](/blog/study-in/usa/), [Canada](/blog/study-in/canada/), [Germany and the rest of Europe](/blog/study-in/europe/), [Australia](/blog/study-in/australia/), [New Zealand](/blog/study-in/new-zealand/), [Singapore](/blog/study-in/singapore/), [Malaysia](/blog/study-in/malaysia/) or [Dubai](/blog/study-in/dubai/). Each link leads to that country's main guide.

This guide covers only what is different for Indian citizens and for people who live in India. To compare countries, read [which country should you study in](/blog/which-country-should-you-study-in/).

## What is different for Indians on a UK Student visa?

### Do you need a TB test?

You need one if all of these are true:

- you are coming to the UK for 6 months or more
- you have lived in India for 6 months or more
- you lived there within the last 6 months

The rule follows where you have lived, not your passport. If you live in another country, check it on the [gov.uk list](https://www.gov.uk/tb-test-visa/countries-where-you-need-a-tb-test-to-enter-the-uk).

You must use a clinic approved by the Home Office. The gov.uk list for India, updated on 16 October 2026, has clinics in more than 20 cities. Each clinic sets its own fee: ₹1,800 to ₹3,700 for anyone aged 11 or over.

Tell the clinic the test is for a UK visa, and bring the passport you will apply with. The certificate is valid for 6 months from your x-ray.

### Do you have to send bank statements?

Yes. Paragraph ST 22.1 of Appendix Student lists the nationalities that can skip sending proof of money. As of October 2026, India is not on it.

So send your financial evidence with your application. Read [bank statements for a UK Student visa](/blog/uk-student-visa-bank-statements/), and [education loans for a UK Student visa](/blog/education-loan-uk-student-visa/) if you are borrowing.

### Where do you give your fingerprints?

UK visa application centres in India are run by VFS Global. The gov.uk [list of visa application centres](https://www.gov.uk/find-a-visa-application-centre) links to VFS for addresses. For fees and timings, see the [UK Student visa guide](/blog/uk-student-visa-guide/).

## What do Germany, Ireland, Australia and the USA ask of Indians?

### Germany: the APS certificate and the dMAT

APS India checks academic documents from Indian institutions. It says its certificate is "generally required" for the German student visa.

Checks take at least two weeks, and paying by manual bank transfer slows them down. The certificate comes as a PDF, so keep the original file.

From the summer semester 2027 intake, some master's applicants from India also need the dMAT, an aptitude test. It applies if your previous degree is in an affected field. As of October 2026, APS India says it will publish the updated list of fields shortly.

- The fee is €150, paid when you register.
- Bachelor's and PhD applicants are not affected.
- You are exempt if you registered for APS, or sent complete APS documents, before 29 June 2026.
- You are also exempt if you already hold an APS certificate.
- The next test cycles are in mid-January and mid-March.

The German Missions in India charge a €75 visa fee, payable only in rupees. See [studying in Germany](/blog/study-in-germany/).

### Ireland: DigiLocker copies and sponsor money

Indian citizens need a visa for Ireland. The study visa checklist of the Embassy of Ireland in New Delhi, dated 8 July 2025, makes two points:

- applicants from India should send DigiLocker versions of their qualifications with the originals, where possible
- in its section on sponsors, gold valuations, chit funds and volatile funds, such as crypto or market-linked funds, are not accepted

### Australia: longer stays after you graduate

Under the Australia-India Economic Cooperation and Trade Agreement, Indian nationals get longer stays on the Temporary Graduate visa. Study Australia lists these for its Post-Higher Education Work stream:

| Your qualification | Stay for Indian nationals |
|---|---|
| Bachelor's, including honours | Up to 2 years |
| Bachelor's with first class honours in STEM, including ICT | Up to 3 years |
| Master's (coursework, extended or research) | Up to 3 years |
| PhD | Up to 4 years |

Other graduates usually get 2 to 3 years. You must be 35 or under when you apply, with some exceptions.

### USA

India is not on the US visa suspension lists under Presidential Proclamation 10998. That proclamation has been in force since 1 January 2026.

## How do UK universities treat Indian degrees and Class 12 English?

Each university sets its own rules. Three examples, from each university's own pages:

- **University of Manchester:** for a taught master's, it considers a three-year degree with First Class from a reputable university. The usual minimum is 60% to 80%, depending on your university. It may accept Standard XII English, depending on the board and your overall score.
- **University of Birmingham:** it considers three-year and four-year degrees. A three-year degree needs first class, though its Business School also considers a very good upper second. For a 2:1, it normally expects 55% to 60% or more.
- **Cardiff Metropolitan University:** for undergraduate entry, it accepts 60% in the English part of CBSE or ISC Standard XII in place of IELTS. Some postgraduate courses ask for 80%.

Manchester does not accept a letter saying your university taught in English. To choose a test, read [which English test you need](/blog/english-tests-for-study-abroad/).

## Will your foreign degree be recognised in India?

The University Grants Commission (UGC) grants equivalence to foreign qualifications under regulations dated 4 April 2025. You apply on the [UGC equivalence portal](https://equivalence.ugc.ac.in). The rules give the UGC 15 working days to decide.

The main conditions are:

- the institution is recognised under the laws of its home country
- your entry requirements and course length are similar to a comparable Indian programme (up to 10% variation in credits is allowed)
- qualifications from a franchise campus or institution are not recognised
- an off-shore campus counts only if its host and home countries both approve it
- medicine, pharmacy, nursing, law, architecture and other council-regulated subjects are outside these rules

The Association of Indian Universities (AIU) still lists its own equivalence certificate, through its [evaluation portal](https://evaluation.aiu.ac.in/). Ask the body in India that needs your degree which one it wants.

Check all this before you pay a deposit, especially for a [branch campus in Dubai](/blog/study-in/dubai/).

## How do you send money for fees from India?

Under the RBI's Liberalised Remittance Scheme (LRS), you can send up to US$250,000 per financial year, as of October 2026. Studies abroad are an allowed purpose. You must give your PAN, and you declare the purpose on Form A2.

The Income Tax Department says tax collected at source (TCS) applies to LRS remittances above ₹10 lakh in a financial year. It lists an exception for education paid from a loan under section 80E.

In the Budget speech for 2026-27, on 1 February 2026, the Finance Minister proposed cutting TCS on LRS remittances for education from 5% to 2%. This is for amounts above ₹10 lakh. Under the Finance Bill 2026, the change would apply from 1 April 2026. Ask your bank which rate it applies when you pay.

## Which scholarships can Indian students apply for?

The National Overseas Scholarship (NOS), run by the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment, funds master's and PhD study. Its 2025-26 guidelines say:

- it is for Scheduled Castes, denotified, nomadic and semi-nomadic tribes, landless agricultural labourers and traditional artisans
- there are 125 awards a year, and 30% are for women
- family income must be ₹8 lakh a year or less
- you must be 35 or under on 1 April of the selection year
- you need an unconditional offer from a top 500 QS-ranked university
- it pays tuition plus £9,900 a year for living costs in the UK, or US$15,400 in other countries

Other schemes open to Indian citizens:

- **Chevening:** India has its own Chevening page. The 2027-2028 round is closed.
- **Commonwealth Scholarships:** India is eligible. Master's Scholarships go through the Ministry of Education, and the 2027/28 round has closed. Shared Scholarships open on 10 November 2026 and close at 16:00 GMT on 8 December 2026.
- **Fulbright-Nehru:** as of October 2026, USIEF lists awards for doctoral and postdoctoral research and for academics and professionals. It lists no master's fellowship.
- **DAAD:** DAAD has a Regional Office in New Delhi, and its India site helps you find scholarships.

India is not on the Australia Awards list. For how each scheme works, read [government scholarships for international students](/blog/government-scholarships-for-international-students/).

## Which documents need extra steps in India?

- **Apostille:** India has been a party to the Apostille Convention since 14 July 2005. The Ministry of External Affairs (MEA) charges ₹50 for each apostille.
- **e-Sanad:** this MEA service handles documents held in a digital depository. The MEA acts only after the board or university that issued the document verifies it. For CBSE documents from 2014 onwards, paper copies are not accepted.
- **Police clearance:** apply for a certificate through Passport Seva. Since 28 September 2022, Post Office Passport Seva Kendras offer it too.

## What to do next

1. For the UK, book your TB test at an approved clinic once your course is confirmed. The certificate lasts 6 months.
2. For Germany, start your APS now, and check whether your field needs the dMAT.
3. Check how each university on your shortlist treats your degree and your Class 12 English.
4. Before you pay a deposit, check the course against the UGC rules, and plan your LRS payments with your bank.
