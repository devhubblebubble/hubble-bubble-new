Studying in the UK means paying tuition, a visa fee, a health surcharge and your living costs. As of October 2026, 2027 entry fees for one-year master's courses at four example universities run from £28,275 to £48,600. The Student visa costs £558 and the health surcharge for a 12-month course is £1,164. Two example universities put living costs at about £1,100 to £1,700 a month.

## What do you have to pay for?

You pay some costs to your university and some to the UK government. These are the main ones:

- Tuition fees, paid to the university. Each example university below asks for a deposit first.
- The Student visa application fee, paid to the UK government.
- The Immigration Health Surcharge, paid as part of your visa application.
- Living costs: rent, food, travel, phone and bills.
- One-off costs such as flights, and a tuberculosis test if you need one.

For the visa, you also have to show a set amount of money, usually in a bank account. That is a rule about savings, not an extra bill. It is explained further down.

## How much are tuition fees for international students?

Each university sets its own fees, and the fee changes with the course. The table uses four universities as examples, with fees from each university's own course page for September 2027 entry. Every course is a full-time master's lasting one year.

| University | Where | Course | International fee | Deposit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| University of Glasgow | Scotland | [MSc International Relations](https://www.gla.ac.uk/postgraduate/taught/internationalrelations/) | £28,275 | £2,000 |
| University of Glasgow | Scotland | [MSc Data Science & Artificial Intelligence](https://www.gla.ac.uk/postgraduate/taught/datascience/) | £34,815 | £2,000 |
| Cardiff University | Wales | [MSc Data Science and Analytics](https://www.cardiff.ac.uk/study/postgraduate/taught/courses/course/data-science-and-analytics-msc-full-time-september-start) | £32,450 | £3,000 |
| University of Manchester | England | [MSc Advanced Computer Science](https://www.manchester.ac.uk/study/masters/courses/list/21573/msc-advanced-computer-science/) | £41,400 | £2,500 |
| UCL | London | [MSc Data Science](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/prospective-students/graduate/taught-degrees/data-science-msc) | £48,600 | 10% of the fee |

Two things stand out. At Glasgow, the two courses differ by £6,540. And the UCL course in London costs £13,785 more than Glasgow's course in a similar subject.

These are only examples, as of October 2026. Check the fee on your own course page, and make sure it is for the year you start.

## How much is the UK Student visa fee?

The Student visa costs £558 if you apply from outside the UK, as of October 2026. If anyone joins you on your visa, you pay the fee for each of them too. gov.uk says a decision usually takes 3 weeks when you apply from outside the UK.

If you hope to bring family, read [Can you bring family on a UK Student visa?](/blog/uk-student-visa-dependants/) first. For the full application, read [UK Student visa 2026: cost, money and timeline](/blog/uk-student-visa-guide/).

## How much is the Immigration Health Surcharge?

The Immigration Health Surcharge is the payment for UK healthcare that you make as part of your visa application. Students pay £776 a year, as of October 2026.

The surcharge follows the length of your visa, not your course. A Student visa lasts longer than the course itself. For a course of 12 months or longer, the visa can start up to 1 month before the course and ends 4 months after it.

gov.uk works out the charge like this:

- 6 months or less, applying from outside the UK: nothing to pay.
- More than 6 months but less than 1 year: a full year, £776.
- More than 1 year and up to 18 months: a year and a half, £1,164.
- More than 18 months but less than 2 years: two full years, £1,552.

So a 12-month master's gives a visa of up to 17 months. That is in the 18 months or less band, so you pay £1,164.

## How much money must you show for the visa?

You must show you can pay your course fees and support yourself. UK Visas and Immigration checks this as a legal minimum. It is not a full budget.

You need two amounts added together:

- The course fees you still owe for your first academic year (up to 9 months). The amount is on your Confirmation of Acceptance for Studies (CAS).
- A set amount for living costs for each month of your course, up to 9 months.

| Monthly living cost amount | Until 29 November 2026 | From 30 November 2026 |
|---|---|---|
| London, per month | £1,529 | £1,570 |
| London, 9 months | £13,761 | £14,130 |
| Outside London, per month | £1,171 | £1,203 |
| Outside London, 9 months | £10,539 | £10,827 |

London means the City of London and the 32 London boroughs. The new amounts come from Statement of Changes HC 584. Applications made before 30 November 2026 are decided under the rules in force on 29 November 2026.

The money must stay in an account for 28 days in a row. For whose account you can use, how recent the statement must be and other rules, read [Bank statements for a UK Student visa](/blog/uk-student-visa-bank-statements/). If you plan to borrow, read [Can you use an education loan for a UK Student visa?](/blog/education-loan-uk-student-visa/).

## Do you have to pay a deposit?

All four example universities ask international students for a tuition deposit:

- University of Glasgow: £2,000 to secure your place.
- Cardiff University: £3,000.
- University of Manchester: £2,500 towards your tuition fees, paid before it issues a CAS, for self-funded international applicants.
- UCL: 10% of the first-year fee, which is £4,860 on the MSc Data Science.

Plan for the deposit early. At Manchester, for example, the CAS comes after the deposit, and you need a CAS to apply for your visa.

Course fees you have already paid reduce the fees you must show for the visa. The payment must be on your CAS, or you need a receipt from the university.

An accommodation deposit can reduce the visa amount too. If you pay it to your university for accommodation the university arranges, up to £1,529 can be taken off. From 30 November 2026 this limit becomes £1,570.

## How much are living costs in the UK?

Universities publish their own estimates. Here are two examples:

- The University of Glasgow suggests £1,100 to £1,700 a month to live and study in Glasgow. It published this in August 2026, based on the UCAS Budget Calculator.
- The University of Manchester's guide for a full-time postgraduate student is £18,188 for 52 weeks, about £1,510 a month. It uses approximate 2026/27 prices, and £10,000 of it is accommodation.

Manchester says its figures are a guide only, and Glasgow says your costs could be more or less. Your own costs depend on your rent, your city and how you live.

Notice the gap with the visa amount. The visa asks for £1,171 a month outside London for 9 months. A one-year master's needs 12 months of living costs, and Manchester's own guide is £1,510 a month. Budget with your university's figure, and treat the visa figure as the minimum you must show.

## What does a one-year master's outside London cost in total?

Here is one worked example. It uses the University of Manchester's MSc Advanced Computer Science, starting in September 2027. Every figure comes from the sources listed for this page. Your course and costs will be different.

| Cost (example) | Amount | Paid to |
|---|---|---|
| Tuition fee, 2027 entry | £41,400 | University |
| Student visa application | £558 | UK government |
| Health surcharge, visa of up to 17 months | £1,164 | UK government |
| Living costs, 52 weeks (university guide, 2026/27 prices) | £18,188 | Your own spending |
| Total for the year | £61,310 | |

The total leaves out flights and a tuberculosis test if you need one. Living costs in 2027/28 may also be higher than the 2026/27 guide.

The money you show for the visa is a different number. Any application made on or after 30 November 2026 uses the new rate of £1,203 a month outside London. Say you apply in 2027 and have paid the £2,500 deposit:

- Fees still owed: £41,400 minus £2,500, which is £38,900.
- Living costs: 9 months at £1,203, which is £10,827.
- Total to hold for 28 days: £49,727.

That is £10,827 for living costs on paper, against £18,188 in the university's own guide.

## What are the common mistakes when planning UK costs?

- Using the visa amount as your budget. It covers 9 months at a set rate, not your real costs for 12 months.
- Forgetting that the health surcharge follows your visa length, not your course length.
- Leaving the deposit late. At some universities, such as Manchester, there is no CAS until it is paid.
- Comparing fees from different years. Check that each fee page is for the year you start.
- Saving to the old monthly amounts for an application made on or after 30 November 2026.
