As of October 2026, UK students apply for undergraduate courses through UCAS, and the fee for 2027 entry is £34.50. In 2026/27, universities in England can charge up to £9,790 a year, and SAAS can pay eligible Scottish students' fees in Scotland. British citizens can live and study in Ireland under the Common Travel Area. Germany, the Netherlands, the USA, Canada and Australia need a student visa or permit.

## Your options

You can study in your own nation, elsewhere in the UK or abroad. The Study in guides cover [the UK](/blog/study-in/uk/), [Ireland](/blog/study-in/ireland/), [Europe](/blog/study-in/europe/), [the USA](/blog/study-in/usa/), [Canada](/blog/study-in/canada/), [Australia](/blog/study-in/australia/) and [New Zealand](/blog/study-in/new-zealand/). Fees and funding differ between England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland, so check the rules for the nation where you live.

## How do you apply to a UK university?

For undergraduate courses, you apply through UCAS. For 2027 entry, as of October 2026:

- The fee is £34.50 for up to five choices. If you got UK government-funded free school meals at secondary school, or you are a care leaver, you may not have to pay it.
- The deadline for Oxford, Cambridge and most medicine, dentistry and veterinary courses was 18:00 UK time on 15 October 2026.
- For most other courses, it is 18:00 UK time on 13 January 2027.
- Applications that arrive after 30 June 2027 go into Clearing.

For a master's, you apply directly to each university. [How to apply to a UK university without an agent](/blog/apply-to-uk-university-without-an-agent/) walks through the UCAS steps.

## How does fee status work?

Your fees and funding depend on where your home is, which nation you study in and how long you have lived in the UK. In England, for example, you can get a Tuition Fee Loan and a Maintenance Loan if your home is in England and you have lived in the UK, the Channel Islands or the Isle of Man for 3 years before your course starts.

Each nation has its own funding body: Student Finance England, SAAS in Scotland, Student Finance Wales and Student Finance NI. You apply to the body for the nation you live in, even if you study in another one.

Scotland shows how much this matters. At the University of Glasgow, the fee for Scottish students is £1,820 a year, which SAAS can pay in full if you meet its residence conditions. Students from England, Wales and Northern Ireland pay £9,790 a year in 2026/27, and Glasgow says that fee rises in each year of the course.

## How much can you get for tuition in 2026/27?

| Your home | Tuition support in 2026/27, as of October 2026 |
|---|---|
| England | Tuition Fee Loan up to £9,790, the most universities in England can charge |
| Scotland | Fees paid by SAAS at Scottish universities, if you meet its residence conditions |
| Wales | Tuition Fee Loan up to £9,790, depending on your course fee |
| Northern Ireland | Tuition Fee Loan up to £4,985 in Northern Ireland, or £9,790 in England, Scotland or Wales |

For courses that start from January 2027, student finance loans in England move to the Lifelong Learning Entitlement (LLE). You can apply from late October 2026. New learners can borrow up to £39,160 for tuition, which gov.uk says equals 4 years at the 2026/27 maximum fee.

## What can you get for living costs?

For 2026/27, the most you can get for a full year is:

- **England:** a Maintenance Loan of up to £10,830 if you live away from home outside London, £14,135 in London, or £9,118 with your parents.
- **Wales:** £12,590 in grant and loan together if you live away from home outside London, or £10,685 at home. The lower your household income, the bigger the grant part.
- **Northern Ireland:** a Maintenance Loan of up to £8,352 away from home outside London, £11,699 in London or £6,471 with your parents, plus a grant of up to £3,569 depending on household income.
- **Scotland:** SAAS sets its own bursaries and loans, so check the current amounts with SAAS.

## Can you study in Ireland as a UK student?

Yes. Under the Common Travel Area, British citizens can move freely to Ireland and live there, gov.uk says. You can work without needing any permission, and you can access all levels of education on terms no less favourable than Irish citizens.

Tuition can be free under Ireland's Free Fees Initiative. You may qualify if:

- you are a UK, EEA or Swiss citizen
- you have lived in the UK, the EEA or Switzerland for at least 3 of the 5 years before your course
- it is your first full-time undergraduate course, and it lasts at least 2 years
- you study at a university or other publicly funded college, not a private college

You still pay the student contribution. For 2026/27 it is €2,350, after Ireland's Budget 2027 cut it by €150. Students from Northern Ireland can apply for a Student Contribution Loan to cover it.

For undergraduate courses in Ireland, you apply through the CAO. The 2027 CAO application opens on 5 November 2026 at 12:00. Read more in [studying in Ireland](/blog/study-in/ireland/).

## What changed for studying in the EU after Brexit?

gov.uk's country guides set out the rules for UK nationals. To study in Germany, you must apply for a student visa or residence permit. In the Netherlands, you must meet all visa and residency requirements before you travel. If you have lived in either country legally since before 1 January 2021, you may have rights under the Withdrawal Agreement.

Fees depend on the country. In the Netherlands, the lower statutory fee of €2,694 for 2026/27 is for students from the EU, the EEA, Switzerland and Surinam. The UK is not on that list. Study in NL puts average institutional fees at €9,000 to €20,000 a year for a bachelor's and €12,000 to €30,000 for a master's.

For healthcare in the EEA or Switzerland, you can apply for a UK Student GHIC with a letter from your university. The NHS says a GHIC does not replace travel insurance. See [studying in Europe](/blog/study-in/europe/).

### Is the UK rejoining Erasmus+?

Yes. On 15 April 2026, the UK and the EU signed the legal text to bring the UK into Erasmus+ in 2027, gov.uk says. The British Council is set to become the UK's National Agency. The next funding round is expected to open in November 2026 and close in February 2027. Students cannot apply directly for the funding, so ask your university whether it will take part.

### What about the Turing Scheme?

In 2026/27, the Turing Scheme funds study, work experience or volunteering abroad for 14 days to 12 months. Your UK university or college applies for it, not you. Grants for living costs run from £12 to £23 a day, depending on the destination, the length and your background. gov.uk says the scheme will not operate in the 2027/28 academic year.

If you spend a year of your UK course abroad, students from England can get a Maintenance Loan of up to £12,403 for 2026/27. Student Finance NI's overseas loan rate is up to £9,956.

## Can you study in the USA as a UK student?

Yes. For a university degree, you need an F-1 student visa. You first apply to a school approved by the Student and Exchange Visitor Program (SEVP), which issues your Form I-20. Then:

1. Pay the $350 SEVIS fee.
2. Fill in the online visa form and pay the $185 application fee.
3. Book an interview at the US Embassy or Consulate in your country of residence.
4. Set your social media accounts to public, as the US Embassy in the UK asks of F visa applicants.

You can get the visa up to 365 days before your course starts, but you cannot enter the USA more than 30 days before it.

For funding, the US-UK Fulbright Commission offers postgraduate awards to UK citizens with at least a 2:1. Dual US-UK citizens and Green Card holders cannot apply. The 2027/28 round opened in February 2026 and closed on 5 May 2026. See [studying in the USA](/blog/study-in/usa/).

## What about Australia, Canada and New Zealand?

Australia and Canada both need a student visa or permit.

- **Australia:** you apply for a Student visa (subclass 500) once your provider gives you a Confirmation of Enrolment (CoE). Study Australia says the application rules changed on 2 October 2026, so check the current rules. See [studying in Australia](/blog/study-in/australia/).
- **Canada:** most foreign nationals need a study permit, which costs CAD 150. If you fly in as a British citizen, you need an eTA, not a visitor visa. Canada issues it automatically when it approves your first study permit. See [studying in Canada](/blog/study-in/canada/).

For New Zealand, read [studying in New Zealand](/blog/study-in/new-zealand/).

## Do you need your documents legalised?

If a university or visa office abroad asks for an apostille, the UK's Legalisation Office issues it. It is a stamped official certificate, attached after the office checks a document's signature, stamp or seal. As of October 2026, it costs £45 per document on paper, or £35 as an e-Apostille. A qualification certificate must first be certified by a UK public official, such as a notary or solicitor. Paper applications usually take up to 25 working days, plus postage.

## What to do next

1. Check your funding body's 2026/27 rules: Student Finance England, SAAS, Student Finance Wales or Student Finance NI.
2. If you apply through UCAS, note 13 January 2027 for most courses.
3. If you want to study abroad, check the visa rules and fees for that country, and ask your university about Erasmus+ from 2027.
4. For the USA, plan for the next Fulbright round early.
