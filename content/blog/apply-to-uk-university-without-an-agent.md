You can apply to a UK university yourself. For undergraduate study, apply through UCAS. As of October 2026, the fee for 2027 entry is £34.50 for up to five choices. The deadline for most courses is 18:00 UK time on 13 January 2027. For a master's, apply on each university's own website. Some charge nothing to apply, and others charge a fee.

## Can you apply without an agent?

Yes. Agents are optional. The British Council says agents cannot give you favourable entry requirements or other application options. They also cannot give you information that differs from what a student applying directly receives.

Applying yourself takes more reading and planning. In return, you control every choice, every login and every document. If you are weighing up an agent, read [how study abroad agents get paid](/blog/how-study-abroad-agents-get-paid/) first.

## How do you apply for an undergraduate degree through UCAS?

UCAS is the UK's application service for undergraduate courses. Here are the steps for 2027 entry.

1. Register in the UCAS Hub. Applications for 2027 entry opened on 12 May 2026, and you can send a completed application from 1 September 2026.
2. Fill in every section, from personal details and nationality to education and employment. You cannot skip mandatory questions.
3. Enter your qualifications using the actual name and grade on your certificate. UCAS says not to use a UK equivalent.
4. Add up to five choices. They can be the same or different courses, at the same or different universities.
5. Write your personal statement. The format is below.
6. Get a reference from someone who is not a family member, friend, partner or ex-partner. If you apply on your own, UCAS says a school, college or UCAS registered centre can supply it.
7. Pay the fee and send your application. Once every section is complete and you have a reference, you pay £34.50 and send it to UCAS.
8. Send proof of your results if asked. For most international qualifications, you send certificates or transcripts to the university yourself.

## What are the UCAS deadlines for 2027 entry?

All UCAS deadlines are at 18:00 UK time.

| Deadline | What it is for |
|---|---|
| 15 October 2026 (passed) | Oxford, Cambridge, and most medicine, dentistry and veterinary courses |
| 13 January 2027 | Equal consideration for most undergraduate courses |
| 30 June 2027 | Last date before applications go into Clearing |

Universities must consider all applications received by 13 January equally. After that date, they can still consider you if they have places left.

UCAS also says many universities and colleges keep accepting applications from international students until later in the year, nearer the start of the course. Check each course page.

Applications that reach UCAS after 18:00 on 30 June 2027 go into Clearing automatically.

## What is the new UCAS personal statement?

For 2026 entry onwards, the UCAS personal statement is three questions:

1. Why do you want to study this course or subject?
2. How have your qualifications and studies helped you to prepare for this course or subject?
3. What else have you done to prepare outside of education, and why are these experiences useful?

The overall limit is still 4,000 characters, including spaces. Each answer needs at least 350 characters.

Write it yourself, in your own words, and keep every claim true. For a master's statement, read [how to write a personal statement for a master's](/blog/masters-personal-statement/).

## How do you apply for a master's without an agent?

For a master's, you usually apply on each university's own website. Each university has its own online form, so keep a list of your logins.

Application fees vary. For example, Cardiff University says it does not charge an application fee for any of its postgraduate programmes. The University of Oxford charges £75 for taught courses, and says the fee cannot be refunded. These are two examples, as of October 2026.

As one example of the documents you need, Cardiff University asks for:

- copies of your degree certificate and academic transcripts, or a partial transcript if you are still studying
- the contact details of a referee
- evidence of your English language level, if English is not your first language
- a copy of your passport, if you need a visa to study

Some universities also ask for a personal statement. The University of Aberdeen, for example, suggests 250 to 500 words answering three set questions.

For English tests, see [which English test do you need](/blog/english-tests-for-study-abroad/). For the full process, see [how to apply for a master's abroad](/blog/how-to-apply-for-a-masters-abroad/).

## What happens after you get an offer?

The steps are the same with or without an agent.

1. Read the offer and any conditions. See [conditional or unconditional offer](/blog/conditional-vs-unconditional-offer/).
2. If your offer asks for a deposit, pay it directly to the university.
3. Get your CAS (Confirmation of Acceptance for Studies). The university issues it after it makes you an unconditional offer. A CAS is valid for 6 months from the date it is assigned. See [what is a CAS](/blog/what-is-a-cas/).
4. Apply for your Student visa. You need the CAS before you start. The earliest you can apply is 6 months before your course starts.
5. Pay the visa costs. As of October 2026, the fee is £558 from outside the UK, plus the healthcare surcharge of £776 a year.
6. Wait for the decision. From outside the UK, you usually get a decision within 3 weeks.

You also need enough money to support yourself and pay for your course. Read [bank statements for a UK Student visa](/blog/uk-student-visa-bank-statements/) and the [UK Student visa guide](/blog/uk-student-visa-guide/) for the amounts and the evidence.

Check that every detail on your visa form matches your CAS. UKCISA warns that your application may be refused if the two do not match.

## What mistakes should you avoid?

- Converting your grades into UK equivalents. UCAS asks for the actual name and grade on your certificate.
- Leaving your reference until the end. UCAS suggests asking early, because referees can take time.
- Missing 13 January 2027 when your course uses it as the equal consideration date.
- Paying tuition to anyone other than the university.
- Letting someone else hold your logins. Use your own email address and keep your passwords.

4. When you get an unconditional offer, read [what is a CAS](/blog/what-is-a-cas/) and plan your visa.
