As of October 2026, the US has suspended the entry of Nigerian nationals on F, M and J visas. This rule has applied since 1 January 2026 (State Department notice of 2 February 2026). The UK, Canada, Ireland and Germany remain open, with extra steps such as a UK TB test. Some UK universities accept C6 in WAEC English, and some ask HND holders for a Postgraduate Diploma before a master's.

## Where can Nigerian students study abroad?

Your options include the [UK](/blog/study-in/uk/), [Canada](/blog/study-in/canada/), [Ireland](/blog/study-in/ireland/), [Germany and the rest of Europe](/blog/study-in/europe/), [Australia](/blog/study-in/australia/) and [Malaysia](/blog/study-in/malaysia/). The [USA](/blog/study-in/usa/) has an entry suspension for Nigerian students, set out below.

This guide covers only what is different for Nigerian citizens. For the general steps, read [How to apply for a master's abroad, step by step](/blog/how-to-apply-for-a-masters-abroad/).

## What visa rules apply to Nigerian citizens?

Most rules below depend on your passport. The UK TB test and Canada's medical exam depend on where you have lived. So they can apply to anyone living in Nigeria, and may not apply to a Nigerian who lives elsewhere.

### UK: TB test, money and English

Nigeria is on the UK's TB test list. You need a test if all of these are true:

- you are coming to the UK for 6 months or more
- you have lived in a listed country for 6 months or more
- you were living there, or in another listed country, within the last 6 months

The certificate is valid for 6 months from the date of your x-ray.

You must use a clinic approved by the Home Office. As of October 2026, the gov.uk list for Nigeria (last updated 13 May 2026) shows these:

| Clinic | Where | Fee, age 11 and over |
|---|---|---|
| IOM Migration Health Assessment Centre | Abuja, Benin City and Lagos (Ikeja) | US$94, paid in naira |
| Q-Life Family Clinic | Victoria Island, Lagos | ₦120,000 |
| St. Nicholas Hospital | Lagos Island | ₦120,000 |
| The Consultants Practice | Ikoyi, Lagos | ₦120,000 |

Pay by bank transfer, with your passport number as the reference. Cash is not accepted. Appointments can take up to 10 days in the June to September student visa peak.

Nigeria is not on the list of nationalities that may skip sending proof of money, so send your financial evidence with your application. See [Bank statements for a UK Student visa](/blog/uk-student-visa-bank-statements/).

Nigeria is also not on the list of countries whose nationals are exempt from proving English. For degree-level study, your university can assess your English itself, at a level equivalent to CEFR B2.

You apply online first, then book an appointment at a visa application centre. The [gov.uk list of centres](https://www.gov.uk/find-a-visa-application-centre) links to VFS Global's booking page for Nigeria. For costs and timings, read [UK Student visa 2026: cost, money and timeline](/blog/uk-student-visa-guide/).

### USA: entry suspension and visa terms

Presidential Proclamation 10998 took effect at 12:01 a.m. EST on 1 January 2026. It suspends the entry of nationals of Nigeria as immigrants, and "as nonimmigrants on B-1, B-2, B-1/B-2, F, M, and J visas". F and M are student visas, and J is the exchange visitor visa.

The State Department's notice of 2 February 2026 says applicants "may still submit visa applications and schedule interviews, but they may be ineligible for visa issuance or admission to the United States." It also says visas issued before 1 January 2026 have not been and will not be revoked under the proclamation. Exceptions include dual nationals who apply with the passport of a country that is not listed.

For visas that are issued, the reciprocity table for Nigeria lists F-1 and J-1 visas as one entry, valid for 3 months, with no reciprocity fee.

### Canada: visitor visa and medical exam

Nigeria is on Canada's list of visa-required countries, so you need a visitor visa to travel there, as well as your study permit.

For a stay of more than 6 months, you may need a medical exam. You need one if you lived in or travelled to a listed country for 6 months or more in a row in the year before. Nigeria is on that list. See [Studying in Canada](/blog/study-in/canada/) for the study permit steps.

### Ireland and Germany

Ireland lists Nigeria as a visa-required nationality (list updated 12 May 2025). See [Studying in Ireland](/blog/study-in/ireland/).

Germany also needs a visa from Nigerian citizens. For study, the German Consulate works with the service provider VFS to take in applications. Its checklist asks for your WAEC or National Diploma results and all originals in date order. If you were born on or after 15 December 1992, it asks for an NPC birth certificate. See [Studying in Europe](/blog/study-in/europe/).

## Do universities abroad accept WAEC, NECO and HND?

Each university sets its own rules. These three examples come from each university's own Nigeria page.

- **University of Sussex (UK).** For a bachelor's, it recommends a foundation year after the SSCE (WAEC or NECO). Year 1 entry needs good SSCE grades plus a year at a recognised Nigerian university, or a foundation year. An HND can lead to Year 1, or to Year 2 with two years of university study. For a master's, it asks for at least a second-class lower (a CGPA of 3.0 to 3.49 out of 5). C6 or above in WAEC or NECO English meets its English requirement.
- **University of Westminster (UK).** For a master's, it asks for a Nigerian bachelor's with a CGPA of at least 2.5 out of 5, or 3.5 for courses that ask for a UK 2:1. HND holders need "HND plus a Postgraduate Diploma with high grades". For undergraduate courses, it accepts WAEC English at C6 if the result is under 5 years old and verified on the WAEC Digital Certificate Portal.
- **University of Toronto (Canada).** Its graduate school lists Nigeria among the countries whose degrees normally exempt you from an English test. Your university must be recognised, and all teaching and exams must have been in English. Some applicants may still need to prove their English.

At these examples, WAEC or NECO alone does not lead straight into Year 1 of a UK bachelor's. For test options, read [Which English test do you need?](/blog/english-tests-for-study-abroad/)

## Will your foreign degree be recognised in Nigeria?

The Federal Ministry of Education evaluates foreign academic credentials through its Evaluation and Accreditation System (ESS) portal. You register, pay a non-refundable fee through Remita, upload scans of your certificates and book an appointment. For physical verification, you take one original and two photocopies to the Accreditation Venue at the Federal Secretariat Complex in Abuja.

The National Universities Commission (NUC) publishes the guidelines for transnational education (TNE), where a foreign university teaches in Nigeria. It lists these institutions on its website, with the model each one uses, such as a branch campus.

Before you pay a deposit, check two things:

1. If a foreign university will teach you in Nigeria, check that it is on the NUC's TNE list.
2. For a branch campus abroad or an online course, ask the Ministry first whether the ESS will evaluate it.

For campuses abroad, see [UK and Australian university campuses in Dubai](/blog/study-in/dubai/).

## How do you pay fees and find scholarships?

Your bank applies the Central Bank of Nigeria's foreign exchange rules to school fees, so ask it which documents it needs before you pay a deposit.

These schemes are open to Nigerian citizens:

| Scheme | What it is for | Status, October 2026 |
|---|---|---|
| Chevening | UK master's | 2027-2028 round closed |
| Commonwealth Master's | UK master's, through the Federal Scholarship Board, Abuja | Closed 20 October 2026 |
| Commonwealth Shared | UK master's, through a UK university | Opens 10 November, closes 8 December 2026 |
| GREAT Scholarships | £10,000 off UK tuition | 2026-27 list shows 14 universities |
| PTDF Overseas Scholarship | MSc or PhD at partner universities in the UK, Germany, France or Malaysia | 2026 round closed 27 February 2026 |
| Fulbright Foreign Student | Doctoral research in the US | Page shows 2026/2027 |
| Australia Awards | Study in Australia | Nigeria takes part |

For a PTDF MSc, you need at least a 2:1, or a 2:2 with relevant industry experience. You must have completed NYSC and have five O'Level credits, including English and Mathematics. It pays return flights, health insurance, tuition and bench fees, and allowances for accommodation and living costs. Your course must be relevant to PTDF's mandate.

For Commonwealth Scholarships, you must live permanently in Nigeria or another eligible Commonwealth country, and hold at least a 2:1, or a 2:2 plus a relevant postgraduate qualification. You must also be unable to afford UK study without the award. Shared Scholarship applicants must not have studied or worked for one academic year or more in a high-income country.

The US Embassy's Fulbright programme is for doctoral students at least two years into their programme. Master's applicants cannot apply, and the US suspension above also covers J visas.

For more schemes, read [Government scholarships for international students](/blog/government-scholarships-for-international-students/).

## Which documents need extra steps?

- WAEC lists a Digital Certificate service and online results verification on its website. Some universities, such as Westminster, ask for verified results.
- Nigeria is not a party to the Apostille Convention, so Nigerian documents cannot get an apostille. If a university or embassy asks for legalised copies, ask which route it accepts.
- German missions stopped legalising Nigerian documents in May 2000. They use a verification process instead, which only a German authority, such as a court or registry office, can request.

## What to do next

1. Choose your country first. If it is the US, read the State Department notice before you pay any fee.
2. If you live in Nigeria, book your TB test early, especially for a June to September application.
3. Read the Nigeria page of each university on your list for its WAEC, HND and CGPA rules.
4. If you are eligible, apply for a Commonwealth Shared Scholarship between 10 November and 8 December 2026.
