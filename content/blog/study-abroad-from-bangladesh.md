As of October 2026, if you live in Bangladesh, a UK Student visa needs a TB test. Approved clinics are in Dhaka, Sylhet and Chattogram. You must also send proof of money. Manchester and Birmingham, for example, do not accept the HSC for direct entry to a bachelor's. Back home, UGC equivalence for a foreign degree takes at least 90 to 120 working days.

## What are your options?

You can study in the [UK](/blog/study-in/uk/), [Ireland](/blog/study-in/ireland/), the [USA](/blog/study-in/usa/), [Canada](/blog/study-in/canada/), [Germany and the rest of Europe](/blog/study-in/europe/), [Australia](/blog/study-in/australia/), [New Zealand](/blog/study-in/new-zealand/), [Singapore](/blog/study-in/singapore/), [Malaysia](/blog/study-in/malaysia/) or [Dubai](/blog/study-in/dubai/). Each link leads to that country's main guide.

This guide covers only what is different for Bangladeshi citizens and for people who live in Bangladesh. To compare countries, read [which country should you study in](/blog/which-country-should-you-study-in/).

## What is different for Bangladeshis on a UK Student visa?

### Do you need a TB test?

You need one if all of these are true:

- you are coming to the UK for 6 months or more
- you have lived in Bangladesh for 6 months or more
- you lived there within the last 6 months

The rule follows where you have lived, not your passport. If you live in another country, check it on the [gov.uk list](https://www.gov.uk/tb-test-visa/countries-where-you-need-a-tb-test-to-enter-the-uk).

You must use a clinic approved by the Home Office. The gov.uk list for Bangladesh, updated on 12 March 2025, includes these:

| Clinic | City | Fee, age 11 and over |
|---|---|---|
| Green Crescent Health Services | Dhaka | Tk 5,500 |
| IOM Migration Health Assessment Clinic | Dhaka | US$70 |
| Green Crescent MediLab | Sylhet | Tk 5,500 |
| IOM Migration Health Assessment Clinic | Sylhet | US$70 |
| Green Crescent Medical Diagnostic | Chattogram | Tk 5,500 |

For children under 11, Green Crescent charges Tk 3,500 and IOM charges US$30. At IOM you pay in taka, at the IOM exchange rate, and follow-up tests cost nothing extra. Green Crescent charges Tk 14,000 for further investigation.

Green Crescent's Dhaka and Sylhet clinics say you do not need an appointment. Bring photo ID, a photocopy of it for Green Crescent, and any earlier chest x-ray or TB reports. The certificate is valid for 6 months from your x-ray.

### Do you have to send bank statements?

Yes. Paragraph ST 22.1 of Appendix Student lists the nationalities that can skip sending proof of money. As of October 2026, Bangladesh is not on it.

So send your financial evidence with your application. [Bank statements for a UK Student visa](/blog/uk-student-visa-bank-statements/) explains the rules.

### Where do you give your fingerprints?

UK visa application centres in Bangladesh are run by VFS Global. The gov.uk [list of visa application centres](https://www.gov.uk/find-a-visa-application-centre) links to VFS for addresses and opening times. For fees and timings, see the [UK Student visa guide](/blog/uk-student-visa-guide/).

## What about Ireland and the USA?

- **Ireland:** Bangladeshi citizens need a visa. Ireland's list of visa-required nationalities, updated on 12 May 2025, includes Bangladesh. See [studying in Ireland](/blog/study-in/ireland/).
- **USA:** Bangladesh is not on the US visa suspension lists under Presidential Proclamation 10998, in force since 1 January 2026. See [studying in the USA](/blog/study-in/usa/).

## How do UK universities treat HSC results and Bangladeshi degrees?

Each university sets its own rules. Three examples, from each university's own pages:

- **University of Manchester:** for a taught master's, it asks for at least a four-year bachelor's degree from a reputable Bangladeshi university. The usual minimum is a GPA of 3.2 out of 4. For a bachelor's, it does not take the HSC for direct entry, so you need a recognised foundation programme first. Its integrated foundation year in science and engineering asks for 80% or more in the HSC, with at least 80% in maths and physics.
- **University of Birmingham:** for postgraduate study, it accepts a three-year honours degree followed by a master's degree. It may also consider a bachelor's of at least four years. For a 2:1, it expects a GPA of 3.0 to 3.3 out of 4, or 65% or more, depending on the awarding institution. HSC students need an approved foundation programme before a bachelor's.
- **Cardiff Metropolitan University:** in place of its standard IELTS 6.0 (no part below 5.5), it accepts "HSC (12th) Grade A and above". For undergraduate entry, you also need an English interview. For postgraduate entry, you also need a medium of instruction letter from a listed Bangladeshi institution.

Neither Manchester nor Birmingham lists a three-year pass degree on its own as enough for a master's. Manchester also does not accept a medium of instruction letter as evidence of your English. To choose a test, read [which English test you need](/blog/english-tests-for-study-abroad/).

## Will your foreign degree be recognised in Bangladesh?

The University Grants Commission (UGC) of Bangladesh gives equivalence for foreign degrees. Its notice says it does not handle:

- law, pharmacy, medical or nursing degrees, which go to the Bar Council, the Pharmacy Council, the BMDC or the Nursing Council
- diplomas, ACCA, CA, CMA, CPA, online PhDs, other professional degrees, and degrees of under three years
- degrees from a branch campus or study centre in Bangladesh without proper government approval

There are two stages:

1. Book an appointment through the "Foreign Degree Equivalence" box on the UGC website. Bring all your original documents to be checked in person.
2. After that check, apply through the UGC's online portal.

As of October 2026, the fee is Tk 2,500 per degree or certificate, paid by pay order or bank draft. It takes at least 90 to 120 working days from your final online submission.

Keep your papers from the start. The UGC asks for:

- your admission or enrolment letters
- your passports and visas for the study period
- your original SSC, HSC and degree certificates and transcripts
- your course syllabuses, and any thesis or project paper

## How do you send money for fees from Bangladesh?

Bangladesh Bank sets the rules, and authorised dealer banks follow them:

- Banks release foreign exchange for study abroad under paragraph 10, chapter 11 of Bangladesh Bank's Guidelines for Foreign Exchange Transactions (GFET) 2018. The fees go to your educational institution.
- Since FE Circular No. 23 of 29 May 2017, a bank can pay a payment processing company instead. Your admission documents, such as an I-20 or an acceptance letter, must clearly say fees go through it.
- Since FE Circular No. 29 of 10 August 2017, banks can also pay for health insurance that your visa or admission requires.
- For travel to study abroad, up to US$5,000 a trip can be given in US dollar notes and coins.

Ask your bank which documents it needs before you pay a deposit.

## Which scholarships can Bangladeshi students apply for?

- **Commonwealth Scholarships:** Bangladesh is eligible for both master's schemes. For Commonwealth Master's Scholarships, you apply through the Secondary and Higher Education Division of the Ministry of Education as well as the CSC. The 2027/28 round has closed. Commonwealth Shared Scholarships open on 10 November 2026 and close at 16:00 GMT on 8 December 2026. For Shared Scholarships, you must not have studied or worked for more than one academic year in a high income country.
- **Chevening:** Bangladesh has its own Chevening page. The 2027-2028 round is closed.
- **Fulbright:** Bangladesh is on the Fulbright Foreign Student Program's country list, for graduate study in the USA. Each country's applications are run by a Fulbright commission or the US Embassy. Check with the US Embassy in Dhaka for the current call.
- **Australia Awards:** Bangladesh is a participating country, for study in [Australia](/blog/study-in/australia/).

For how each scheme works, read [government scholarships for international students](/blog/government-scholarships-for-international-students/).

## Which documents need extra steps?

- **Apostille:** Bangladesh joined the Apostille Convention on 29 July 2024, and it came into force for Bangladesh on 30 March 2025. The competent authority is the Director General (Consular and Welfare) at the Ministry of Foreign Affairs in Dhaka. It has an online e-Register.
- **Passports and visas:** keep every passport that holds your student visa. The UGC asks for them when you apply for equivalence.
- **Medium of instruction letter:** Cardiff Met's postgraduate route needs one from a listed institution. Ask your university for it early.

## What to do next

1. Book your UK TB test at an approved clinic in Dhaka, Sylhet or Chattogram once your course is confirmed.
2. If you have a three-year honours degree, check whether each university also wants a master's.
3. Before you choose a branch campus or study centre in Bangladesh, check it has government approval.
4. Ask your bank which documents it needs to pay your fees and any compulsory health insurance.
