A conditional offer means you get the place once you meet some conditions, such as final results, an English score or documents. An unconditional offer means the place is yours if you want it. After either, reply by the deadline and pay any deposit. Then wait for your visa document, such as a CAS in the UK or a CoE in Australia.

## What is the difference between a conditional and an unconditional offer?

UCAS explains it simply. A conditional offer means you still need to meet some conditions, usually exam results. An unconditional offer means the place is yours if you want it.

An unconditional offer can still have non-academic conditions, such as a health check. Read the whole offer either way. It tells you what to send, how and by when.

## What conditions do master's offers have?

It depends on the university and the course. The University of Birmingham says conditions "could be things like your undergraduate degree certificate, your English Language test results or a reference".

The University of Oxford shows other kinds of condition. Final transcripts must come directly from your university. You also complete a Financial Declaration to show how you will pay your fees and living costs.

Conditions can include:

- **Results:** a final grade or degree class, shown on your transcript or certificate.
- **English:** a test score by a set date, unless you are exempt.
- **Documents:** your degree certificate, transcripts or references.
- **Money:** proof of funding, at some universities.

If you are not sure which test will meet an English condition, see [which English test you need](/blog/english-tests-for-study-abroad/).

## What should you do after a conditional offer?

1. Read the offer in full, including the terms and the deadline for meeting conditions.
2. Accept it by the reply date, if it is a course you want.
3. Meet each condition, and send proof the way the university asks.
4. Pay the deposit if one is asked for, after reading the refund terms.
5. Wait for the university to confirm your place, then for your visa document.

Study Australia's advice works for any country. Read your offer in full before accepting it, and do not accept it if you disagree with any of its terms.

## What should you do after an unconditional offer?

1. Accept the offer by the reply date.
2. Pay the deposit, if asked, after reading the refund terms.
3. Ask when your visa document will be issued, and check every detail on it.
4. Apply for the visa once you have that document and the dates allow. See the [UK Student visa guide](/blog/uk-student-visa-guide/).

## Which deadlines should you watch?

Deposit, conditions and visa document dates sit close together. Here are the University of Birmingham's dates for international applicants to most taught courses, as one example:

| Step | January 2027 start | September 2027 start |
|---|---|---|
| Apply by | 30 October 2026 | 2 July 2027 |
| Pay deposit by | 7 December 2026 | 30 July 2027 |
| Meet conditions by | 14 December 2026 | 30 August 2027 |
| CAS issued by | 14 to 18 December 2026 | 3 September 2027 |

Some courses have different dates, so check your own offer. Put every date in your calendar the day the offer arrives.

## Can you get your deposit back?

It depends on the university. Each one sets its own terms, so read them before you pay.

One example: as of October 2026, the University of Birmingham asks for a £3,000 tuition fee deposit for taught master's courses. Its policy says it refunds the deposit if:

- your student visa is refused, unless the application was fraudulent (you must send the refusal document)
- the university cancels your programme
- you cannot travel to the UK for reasons outside your control, with evidence
- your qualifications or English test are not accepted for your offer, with evidence

It does not refund the deposit once you have enrolled. If an offer is withdrawn because of fraudulent documents, no refund request is considered. There is also a deadline for asking for a refund.

You may also have a short right to cancel. The University of Oxford, for example, lets you cancel within 14 calendar days of accepting, without giving a reason. It then refunds any deposit you paid.

## Which document comes after the offer?

Once your place is confirmed, the university gives you the document you need for the visa.

- **UK:** a CAS (Confirmation of Acceptance for Studies). Gov.uk says your education provider sends it once it has offered you a place. Apply for your visa within 6 months of receiving it. See [what is a CAS](/blog/what-is-a-cas/).
- **Australia:** a CoE (Confirmation of Enrolment). Study Australia says it comes after you accept your Letter of Offer and pay your deposit.
- **Canada:** a letter of acceptance. Most students also need a PAL/TAL, which usually means accepting the offer and paying some or all of your tuition first. Master's and doctoral students at public designated learning institutions (DLIs) do not need one from 1 January 2026.
- **Germany:** proof of admission. The German Missions in India list "proof of admittance to the study course".
- **Ireland:** a letter confirming "unconditional placement" on an eligible course. The Embassy of Ireland in New Delhi also asks you to pay fees first. Pay them in full if they are under €6,000, or at least €6,000 if they are more.

## How do firm and insurance choices work on UCAS?

This is for undergraduate applications through UCAS. For most master's courses you apply directly to each university, so UCAS replies do not apply.

- **Firm:** your first choice.
- **Insurance:** your back-up, used only if you miss the conditions of a conditional firm choice. UCAS advises choosing one with lower offer conditions, at a place you would still be happy to go to.
- **Decline:** you must decline all other offers.

You can accept one firm choice and one insurance choice, if you want one. Once you accept a firm choice, the place is yours if you meet the conditions.

UCAS reply dates for 2027 entry depend on when your last decision arrives:

| Last decision by | Reply by |
|---|---|
| 31 March 2027 | 5 May 2027 |
| 12 May 2027 | 2 June 2027 |
| 14 July 2027 | 21 July 2027 |

The first two dates do not apply if you are using Extra. Your own reply date is shown in your application. No reply swaps are possible after 22 July 2027.

## How can you spot a fake offer letter?

Fake offers do happen. The University of Oxford says it has been told of "bogus letters claiming to be from the University of Oxford". These letters offer places or scholarships and ask for payment.

Check these things:

- **The sender.** Oxford says genuine messages come from an "ox.ac.uk" email address. York University in Canada says its links "will always have yorku.ca in them".
- **The portal.** At Oxford, offer documents are in Self-Service. At York, the offer letter appears in the MyFile applicant portal. A real offer should match what you see when you log in.
- **Payment requests.** Oxford "will never ask for money to be paid in order to secure a scholarship." York says you "will never be asked to pay an application fee to an individual". uni-assist says payment requests come only from uni-assist, through its own portal.
- **Pressure.** York warns of urgent or threatening messages that push you to click a link or send money.

If you have any doubt, contact the university's admissions office yourself, using details from its official website. Do not use the phone number or email address in the letter.

Canada adds its own check. When you apply for a study permit, your school is asked to confirm that your letter of acceptance is valid.

If an agent sent you the offer, see [how to check a study abroad agent](/blog/how-to-check-a-study-abroad-agent/).

## What to do next

1. Write down every date in your offer: reply, deposit, conditions and visa document.
2. Read the deposit refund terms before you pay anything.
3. If anything looks wrong, contact the university's admissions office directly.
