As of October 2026, Saudi citizens must send evidence of money with a UK Student visa application. Saudi Arabia is not on the UK's lighter evidence list, and it is not on the UK TB test list either. For the Ministry of Education to grant equivalency for a foreign degree, you should normally study full time at a recommended university. If you pay for yourself, get the ministry's approval first.

## Where can Saudi students study?

Options include [the UK](/blog/study-in/uk/), [the USA](/blog/study-in/usa/), [Canada](/blog/study-in/canada/), [Australia](/blog/study-in/australia/), [Europe](/blog/study-in/europe/) and [Dubai](/blog/study-in/dubai/). Read the equivalency rules below before you choose a university, because they affect whether your degree is recognised at home.

## What is different for Saudi citizens applying for a UK Student visa?

### Do you need to send bank statements?

Yes. Paragraph ST 22.1 of Appendix Student excuses some nationalities from sending evidence of money and the documents used to get their offer, if they apply from the country where they live or from the UK. Saudi Arabia is not on that list, so send both with your application.

The money must be in your account for at least 28 days in a row. The end of that 28-day period must be within 31 days of the date you apply. [Bank statements for a UK Student visa](/blog/uk-student-visa-bank-statements/) covers the amounts and what the statements must show.

### Do you need a TB test?

Your passport does not decide this. Where you have lived does. You need a TB test if all of these are true:

- you are coming to the UK for 6 months or more
- you have lived in a listed country for 6 months or more
- you were living there, or in another listed country, within the last 6 months

Saudi Arabia is not on the list. If you lived in a listed country, such as Pakistan or India, within the last 6 months, you may still need a test.

### Where do you give your fingerprints?

You apply online, then prove your identity. Depending on your passport, you use the UK Immigration: ID Check app, or you give your fingerprints and photo at a visa application centre. On gov.uk's [visa application centre finder](https://www.gov.uk/find-a-visa-application-centre), the Saudi Arabia entry links to VFS Global's centre page for Saudi Arabia.

The fee is £558 from outside the UK, and a decision usually takes 3 weeks. For the full process, see the [UK Student visa guide](/blog/uk-student-visa-guide/).

### What about short courses?

For a course of 6 months or less, Saudi citizens need an electronic travel authorisation (ETA) or a Standard Visitor visa, not a Student visa. An ETA costs £20 and lets you come for up to 6 months.

## Which other visa rules depend on a Saudi passport?

As of October 2026, from each government's official pages:

| Destination | Rule for Saudi citizens |
|---|---|
| USA | F-1 student visa: no reciprocity fee, multiple entries, valid up to 60 months |
| Canada | Visitor visa needed, not an eTA |
| Germany | Visa needed, even for short visits |

In Canada, if your study permit is approved from outside the country, you get a port of entry letter and the visitor visa you need. The letter is not the permit itself. See [studying in Canada](/blog/study-in/canada/).

## Do universities abroad accept Saudi qualifications?

Each university sets its own rules. Two examples, from their own country pages, as of October 2026:

- The University of Edinburgh does not currently accept the Tawjihiyya for direct entry. Applicants with it will usually need a foundation year. A levels, the IB and US qualifications can give direct entry.
- The University of Glasgow does not accept national school qualifications for undergraduate entry. For a master's, it normally accepts a 4-year bachelor's degree. Where it asks for a UK 2:1, that means a GPA of 3/4 or 4/5, 80% or a B. Where it asks for a 2:2, it means 2.8/4 or 3.5/5, 75% or a C+.

Neither page lists an English test waiver for Saudi qualifications, and Edinburgh says all applicants must show their English. See [which English test you need](/blog/english-tests-for-study-abroad/).

## How does degree equivalency work in Saudi Arabia?

The Ministry of Education runs the service Equivalency of Qualifications Issued Outside the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia. As of October 2026, it is free, it takes 5 business days, and you apply online at eqs.moe.gov.sa.

### What does the ministry check?

- The university and programme should be on the ministry's list of recommended universities and programmes. If they are not, the case is reviewed separately.
- Study should be regular and full-time, and you should live in the country of study. Master's and doctoral students may study part-time, as the rules for each stage allow.
- You should complete at least 75% of the degree at recommended or accepted universities. If you transfer, you must complete at least 25% of the graduation requirements at the university that awards the degree.
- Your previous certificate must be from Saudi Arabia or already have equivalency.
- If you pay for yourself, you need the ministry's approval before you study abroad. The ministry says this approval does not commit it to grant equivalency after you graduate.

### How do you check a university before you apply?

Use the ministry's Inquiry about the Recommended Universities service. Log in through Safeer, choose the country, university, specialty and degree, then search. It is free.

The ministry builds the list from each university's standing and academic level, using the criteria of its Certificate Equivalency Committee.

### Which documents will you need?

For equivalency, you upload your national ID and passport, the certificate and your transcript. The documents must be certified by the Saudi cultural attaché in the country that issued the certificate. If that country has no Saudi cultural attaché, the nearest Saudi embassy or cultural attaché can certify them. Arrange this before you leave the country where you studied.

## How does the Saudi government scholarship work?

The Custodian of the Two Holy Mosques Scholarship Program was approved in 2005. It sends Saudi students abroad for degrees that meet the needs of the labour market and the Kingdom's development.

In March 2022, the Ministry of Education announced a new strategy. It has four tracks:

- Alrowad (Pioneers), open to science and humanities subjects
- Research and Development
- Imdad (Supply)
- Wa'ed (Promising)

The aim is to send 70,000 students to the world's top 200 institutions by 2030. For 2023/2024, applications ran from 4 December 2022 to 5 May 2023, through three of the tracks. The conditions for each cycle are published on the ministry's scholarship portal, so read the current ones there.

The ministry's scholarship page, which shows university lists for 2021/2022 and 2022/2023, sets conditions for its Track of Excellence such as:

- Saudi nationality
- direct, unconditional admission to one of the listed subjects and universities
- a programme that is not executive
- study in the country of the university's main campus
- equivalency for your previous qualification, if it is from abroad

The main campus rule matters if you are thinking about a branch campus in another country. You apply through Safeer, the ministry's electronic platform.

As of October 2026, Saudi Arabia is not in the country list on Chevening's application page. For other schemes, see [government scholarships for international students](/blog/government-scholarships-for-international-students/).

## What if you live in Saudi Arabia but are not a Saudi citizen?

Visa rules follow your passport. Where you apply usually follows where you live.

- UK evidence: the lighter rule looks at your nationality and where you apply from. If your nationality is on the list, such as [the UAE](/blog/students-from/uae/) or Malaysia, it can still apply when you apply from Saudi Arabia, where you live.
- UK TB test: this depends on where you have lived, so your time in Saudi Arabia counts.
- US visa: reciprocity fees and visa lengths depend on your nationality.
- At home: the scholarship programme is for Saudi nationals.

For your own nationality's rules, see the guides for students from [India](/blog/students-from/india/), [Pakistan](/blog/students-from/pakistan/), [Egypt](/blog/students-from/egypt/), [Bangladesh](/blog/students-from/bangladesh/) and [other countries](/blog/topics/home-country/).

## What to do next

1. Check your university and programme on the ministry's recommended list before you apply.
2. If you will pay for yourself, get the ministry's approval to study abroad before you enrol.
3. For the UK, put the money in your account at least 28 days before you apply, and leave it there.
4. Before you leave your study country, have your certificate and transcript certified by the Saudi cultural attaché.
