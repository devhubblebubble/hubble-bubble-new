As of October 2026, Omani citizens need a UK Student visa for courses over 6 months, but may not need to send bank statements. If you live in Oman, you need no TB test. Back home, Oman's higher education ministry, MoHERI, says you must get its written approval before you join any institution abroad. Diplomas, bachelor's and master's must be full-time, and a three-year UK bachelor's needs honours.

## Where can you study from Oman?

You can study in the [UK](/blog/study-in/uk/), [Ireland](/blog/study-in/ireland/), the [USA](/blog/study-in/usa/), [Canada](/blog/study-in/canada/), [Europe](/blog/study-in/europe/), [Australia](/blog/study-in/australia/), [New Zealand](/blog/study-in/new-zealand/), [Singapore](/blog/study-in/singapore/), [Malaysia](/blog/study-in/malaysia/) or [Dubai](/blog/study-in/dubai/). This guide covers what is different for students from Oman. For the general steps, read [How to apply for a master's abroad](/blog/how-to-apply-for-a-masters-abroad/).

## What UK visa rules apply to Omani citizens?

For a course longer than 6 months, you need a Student visa. For a course of 6 months or less, you need an electronic travel authorisation (ETA) or a Standard Visitor visa. As a visitor, you can study at an accredited institution for up to 6 months, including English language courses. As of October 2026, an ETA costs £20.

Two rules work in your favour if you are an Omani citizen living in Oman:

- No TB test. Oman is not on the UK's list of countries where you need a tuberculosis test. You need one only if you have lived in a listed country for 6 months or more and were living there, or in another listed country, within the last 6 months.
- Fewer documents. Oman is on the list in paragraph ST 22.1 of Appendix Student. If you apply from the country where you live, or from the UK, you do not need to send your financial evidence with the application. The same goes for the documents you used to get your offer.

The decision maker may still ask for that evidence, so keep it ready. You must still hold the money for 28 days in a row. As of October 2026, that is £1,529 a month in London or £1,171 a month elsewhere, for up to 9 months. For applications made from 30 November 2026, the figures are £1,570 and £1,203. [Bank statements for a UK Student visa](/blog/uk-student-visa-bank-statements/) explains how to show it.

To find the UK visa application centre, use GOV.UK's finder. For Oman, it links to the VFS Global page for the UK visa centre there. The fee, the health surcharge and the timeline are in the [UK Student visa guide](/blog/uk-student-visa-guide/).

## Which other visa rules depend on an Omani passport?

As of October 2026, from each government's official pages:

| Destination | Rule for Omani citizens |
|---|---|
| UAE (Dubai) | As a GCC citizen, no visa, entry permit or sponsor needed to enter |
| USA | F-1 student visa: no reciprocity fee, multiple entries, valid for up to 60 months |
| Canada | Visitor visa needed, not an eTA |
| Germany | Visa needed before you travel |

For the UAE, you show your passport or national ID card when you arrive. The UAE page covers entry only, so ask your university what it needs to enrol you. MoHERI's rules, below, apply to any institution outside Oman, so check a Dubai campus against them too. [UK and Australian university campuses in Dubai](/blog/branch-campuses-in-dubai/) explains what degree you get there.

## What if you live in Oman on another passport?

Visa rules follow your passport. The UK TB rule follows where you have lived.

- If you have lived in Oman for the last 6 months or more, you do not need a UK TB test. This is true even if your passport is from a listed country, such as India or Pakistan.
- The ST 22.1 exemption depends on your nationality. India and Pakistan are not on its list, so their citizens send full financial evidence and the documents used to get their offer.
- If you later need a foreign degree equated in Oman, MoHERI's service is open to non-Omani applicants too. For most of them, the paid fast track described below is compulsory.

For the rules that follow your passport, see the Students from pages, for example [India](/blog/students-from/india/) and [Pakistan](/blog/students-from/pakistan/).

## How do universities abroad treat Omani qualifications?

Here are two examples from UK universities' own country pages, as of October 2026:

- The University of Glasgow does not accept Omani school qualifications on their own. It asks for A-levels, the IB, Glasgow International College or another accepted qualification. For a master's, it normally wants a bachelor's with a GPA of 3.0 where it asks for a UK 2:1, and 2.7 for a 2:2.
- The University of Birmingham says holders of the Thanawiyan are not normally eligible for direct entry to a bachelor's. It considers them for its foundation programme with an overall average of 85% or above. Its Engineering and Physical Sciences Pathway also needs 85% in Mathematics.
- For a master's, Birmingham considers four-year bachelor's degrees from recognised Omani universities. It expects a GPA of 3.0/4, 3.75/5 or 75% for a 2:1, and 2.8/4, 3.5/5 or 70% for a 2:2.

Neither page lists an English test waiver for Omani qualifications, so plan to take a test. See [Which English test do you need?](/blog/english-tests-for-study-abroad/) Each university sets its own rules, so check your course page too.

## Will Oman recognise your foreign degree?

The Ministry of Higher Education, Research and Innovation (MoHERI) equates qualifications from outside Oman. Its Qualification Equivalency department recommends institutions abroad, and its committee decides whether to equate each qualification. Its rules, as of October 2026:

- You must get written approval from the ministry before you join any institution of higher education.
- The institution must be recommended by MoHERI before you join it. The committee can refuse to equate a qualification from an institution without written approval.
- Diplomas, bachelor's and master's must be studied full-time. Irregular attendance can lead to a refusal.
- E-learning is only for master's and PhD programmes. It does not apply to undergraduate or diploma study.
- E-learning is not allowed for any programme that needs practical skills, such as medical, clinical and health assistance, or engineering programmes.
- A three-year BA, as in the UK, Australia, New Zealand and Ireland, needs honours to be equated as a bachelor's. An ordinary degree counts as a higher diploma.
- A Doctor of Medicine, Doctor of Pharmacy (PharmD) or Juris Doctor (JD) counts as a bachelor's degree, not a doctorate.

The ministry's list of recommended institutions is updated from time to time. Its Abroad Universities page also offers lists of fake universities and of universities offering distance learning. Check all three before you apply.

## What happens when you apply for equivalency?

The equivalency service covers university qualifications taken after the General Education Diploma. It does not cover professional certificates or training courses.

You fill in a proof of residence form and attach the entry and exit stamps for your country of study. You do not need the stamps if your study started before 2015, your certificate was issued in a GCC country, your study was remote, or you are an expatriate who studied in the country where you live. So keep the passports that hold those stamps.

Your certificates are authenticated in one of two ways:

- Through the ministry, which can take some time. This route is for qualifications obtained only from Jordan, Egypt, Kuwait or the UAE.
- Through the Data Flow company's fast track. As of October 2026, the ministry puts the fee at about 33 rials, and the company may ask for more depending on the institution. It is optional for Omani students, compulsory for most non-Omani applicants, and not available to Omani students sponsored by the ministry.

## What scholarships can Omani students apply for?

- MoHERI scholarships. The ministry has run a National Postgraduate Scholarship Programme for study abroad, with applications through the Higher Education Admission Centre (HEAC) online system. The guide on the ministry's site is for 2020/21, and the ministry says it reviews the eligibility criteria from time to time. Read the current guide on the HEAC site before you apply.
- Fulbright. Oman is on the Fulbright Foreign Student Program's country list. It is for graduate students, young professionals and artists. You apply through the Fulbright office for your country, which is a Fulbright Commission or the US Embassy.
- Chevening. As of October 2026, Oman is not in Chevening's list of countries you can apply from.

For other schemes, see [Government scholarships for international students](/blog/government-scholarships-for-international-students/).

## Which documents need extra steps?

Oman is a party to the Hague Apostille Convention. It came into force for Oman on 30 January 2012. An apostille is a stamped official certificate. It confirms that the signature, stamp or seal on a document matches official records.

If you are asked to legalise a UK certificate, the UK Legalisation Office adds the apostille. As of October 2026, it costs £45 per document, plus postage or courier, and usually takes up to 25 working days. A qualification certificate must first be certified by a UK public official, such as a notary or a solicitor.

## What to do next

1. Check that your institution is on MoHERI's recommended list, and get the ministry's written approval before you enrol.
2. Choose a full-time course. For a three-year bachelor's in the UK, Australia, New Zealand or Ireland, aim for honours.
3. Keep your entry and exit stamps for the equivalency application.
4. Hold your visa money for 28 days, even if you may not need to send statements.
