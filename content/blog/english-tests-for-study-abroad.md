It depends on the country and the university. For a UK Student visa at degree level, you need English at CEFR level B2, and your university can assess it itself. Australia sets visa minimums such as IELTS 6.0 and PTE Academic 47. In Canada, Ireland and Germany, the university's own score matters most. As of October 2026, check the score on each course page.

## Why check both the visa rule and the university's score?

The UK, Australia and Ireland set a minimum level for the visa. Each university then sets its own score for each course, and that score can be higher.

Ireland shows the gap. For most courses, the Irish visa minimum is IELTS Academic 5.0. University College Dublin (UCD) asks graduate applicants for IELTS Academic 6.5, with no part below 6.0.

UCAS says the level for UK courses "varies from university to university, and even from course to course within the same university". So start with each course page on your shortlist. The full application steps are in [how to apply for a master's abroad](/blog/how-to-apply-for-a-masters-abroad/).

## Which English tests count for a UK Student visa?

Gov.uk lists four ways to prove your English:

- "having a UK school qualification"
- "having a degree from a UK institution"
- "having a degree from an institution that was outside the UK and taught in English"
- "passing an English test from an approved provider"

The approved tests are called Secure English Language Tests (SELTs). If you take the test outside the UK, the providers are:

| Provider | Tests on the gov.uk list include |
|---|---|
| IELTS SELT Consortium | IELTS for UKVI |
| LANGUAGECERT | LANGUAGECERT Academic SELT |
| Pearson | PTE Academic UKVI |
| PSI Services (UK) Ltd | Skills for English UKVI |

In the UK, the list is the same, except that Trinity College London replaces PSI. You must sit the test at an approved test location. Results are valid for 2 years from the date the test is awarded.

For study at degree level or above, you need the equivalent of CEFR level B2. Below degree level, it is B1.

### Can your university assess your English itself?

Yes, if you are studying at degree level or above. Gov.uk says your "Higher Education Provider (HEP) can assess your level of English themselves". It adds: "This means they may ask you to do a different test." That test "must still be equivalent to a CEFR level B2".

So your offer letter decides which test you need. If it names a SELT, book a SELT.

### Who does not need to prove their English?

You do not need to if you are from a country on the gov.uk list, such as Australia, Ireland, New Zealand or the USA. The same applies if you completed a qualification equivalent to a UK degree in one of those countries. Nationals of Canada are exempt too.

## What does Australia ask for?

For a Student visa, the Australian Government sets these minimum scores for tests taken from 7 August 2025.

| Test | Minimum | With 10 weeks of ELICOS | With 20 weeks of ELICOS |
|---|---|---|---|
| IELTS Academic or General Training | 6.0 | 5.5 | 5.0 |
| PTE Academic | 47 | 39 | 31 |
| TOEFL iBT | 67 | 51 | 37 |
| LANGUAGECERT Academic | 61 | 54 | 46 |
| C1 Advanced | 161 | Not accepted | Not accepted |

ELICOS means English Language Intensive Courses for Overseas Students. The lower scores apply if an ELICOS of that length comes with your main course. The 10-week column also covers foundation and eligible pathway programmes.

CELPIP General, the Michigan English Test (MET) and OET are accepted too, with their own scores. Tests taken before 7 August 2025 use an older table.

Rules to know:

- Home Affairs "does not accept evidence from English language tests that are delivered completely online". Take your test at a secure test centre.
- For TOEFL iBT, select "Taking TOEFL for Australia" when you register.
- If you send the result with your visa application, the test must be from the 2 years before you apply.
- Your university sets its own entry score, so check the course page too.

More on the country in [studying in Australia](/blog/study-in-australia/).

## What does Canada ask for?

Canadian universities set their own English requirements for admission. One example is the University of Toronto's School of Graduate Studies. It asks for results if your previous university was outside Canada and did not teach mainly in English:

- IELTS Academic: 7.0 overall, with at least 6.5 in each part
- TOEFL iBT: 93 overall, with 22 in writing and speaking

Toronto's tests must be taken within the 24 months before you apply, and some departments ask for higher scores.

### What about the Post-Graduation Work Permit?

If you want to work in Canada after you graduate, plan for a second test. For Post-Graduation Work Permit (PGWP) applications submitted on or after 1 November 2024, you must show English or French skills in all four areas:

- CLB 7 in English, or NCLC 7 in French, if you studied at a university
- CLB 5, or NCLC 5, for college, polytechnic and other non-university programmes

The accepted English tests are CELPIP General, IELTS General Training and PTE Core. In French, they are TEF Canada and TCF Canada. Results must be less than 2 years old when you apply.

Note that the PGWP list has IELTS General Training, while Toronto asks for IELTS Academic. See [studying in Canada](/blog/study-in-canada/).

## What does Ireland ask for?

Irish immigration sets minimum scores for the visa. For courses other than English language, foundation or second-level courses, they include:

- IELTS Academic: 5.0
- TOEFL iBT: 61
- Duolingo English Test: 75

These standards are "for visa purposes", and your college's standard "may be different". Your certificate must be issued within 2 years of your course start date. Online test results from the listed providers are accepted.

In practice, follow your university's score. UCD, for example, asks for IELTS Academic 6.5 with no part below 6.0, and says some courses ask for more. See [studying in Ireland](/blog/study-in-ireland/).

## What does Germany ask for?

German universities set language rules for each programme. European University Viadrina is one example:

- **English-taught programmes:** the accepted proof and level depend on the programme, so check the programme page.
- **German-taught programmes:** German at CEFR C1, shown by certificates such as DSH-2 or TestDaF with level 4 (TDN 4) in all four parts.

For the visa, the German Missions in India say that "Only certificates approved by German government will be accepted". They also do not accept certificates from online exams. Check the checklist for your own country before you book. See [studying in Germany](/blog/study-in-germany/).

## How long do test results last?

| Where | Rule |
|---|---|
| UK visa (SELT) | Valid for 2 years from the date the test is awarded |
| Australia visa | Taken in the 2 years before you apply, if sent with your application |
| Ireland visa | Issued within 2 years of your course start date |
| Canada PGWP | Less than 2 years old when you apply |
| University of Toronto | Taken within 24 months before you apply |

## Which test should you book?

- Start with your shortlist. Write down the test and score each course accepts.
- If you apply to the UK and your offer asks for a SELT, book a test from the gov.uk list, such as IELTS for UKVI or PTE Academic UKVI, at an approved centre.
- If you apply to more than one country, look for one test they all accept. IELTS Academic, PTE Academic and TOEFL iBT are on both the Australian and the Irish visa lists.
- Leave time for a resit in case you miss the score.

## What are the common mistakes?

- **Booking the wrong version.** If your UK offer asks for a SELT, a test that is not on the gov.uk list will not meet that condition.
- **Taking an at-home test for Australia.** Home Affairs does not accept tests delivered completely online.
- **Meeting only the visa minimum.** In Ireland, IELTS Academic 5.0 meets the visa minimum but not UCD's 6.5.
- **Letting a result expire** before your visa application.
- **Skipping the TOEFL step for Australia.** Select "Taking TOEFL for Australia" when you register.
- **Assuming one test covers everything in Canada.** The PGWP list is different from the tests Toronto, for example, asks for at admission.

## What to do next

1. List the English score each course on your shortlist asks for.
2. Choose one test that all of them accept, and book it at a test centre.
3. Check that the result will still be valid on the date you apply for your visa.
