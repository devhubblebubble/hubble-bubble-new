As of October 2026, if you live in Sri Lanka, a UK Student visa needs a TB test. The only approved clinic is the IOM clinic in Colombo, at US$60 for anyone aged 11 or over. You must also send proof of money. Universities such as Manchester ask for a four-year special degree for a master's. Your family can pay your fees through a licensed bank.

## What are your options?

You can study in the [UK](/blog/study-in/uk/), [Ireland](/blog/study-in/ireland/), the [USA](/blog/study-in/usa/), [Canada](/blog/study-in/canada/), [Germany and the rest of Europe](/blog/study-in/europe/), [Australia](/blog/study-in/australia/), [New Zealand](/blog/study-in/new-zealand/), [Singapore](/blog/study-in/singapore/), [Malaysia](/blog/study-in/malaysia/) or [Dubai](/blog/study-in/dubai/). Each link leads to that country's main guide.

This guide covers only what is different for Sri Lankan citizens and for people who live in Sri Lanka. To compare countries, read [which country should you study in](/blog/which-country-should-you-study-in/).

## What is different for Sri Lankans on a UK Student visa?

### Do you need a TB test?

You need one if all of these are true:

- you are coming to the UK for 6 months or more
- you have lived in Sri Lanka for 6 months or more
- you lived there within the last 6 months

The rule follows where you have lived, not your passport. If you live in another country, check it on the [gov.uk list](https://www.gov.uk/tb-test-visa/countries-where-you-need-a-tb-test-to-enter-the-uk).

Sri Lanka has one clinic approved by the Home Office. It is the International Organization for Migration (IOM) Migration Health Assessment Center in Colombo. The gov.uk page, updated on 4 July 2024, gives these fees:

| Age | Fee |
|---|---|
| 11 and over | US$60 |
| Under 11 | US$30 |

You pay in Sri Lankan rupees, directly to the clinic. IOM converts the fee at the UN's monthly rate. The fee does not cover any TB treatment.

Appointments are usually available within a few days. At busy times, especially June to September, they can take up to 10 days. The clinic is open Monday to Friday, 8:30am to 4:30pm.

Bring these to your appointment:

- the passport you will apply with, and photo ID such as your national ID card
- two recent passport-sized colour photographs
- your addresses in Sri Lanka and the UK
- the fee
- details of any past TB or lung disease, with medical notes and x-rays if you can

The certificate is valid for 6 months from your x-ray.

### Do you have to send bank statements?

Yes. Paragraph ST 22.1 of Appendix Student lists the nationalities that can skip sending proof of money. As of October 2026, Sri Lanka is not on it.

So send your financial evidence with your application. [Bank statements for a UK Student visa](/blog/uk-student-visa-bank-statements/) explains the rules.

### Where do you give your fingerprints?

UK visa application centres in Sri Lanka are run by VFS Global. The gov.uk [list of visa application centres](https://www.gov.uk/find-a-visa-application-centre) links to VFS for the address and opening times. For fees and timings, see the [UK Student visa guide](/blog/uk-student-visa-guide/).

## What about Ireland and the USA?

- **Ireland:** Sri Lankan citizens need a visa. Ireland's list of visa-required nationalities, updated on 12 May 2025, includes Sri Lanka. See [studying in Ireland](/blog/study-in/ireland/).
- **USA:** Sri Lanka is not on the US visa suspension lists under Presidential Proclamation 10998, in force since 1 January 2026. See [studying in the USA](/blog/study-in/usa/).

## How do UK universities treat Sri Lankan A-levels and degrees?

Each university sets its own rules. Three examples, from each university's own pages:

- **University of Manchester:** for a taught master's, it considers a four-year Special Bachelor's or engineering degree from a reputable university. It may consider a three-year degree with very high grades. The usual minimum is 60% to 70%, depending on your university. For undergraduate entry, it lists Sri Lankan A-levels for direct entry to the first year.
- **University of Glasgow:** its master's courses normally accept a special or professional degree. Where a course needs a UK 2:1, you need a 2:1, 60% or a GPA of 3.30. For a 2:2, it asks for 55% or a GPA of 3.0. For undergraduate entry, it does not accept Sri Lankan national qualifications. It asks for A-levels, the IB or a foundation at Glasgow International College.
- **Swansea University:** for undergraduate entry, A-level English Language at grade C, plus a credibility interview, counts as IELTS 6.0. For postgraduate entry, it lists a "Sri Lanka O Level" at grade C, plus a medium of instruction letter from a listed university, as equal to IELTS 6.0 to 7.0. The listed universities include the University of Colombo and the University of Peradeniya.

Manchester, by contrast, does not accept a medium of instruction letter as evidence of your English. To choose a test, read [which English test you need](/blog/english-tests-for-study-abroad/).

## Will your foreign degree be recognised in Sri Lanka?

The University Grants Commission (UGC) of Sri Lanka decides which foreign universities it recognises. Its public notice of 9 February 2014 says it recognises institutions listed in two publications. These are the Commonwealth Universities Yearbook and the International Handbook of Universities.

If your degree is not from a Sri Lankan state university, you can apply to the UGC for a Certificate of Recognition. You send the application with an attested copy of your degree.

The notice says it is "the responsibility of the general public to verify the recognition" before registering for a course. So check with the UGC before you pay, especially for a foreign degree taught in Sri Lanka.

## How do you send money for fees from Sri Lanka?

The Central Bank's Department of Foreign Exchange answers the main questions in its FAQs, as of October 2026:

- Your family can send money for tuition, living costs and other study costs through an authorised dealer (a licensed bank). They must give the bank documents that support the payment.
- On a student visa, your bank can load a travel card for you. It first checks documents from your university about your living costs.
- Your parents or guardians can repay a loan you took for your studies abroad, through a licensed bank.
- From your travel allowance, up to US$5,000 can be taken as foreign currency notes.

Foreign Exchange Regulations No. 4 of 2021, published on 3 February 2021, add two rules for students:

- You may borrow from a foreign bank, financial institution or university for your fees and living costs.
- You may open a foreign currency account abroad for your studies. You may also open one when a visa authority requires it, such as a [German blocked account](/blog/study-in-germany/).

For an account you open while abroad, you must bring the balance back to Sri Lanka within one month after the account's purpose ends. For an account a visa authority requires, you must bring the money back within one month if your visa is refused or you decide not to study.

## Which scholarships can Sri Lankan students apply for?

- **Commonwealth Scholarships:** Sri Lanka is eligible for both master's schemes. For Commonwealth Master's Scholarships, you apply through the Ministry of Education (Higher Education Section) as well as the CSC. The 2027/28 round has closed. Commonwealth Shared Scholarships open on 10 November 2026 and close at 16:00 GMT on 8 December 2026. For Shared Scholarships, you must not have studied or worked for more than one academic year in a high income country.
- **Chevening:** Sri Lanka has its own Chevening page. The 2027-2028 round is closed.
- **Fulbright:** the US-Sri Lanka Fulbright Commission lists a Fulbright Master's Fellowship Award for study in the USA. Its page, written for an earlier round, asks for a four-year degree from a recognised Sri Lankan university. It also asks for one year of full-time paid work, and for you to be under 35. Holders of three-year degrees from Sri Lanka or India need a relevant postgraduate qualification. Winners must live in Sri Lanka for at least 2 years afterwards. Check the current call with the Commission.
- **Australia Awards:** Sri Lanka is a participating country, for study in [Australia](/blog/study-in/australia/).

For how each scheme works, read [government scholarships for international students](/blog/government-scholarships-for-international-students/).

## Which documents need extra steps?

- **No apostille:** as of October 2026, Sri Lanka is not on the Hague Conference's list of parties to the Apostille Convention. So Sri Lankan documents cannot be apostilled. Ask each university or embassy which certified copies it accepts.
- **Medium of instruction letter:** Swansea's postgraduate route needs one from a listed university. Ask your university for it early.
- **UGC certificate:** an employer or university in Sri Lanka may ask for proof that your foreign degree is recognised. If so, apply for the UGC's Certificate of Recognition.

## What to do next

1. If your course starts in September, book your TB test at IOM Colombo early, before the June to September rush.
2. Check how each university treats your degree type: special, professional or general, three or four years.
3. Before you pay for a foreign degree, check that the UGC recognises the institution.
4. Ask your bank which documents it needs to send your fees and load a travel card.
