As of October 2026, Qatari citizens can usually apply for a UK Student visa without sending bank statements. Qatar is also not on the UK's TB test list. If you or your employer will pay for a degree abroad, get pre-approval from the Ministry of Education and Higher Education first. It is a key requirement for equivalency, and it expects face-to-face study at an approved university's main campus.

## Where can Qatari students study?

Options include [the UK](/blog/study-in/uk/), [the USA](/blog/study-in/usa/), [Canada](/blog/study-in/canada/), [Australia](/blog/study-in/australia/), [Europe](/blog/study-in/europe/), or a branch campus at home in Education City (see below). Read the pre-approval rules before you choose a university abroad, because they affect whether your degree can be equated at home.

## What is different for Qatari citizens applying for a UK Student visa?

### Do you need to send bank statements?

Usually not. Paragraph ST 22.1 of Appendix Student lists nationalities that do not have to send some evidence with the application. This covers evidence of money and the documents used to get their offer. Qatar is on that list.

This works only if you apply from the country where you live, or from inside the UK. UK Visas and Immigration (UKVI) can still ask for the evidence before it decides. So keep the money in your account for 28 days in a row, with your statements ready. [Bank statements for a UK Student visa](/blog/uk-student-visa-bank-statements/) explains the rules.

### Do you need a TB test?

Your passport does not decide this. Where you have lived does. You need a TB test if all of these are true:

- you are coming to the UK for 6 months or more
- you have lived in a listed country for 6 months or more
- you were living there, or in another listed country, within the last 6 months

Qatar is not on the list. If you lived in a listed country, such as India or Nepal, within the last 6 months, you may still need a test.

### Where do you give your fingerprints?

You apply online, then prove your identity. Depending on your passport, you use the UK Immigration: ID Check app, or you give your fingerprints and photo at a visa application centre. On gov.uk's [visa application centre finder](https://www.gov.uk/find-a-visa-application-centre), the Qatar entry links to VFS Global's centre page for Qatar.

The fee is £558 from outside the UK, and a decision usually takes 3 weeks. For the full process, see the [UK Student visa guide](/blog/uk-student-visa-guide/).

### What about short courses?

For a course of 6 months or less, Qatari citizens need an electronic travel authorisation (ETA) or a Standard Visitor visa, not a Student visa. A visitor can study at an accredited institution for up to 6 months, including English language courses. An ETA costs £20.

## Which other visa rules depend on a Qatari passport?

As of October 2026, from each government's official pages:

| Destination | Rule for Qatari citizens |
|---|---|
| USA | F-1 student visa: no reciprocity fee, multiple entries, valid up to 60 months |
| Canada | No visitor visa. You fly with an eTA |
| Germany | Visa needed, even for short visits |

In Canada, if your study permit is approved from outside the country, you get a port of entry letter and an eTA. The letter is not the permit itself. See [studying in Canada](/blog/study-in/canada/).

## Do universities abroad accept Qatari qualifications?

Each university sets its own rules. Two examples, from their own country pages, as of October 2026:

- The University of Edinburgh says applicants with the Al-Thanawaya Al-Aama (General Secondary Education Certificate) will usually need a foundation year. A levels, the IB and US qualifications can give direct entry.
- The University of Glasgow does not accept national school qualifications for undergraduate entry. For a master's that needs a UK 2:1, it normally accepts a bachelor's degree with a GPA of 3/4, 80% or a B. Where it needs a 2:2, it accepts 2.7/4, 75% or a C+.

Neither page lists an English test waiver for Qatari qualifications, and Edinburgh says all applicants must show their English. See [which English test you need](/blog/english-tests-for-study-abroad/).

## How does degree equivalency work in Qatar?

The Ministry of Education and Higher Education (MOEHE) grants equivalency for university degrees. It follows Cabinet Resolution No. 3 of 2017 on the regulation of university degree equivalency.

It does not grant equivalency for security or military qualifications, training courses of any length, or vocational certificates. Professional or executive master's degrees and professional doctorates (DBA) can be equated.

### What is pre-approval, and who needs it?

Pre-approval is for students who study abroad at their own expense or their employer's. The ministry calls it a key requirement for equivalency after you graduate. Its rules include:

- the university must be on the ministry's list of approved universities, which is updated from time to time
- you must attend, and sit your exams, at the main campus named in the pre-approval decision
- you must report for face-to-face study within six months of the pre-approval, or it is revoked
- you cannot change country, university, subject or level of study without approval
- you cannot hold more than one pre-approval for the same degree

The main campus rule matters if you are thinking about a university's campus outside its home country. Ask the ministry before you enrol.

### What does equivalency cost, and which documents do you need?

Sharek, the Qatar government services portal, lists the service Equivalency of a University Degree Issued Abroad for Qataris. As of October 2026, the fee is QAR 300 and the estimated time is about 1 month and 16 working days.

If you are employed, the request must come from your government or semi-government employer. If you are not, you need a non-employment certificate from the Civil Service Bureau. The documents include:

- copies of your passport and Qatari ID
- the National Service Certificate, for men
- the F-1 study visa application form, if you studied in the USA
- a language certificate, if you did not study in your native language
- transcripts of any transferred credits counted towards the degree

## Can you study at a branch campus in Qatar?

Yes. Several US universities run campuses in Qatar. Examples, from each university's own site, as of October 2026:

| University | What it offers | What its site says |
|---|---|---|
| Carnegie Mellon University in Qatar | Bachelor's degrees in AI, biological sciences, business administration, computer science and information systems | Located in Doha's Education City |
| Georgetown University in Qatar | Bachelor of Science in Foreign Service | Set up in 2005 with Qatar Foundation |
| VCUarts Qatar | BA in Art History, four BFA programmes, MFA in Design | In Education City. Admissions open for autumn 2027 |
| Weill Cornell Medicine-Qatar | Six-Year Medical Education Program and Four-Year Medical Curriculum | Founded in 2001 with Qatar Foundation. Applications open for autumn 2027 |

Texas A&M University at Qatar is closing. Its site says it will wind down operations by 31 August 2028, and all degree programmes will finish by the end of the 2027-2028 academic year.

For degrees from accredited universities in Qatar, the ministry has a separate attestation service (QAR 50), and the academic programme must be approved. Ask the ministry whether your campus and programme qualify before you enrol.

## Which scholarships can Qatari students get?

For 2025-2026, MOEHE opened registration from 1 July to 30 September 2025, on scholarship.edu.gov.qa. The programmes were:

- the Amiri Scholarship Programme, for outstanding students with the highest high school grades, at 24 top-ranked universities worldwide and five local universities
- the Abroad Scholarship Programme
- the Local Scholarship Programme, which includes the Tomouh Program

Check the portal for this year's dates and university lists.

The ministry's page lists Qatar's cultural attachés in the UK (at the Embassy of Qatar in London) and the USA. They support scholarship students: they follow your academic progress, help with housing, transport and health insurance, and handle tuition and monthly payments.

As of October 2026, Qatar is not in the country list on Chevening's application page. For other schemes, see [government scholarships for international students](/blog/government-scholarships-for-international-students/).

## What if you live in Qatar but are not a Qatari citizen?

Visa rules follow your passport. Where you apply usually follows where you live.

- UK evidence: the lighter rule depends on your nationality. If your country is not on the list, send full evidence of money and the documents used to get your offer, even when you apply in Qatar.
- UK TB test: this depends on where you have lived, so your time in Qatar counts.
- US visa: reciprocity fees and visa lengths depend on your nationality.
- At home: the equivalency service above is for Qataris. If you work, or are a candidate for work, in the government or semi-government sector and your employer asks for it, the ministry's Authentication of a University Degree Issued Abroad service applies. It costs QAR 300.

For your own nationality's rules, see the guides for students from [India](/blog/students-from/india/), [Pakistan](/blog/students-from/pakistan/), [Egypt](/blog/students-from/egypt/) and [other countries](/blog/topics/home-country/).

## What to do next

1. Check your university on the ministry's approved list, then apply for pre-approval before you enrol.
2. Plan to study face to face, and sit your exams, at the main campus named in your pre-approval.
3. Watch scholarship.edu.gov.qa for the next registration window. In 2025 it ran from 1 July to 30 September.
4. For the UK, keep your money in place for 28 days, even if you may not need to send statements.
